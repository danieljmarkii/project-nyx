## The mechanism (same as last time)

Each beta has two gates: **eligibility** (the server `app_config` row) and **opt-in** (the local beta shelf). Graduating is three moves, in this order:

1. **The flip** (`enabled: true`, a config write, no PR). On installed builds every account becomes *eligible*, so the toggle appears on the beta shelf for everyone. Nothing turns on by itself.
2. **The removal PR** deletes the key, the registry row, the shelf case, every gate, the flag-off path and its flag-off guard. After it ships there is no switch to fall back to.
3. **The closeout** deletes the `app_config` row (data-only migration) once no installed build reads it, because `resolveAllowlistFlag` fails closed on a missing row.
