**TL;DR — plain English:** Culprit has two analysis engines: the Signal on Home, and the AI read of a vomit or stool photo. Each is careful about its sentences, but neither knows whether it was right, neither hears that the vet has seen the pet, and neither can see weight. Replayed against Nyx's real record, Home asked for a vet about vomiting on 110 evenings in a row, kept asking after the 9/16 visit, never mentioned a 15% weight loss, and the vomit read told the owner three times that the cat "hasn't eaten a full meal recently" when meals were logged but not rated. This project makes the engines **measured**, **calibrated** (urgency in tiers, each ask said once), **accountable** (a care state per concern that hears the owner's answer and the vet visit) and **whole-animal** (weight, treatment and visits become inputs). Everything an owner sees ships dark behind a feature flag, except fixes that only remove a false statement or a reassurance, which ship on their own proof. The build is **about 40 PRs over seven waves (0 to 6), several lanes at a time.** What you do: approve the PMD-12 privacy wording before the 1.2.0 cut (<issue id="e0db6957-2843-4210-bf87-818cd61b3ece" href="https://linear.app/projectnyx/issue/CUL-1313/pmd-12-give-the-engines-evaluation-use-a-stated-purpose-in-the-privacy">CUL-1313</issue>), start the sessions under "Start now", merge as they finish, and rule the open decisions when the table reaches them.

## Latest thinking

* **The Signal and vet visits are one loop (PM, 9/26).** Down: the Signal opens doors into vet visits ("Book a visit" with the reason carried in; "I've called", a small call record with an optional note). Up: only owner answers and dates cross back to set a care state (a tick at the vet, "Talked about it", "I've called", "My vet knows", a recheck date). No visit ever becomes a number the engine counts. Mock round 2, section 03.
* **Home stays clean.** One row per finding (<issue id="3a2d198a-d9d8-4e73-b640-c83da057e534" href="https://linear.app/projectnyx/issue/CUL-1270/signal-on-home-lighter-headline-ask-door-on-every-card-a-small-chart">CUL-1270</issue>, <pull-request id="360f1c1a-c71a-47be-a648-e3f03430a8ca" href="https://linear.app/projectnyx/review/signal-on-home-lighter-a-row-per-finding-titles-that-name-the-claim-no-edb912259abd">Signal on Home, lighter: a row per finding, titles that name the claim, no fold (CUL-1270, CUL-1285, design_v2)</pull-request>). Owners answer one tap away on the finding's screen, never on Home (TD-4). No new Home write class.
* **At GA every concern starts raised.** A past visit like 9/16 gets one question on the finding's screen, at most one a day. "My vet knows" covers a vet involved outside the app.
* **A watched concern comes back on a tested change, not a clock.** The vet's recheck date hands the ask to the appointment strip; a calendar fallback (<issue id="a5be7fb2-0ed1-4a69-b072-0dbc2dd8009a" href="https://linear.app/projectnyx/issue/CUL-1290/en-9b-the-vet-keyed-fallback-one-did-your-vet-want-to-see-her-again">CUL-1290</issue>) is secondary until the harness measures it.
* **A stored escalation is never lowered automatically.** Only the owner's own act lowers a verdict; a stored false sentence gets a dated correction beside it, never a silent rewrite.
* **A care state quiets standing asks only.** Never an intake decline, a photo red flag, a call tier or the burden card (<issue id="62bb1d1d-7025-4d8a-9d16-12768be0572f" href="https://linear.app/projectnyx/issue/CUL-1311/gap-5-absolute-burden-a-pet-vomiting-most-days-in-its-first-weeks-gets">CUL-1311</issue>).

## Guardrails this project inherits

* **A feature flag (PM, 9/25; amended by E-1, 9/26).** Every change to what an owner sees ships dark behind the EN-F allowlist flag, read on the server. The app draws a new field only when the server wrote it; an app-side key exists only for surfaces that start on the phone (the answer controls, the call record), and no key is a beta opt-in. Flag-off is identical to today. The PM's account comes first. A fix that only removes a false statement or a reassurance ships on its own proof, the flag as a kill switch. Every other unit goes live on its pre-registered pass line (PR-16), per phase, naming the oldest installed build that can show it. With the flag off, nothing overwrites, collapses or lowers an escalation stored under it; the Signal's cache regenerates, and no stand-down is minted across a flag change.
* **Tight connectivity with vet visits (PM, 9/26).** Doors go down; only owner answers and dates come up. No visit enters a count. Owners answer one tap away, never on Home.
* **Clinical.** Every per-incident change runs under `clinical-guardrails` (no wellness value in any enum, the deterministic floor, model free text only on its own escalation). No stored escalation is lowered automatically. Every threshold goes through the ruling sheet (E-6 amended): a change that fires less needs harness proof and PM sign-off, and nothing ships a "clinical-grade" claim until a real veterinarian has reviewed it (<issue id="3b890a11-a1ba-4ad5-8f57-0cbedda60d35" href="https://linear.app/projectnyx/issue/CUL-1312/book-one-paid-review-with-a-real-veterinarian-before-engines-v3-wave-4">CUL-1312</issue>). Never tune a floor on the dogfood record or the demo pets. Every clinically load-bearing PR names its counterexample.
* **Privacy.** Until PMD-12's purpose is published (<issue id="e0db6957-2843-4210-bf87-818cd61b3ece" href="https://linear.app/projectnyx/issue/CUL-1313/pmd-12-give-the-engines-evaluation-use-a-stated-purpose-in-the-privacy">CUL-1313</issue>), every evaluation input is synthetic or the PM's own. ## How to run each session

Paste the row's issue (Copy as prompt, naming the PR) or its bundle block into a fresh Claude Code session. The session claims the issue, names the mode, posts its plan and waits for your go-ahead, builds, runs the reviews its row names, and opens a draft PR that references every issue in the row. It closes with `/wrap`. Discovery rows (PR-18a, PR-20, PR-24, PR-39) deliver a spec and a mock round, not code.
