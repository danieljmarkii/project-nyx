1 · The table, and the rules it keeps

The same rows feed the vet report and the app. Bars share one scale (0 to 4 a week) so every rate owns a shape a reader can check without a legend.

A period starts and ends on a recorded event. Trial start and end, a medication course's first and last dose, a change of main food. A course inside a trial splits it: "Rabbit, on prednisolone".
Unreadable says so. When dry food was down all day, timing after meals can't be told, so the cell reads "unreadable", never a number.
Found is its own column. A found pile nobody saw counts in the total and is shown apart, never hidden.
No verdict words. A low row is a number, never "better" or "improved". A quiet stretch is not proof she was well.

4 · Reaction prompts

R-1 · Where does the owner's view live?
A Patterns panel · B a Home strip · C Patterns plus a door from the vomiting card. Recommend C: it is found exactly when it matters and adds nothing to a quiet Home.
R-2 · Which columns does the owner see?
Count and rate only · the full split as on the report. Recommend count and rate: "within 30 min" and "unreadable" need the footnotes a vet reads and an owner skips.
R-3 · What starts a period?
Trial start and end, medication courses, a change of main food · also vet visits. Recommend the first three: a visit changes nothing about her by itself. Open to the build: the app has no "challenge" record, so "Chicken challenge" would read as a food change unless the trial records a reintroduction (CUL-1821, unruled).
R-4 · A short period, like 7.6 days off prednisolone?
Print the rate · print the count and "too short for a rate". Data Scientist recommends the count always and a rate from 14 days, the same coverage idea as CUL-747. Both drawn: section 2 prints every rate, section 3 holds back the short ones. The cost, visible in section 3: "Rabbit again" is 13.5 days old, so at a 14 day floor the one row Dr. Chen cares about shows only its count today. Half a day later it shows 3.1.
R-5 · A pet with no trial, course or food change yet?
Calendar months, labelled as months · a designed empty state until the first event. Recommend months: slow creep shows in months too, and an empty table helps no one. Drawn next round.
R-6 · Which signs?
Vomiting first · every sign at once. Recommend vomiting first; stool, cough and itch reuse the same table once the counting rules for each are checked (a cough log is not a cough).
