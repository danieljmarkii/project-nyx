The trial ledger

The Signal got receipts, a register, and a phone-call script — and two cards down, the wedge feature renders a header, a 4px bar, and one line. This round proposes the same treatment for the diet trial: a ledger (the paper diary grid, digitized), a trial room (one tap in), and “For the recheck” (the trial’s answer to “what to tell your vet”). Nothing here changes the engine, the predicates, or a single ratified state — it renders what TrialFacts already computes.

§ 00

Where this stands

This deck proposes; it supersedes nothing yet. nyx-diet-trial-mockups.html rounds 1–5 remain the design authority for the card’s eleven states and the lifecycle screens — this track adds hosts and evidence around that card, not changes to it. The full reasoning, persona exercises, and research live in docs/sessions/2026-08-12-diet-trial-signal-uplift-exploration.md.

The one-sentence case. The resolver knows eleven states, six withholding reasons, itemized exposures with reasons, and refusal facts in two windows — and Home shows almost none of it. The underwhelm is not missing data; it is unrendered data.

§ 01

What the human elimination-diet world taught us

A research pass this session cataloged mySymptoms, Monash FODMAP, Bearable, Cara Care, Bowelle, Spoonful, Fig, Selectivor, myIBS and others. Three findings shape every frame below:

1 · Nobody renders logging density as a first-class series. Every human overlay chart silently assumes the diary is complete, because the patient is the logger. Our owner is a third-party observer — so the C5 ruling (density disclosed beside any symptom statement) isn’t a caveat we owe, it’s the category’s first honest version of this chart. The ledger makes it ambient.

2 · The market leader discovered our S2 rule, then undermined it. mySymptoms renders every suspicion as two adjacent bars — strength and amount of evidence — then wraps them in a ranked “Top Suspects” list that reads as an indictment, with the correlation≠cause caveat in the manual. The screen wins — which is why every qualifier below is inline, never a legend.

3 · The deepest blue ocean is one phase further out. Monash’s scheduled reintroduction challenge (one food, three days, a per-day dose ladder, outcome feeding the food guide) is proven human tooling for a step that is standard veterinary protocol — and no pet app has any surface for it. Out of this round’s scope; flagged as the roadmap note in §08.

Card v2 verbatim at top (with the §5.4-grammar med-context line); then the ledger (weeks stacked, older weeks collapse behind a count past ~6); the exposure receipt (each row tappable to which rung fired — the Fig steal, already spec’d §5.3 — into the shipped B-616 exposures screen); weekly symptom counts with density printed on every row; the recheck door; the existing doors. Every section is a read of an existing predicate output — no server change anywhere in this deck.

Why the ledger is the right chart, in four constraints it satisfies at once:

  • C5Density is ambient, not appended. A gap renders as a hollow mark — the “counted from days you logged” disclosure made visible, which no human app ships (§01).
  • §6.9It cannot blend. A grid physically has no way to collapse coverage and exposures into one number — the paper diary’s structural honesty, kept.
  • §5.1Two facts, two captions. Marks are coverage days (coveredDayIndices, non-treat meals) and wear the LOCKED coverage sentence; symptom-row density is days with any log — different day sets, never captioned as each other.
  • B-592The window explains itself. Rows stop at the target end while the day counter keeps counting — the overrun pair gets its missing sentence by geometry.
§ 04

§ 07

The spine — TR-1..8, proposed as binding

  • TR-1The ledger is a record, never a scoreboard. No percentage, grade, streak, or blended metric in any form (D2/§6.9 restated).
  • TR-2An exposure mark is ink, never rose. Rose belongs to the animal (safety registers), never to the record — a slip is a recorded fact, not a moral color.
  • TR-3A gap renders as a gap — hollow, honest, unshamed.
  • TR-4Safety faces stay plain (S1 inherited): replacements gain no receipts; in the room they lead, and the record renders unchanged below — a flag never deletes the record.
  • TR-5Mid-trial change never verdicts: no before/during comparison until completion; the adjacency sentence accompanies weekly counts wherever they render; density is inseparable from any symptom count.
  • TR-6The sheet states with denominators and ends at the report. Prep, never a second report, never triage — a live safety state flips its register; existing safety lanes are pointed to, not duplicated.
  • TR-7One predicate, painted. Every mark derives from TrialFacts arrays verbatim; coverage days and any-log days are different sets, never captioned as each other.
  • TR-8Belief vs evidence, inherited. The ledger spans the coverage window; the receipt itemizes the evidence window; nothing re-derives either.

The reintroduction/challenge phase (Monash’s scheduled challenge unit, vet-configured doses, counts-not-verdicts endpoint) is the deepest unclaimed water the research found — standard veterinary protocol, proven human tooling, zero pet competitors.
