04

Drawn for the decision

Three places the team disagreed and could not rule from words. Each pair is drawn on the same record so the difference is the only thing that moves. The frame marked current is the recommendation; its brief is on the issue.

The door on Home

Designer: one line felt small to you in September; the door should be a roomy, field-shaped box. Motion & IA: a box whose tap raises a sheet lies in its first frame, and Home carries no form (C-33); the room is the sheet's three-line field. PM decision needed: does the roomy promise live on Home or in the sheet?

Where Ask lands

Designer: the Ask verb answers inside the sheet, under the note, so the composer is one surface for telling and asking. Motion & IA: the sheet exits carrying the words and the built /ask answers, so one screen holds Ask's rules (D5, D8) and nothing forks. PM decision needed: one sheet that answers, or a composer that hands off.

The crisis register

Dr. Chen: "thrown up three times since noon and won't touch water" at 2am deserves more than a quiet arrival: a buzz, a takeover, the number ready to dial. Motion & IA: plainness is the severity signal; it lands within a frame, above everything, with one door and the save's buzz withheld. Louder teaches that quiet means fine. PM decision needed: the same physics with a withheld haptic, or a distinct register.
