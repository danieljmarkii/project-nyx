The weight card, with a band that makes room for the loss

§04

The rule, and the decision

Band widthThe smallest of ±10%, ±20%, ±30% of the first reading that holds every drawn reading. Never narrower than ±10%. Computed in weightBand, so the a11y label and the delta read the same band.
Past ±30%Clipped as today: hollow dot on the edge, its value printed, “N readings outside the band” in the delta for interior readings.
GridlinesBand edges dashed and labelled. When widened, the ±10% lines stay, fainter, also labelled. The first reading's level is dotted and unlabelled; its dot carries its value.
Last valueRight-aligned to its dot, above it when the dot sits in the lower half, below in the upper half. Never in the gutter.
SizePlot 96pt (was 56). First dot inset by its radius.
UnchangedNeutral grey dots (B-186: a weight trend never reassures), no fill, dots by date, the delta copy and its home-scale gate.
Deciding: what a reading outside ±10% looks like on Patterns.
Options: (A) fixed band, clipped hollow dot, as R4-4 ruled. (B) stepped band, recommended: a big loss looks big, and a small change still looks small because the band never narrows.
Consequence: B amends R4-4 and changes weightBand plus its tests; the layout fixes ship under either.
