The Signal already knows things.
Round 1 is about letting you see them.

Five inputs shaped this round: a code map of every Home surface, the Signal spec's own unfinished mandate, isolated interviews with Jordan, Sam, and Dr. Chen, and a research sweep of Oura, Whoop, Apple Health, Flighty, Gentler Streak, and Linear. They converge hard: keep the sentence in charge, put the evidence one glance away, and let the safety lane's plainness become the signal. Four rungs below, conservative → swing. Nothing is locked; react with R1-1…R1-6 at the end.

01

What we heard, compressed

Each stakeholder was interviewed in isolation against the live implementation and the same six candidate directions. The verdicts aligned to a degree we rarely get:

The research through-line matches: every benchmark app lands on sentence first, evidence one gesture away, mechanism visible on demand — and the craft leaders (Flighty, Linear) differentiate not by adding visuals but by showing the causal machinery and dimming everything else. Flighty doesn't tell you the flight is delayed; it shows you where the inbound aircraft is, and the conclusion becomes yours. That is exactly what "5 of 7 episodes within 30 minutes" wants to be.

Dr. Chen's discipline, in one line: "A visualization that can only fit the numerator should not ship." Five dots huddled against a meal marker is a picture of causation — the strip is honest only if the meals without episodes and the episodes outside the window are drawn too. Hard vetoes: contributor bars, confidence meters, hero numbers shed of their qualifiers, trend arrows on the safety lane, any motion or decoration on a safety card.

02

The spine — rules that hold at every rung

Nine rules distilled from the interviews, the consult, and the shipped invariants. Every frame below obeys them; ratifying them (R1-2) is more important than picking a rung.

  1. The register drop. Safety cards stay austere — text, rail, sample line; no evidence graphic on the card face, no motion, no decorative ground. As benign cards get richer, plainness itself signals severity.
  2. The control-side rule. No evidence visual ships numerator-only. If the control margin doesn't fit on the card, the visual moves to the expanded state where it does.
  3. No borrowed authority. No composite scores, contributor bars, meters, or percentages. The sample line is the honest confidence display.
  4. No hero numbers. The sentence stays the headline; counts stay subordinate with their qualifiers attached.
  5. Change is worded, never arrowed. New / Seen more than last week / Seen less lately on the insight lane only — never "improving," and safety findings keep change inside the sentence.
  6. Quiet is labeled. A presence-gated surface renders absence as one explicit line, never as silent shortening.
  7. The record stays in daylight. No night grounds on the Signal or any record surface; night remains the working-on-the-pet's-behalf register.
  8. Grazer-honest intake visuals. Day-level, drawn against the pet's own baseline band, never event-level activity.
  9. Evidence one gesture away. Card face = sentence + glance evidence; tap = the mechanism; the safety tap = the phone-call script.

08

What this means for B-284's N4, N7, and D8

D8 (the Signal night ground): the team recommends closing it light, without building the night variant. The evidence stacked up on one side — both owners rejected the dark card in isolation (Sam: "a mild note reads as an omen"; Jordan: "a coworker, not a planetarium"), your own iteration-3 note leaned the same way, the PTR night band already failed on-device and was retracted, and Dr. Chen vetoes decorative grounds anywhere near the safety lane. B-284 §7.4 as written wants an on-device A/B of two built variants; building a variant every signal says will die is spec-faithful but wasteful. Your call — R1-4.

N4's content system is absorbed by rung 1 (presence rule, light-card polish, the per-row system). N7 is rung 3, plus the quiet-is-labeled amendment. If you ratify the absorption (R1-5), this track's requirements doc becomes the canonical spec for the surface and B-284 gets a pointer note — one spec per surface, no drift.
