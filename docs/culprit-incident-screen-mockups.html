§1

What ships today, and what the owner never sees

The log modal saves, calls router.back(), and the named card lands over Home for five seconds with Undo and Change time. The photo's read is kicked off in the background (compress, upload, then the vision call) and lands in event_ai_analysis some seconds later. Nothing routes to the incident screen. The read is reachable only through History, a row tap, and a scroll, which is why the differentiator is invisible to the person who just took the photo.

§2

Three routing directions D1 ruled · R1

The constraint on every direction is Principle 1. The quick-log page of the design principles says the confirmation is "a brief completion state, then back to home", and the 2am test is one hand, in the dark, under ten seconds. So a direction is honest only if it adds nothing to the log itself and takes the owner somewhere only when there is something to show. A photoless vomit has no read; routing it to a screen with an empty photo hero would be a punishment for not taking a photo.

R1 · Land on the record ruled — D1

After Save, the modal is replaced by the incident screen rather than dismissed, so the owner arrives on the record with the photo they just took and the read pending. The named card still lands, now over the incident screen, so Undo and Change time survive the move. Back is one tap to Home. Scope: photographed vomit and stool only.

R3 · The escalation stripPersists until opened. Overlaps CUL-208 (a standing flag for worth_a_call) and the Signal's own acute incident_red_flag card, which never folds. Two surfaces for one finding is the risk.

Designer: Principle 1 ends the log on Home. R1 adds a screen the owner has to leave. At 2am that is a tax.

PM, Dr. Chen: The read is the differentiator and, when it escalates, the one sentence the owner must see. Today it is three taps deep and nobody reads it.

Jordan: If the app is about to tell me something about the thing I just photographed, I will wait eight seconds. If it is going to spin for a minute, I will not.

Resolution — ruled by the PM as D1 + D2 (2026-09-05): R1 scoped to the photographed vomit and stool, because that is the only log with a read to show, and the record already IS the confirmation. The escalation-after-you-left case is not R1's to solve: the Signal's acute card already exists for it. Verify it fires from the per-incident row at generate-signal/detection.ts before relying on it.

Deciding: the scope of the landing.

§3

The incident screen D3 ruled · S-A, hero kept

Once the route lands here, the screen has to earn it. Both shapes keep the record in daylight (the D8 rule), keep the verdict copy verbatim from the shipped enum, and keep the read dismissible and never editable while the observations stay editable. What changes is what leads.

S-A · The read leads, the photo stays the hero ruled — D3

The shipped order, tightened. The read moves up under the record with a rail, rose when it escalates, quiet otherwise, and the observations become a compact two-column grid that folds to a strip once seen. The photo stays full-width because it is what the owner just took and how they confirm this is the right incident.

§4

The arrival, drawn with the fold's physics D4 ruled

The design principles allow exactly one considered animation: the transition from "building your picture" to a real insight should feel like something arrived. The per-incident read is that class of moment, one incident down. So the arrival borrows the fold's rule, the rail is the continuous thread: the pending line is a 16pt tick of rail beside the whorl; when the read lands, the rail grows first, the box opens around it with one felt settle, and the sentence lands a beat after the box. Nothing is added that was not already there; the line the owner watched while waiting is the line beside the verdict.

Same physics on every verdict. A Worth a call does not arrive harder or louder; it arrives on a rose rail with no haptic, because silence on safety is structural. Reduced motion is a crossfade. App blur finishes the arrival, never pauses it.

§5

The states the landing has to survive

Routing to a screen that is waiting on a server means the screen must be honest about every way that wait ends. These are all already states of VomitAnalysisSection; the route just makes them visible on arrival instead of on a later visit.

Two triggers race today. The log path kicks the read after upload; the detail screen mounts and kicks it again. Both are idempotent on event_id, but on arrival the mount trigger would fire before the photo has uploaded, and the function would answer "not enough to say" on a photo that is still in flight. The build has to gate the mount trigger while a pending row exists or the upload chain is running. Adjacent to CUL-143.
