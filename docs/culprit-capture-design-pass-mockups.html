Adding a food from its label, three ways

The flow an owner walks to put a new food in the library: snap the front of the bag, add the ingredients if they like, wait while the label is read, confirm what was read, and (from the log sheet) log the meal. D-1defect · live

The failed band promises a retry that never comes. "We'll retry extraction in the background": nothing retries; the only retry is the button on the food's own page. Offline, the photos never uploaded, so that button cannot read them either. Medication capture's twin ("the label photo is saved either way") is untrue when the upload is what failed. An owner who waits for the read leaves the protein set empty, and the add time trial heads up has nothing to compare. Filed as one class (Live harm).

Options: (A) Say what is true: "Couldn't read the label. Fill in what you know below; you can try reading it again from the food's page." (and offline: P8). (B) Build the background retry the sentence promises. Recommended (A) now; (B) is new behaviour for a later row. Direction: QUIET today (it can delay a warning); (A) removes the false promise and adds nothing quieter.
