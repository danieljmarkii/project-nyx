§2 · Ruled: Variant B · live: how loud

The warning, at two intensities

Variant B is ruled — heads-up plus the quiet add line. What's live is the visual register. The PM asked for a danger-style warning; the team's counterweight is real and recorded here rather than argued away: this would be Culprit's first danger-styled surface (even the shipped chicken flag and the detail-screen safety notes are calm teal), the record only knows the food is absent from a list — often because the vet okayed it and it was never entered — and red beside an "add it" action reads as an accusation the sheet then walks back. The middle treatment keeps the PM's "duty to show" — tinted, barred, unmissable, clearly a warning — without asserting harm the record can't back. Rule it by how the frames read.

§4 · When it says nothing, and why

The silence states

Same discipline as the shipped flag: every uncertainty suppresses; nothing ever reassures. There is no "this food is fine" state anywhere in this feature.

no running trial

No trial genuinely running

PM-ruled this round. And per the B-422 convention, "running" reads isTrialRunning (target + grace), never the raw status column — a trial stale-active since March is quiet here.

unknown

Trial list not loaded yet

Fresh install, mid-sync, offline cold start — an unhydrated list must not be read as an empty one.

out_of_window

Feeding outside the trial dates

A backfilled meal from before the trial started says nothing about the trial.

off_diet_protein

Panel read, protein conflict

The stronger shipped flag fires instead (frame 1). Never both — one heads-up per moment.

permitted

On the list

Nothing renders. A permitted food is never praised — absence of a flag is not a verdict (G2).

ledger spent

Already told this trial

One heads-up per food per trial, counted in heads-ups given — never in meals fed.
