The call splits on one question: is the distribution the value? Your original read of this receipt — "closer almost to a distribution curve, uber uber informative" — is the case for A: we own the payload, the per-episode times exist, and withholding them is the anti-viz move. If the goal is just to stop over-promising, B keeps the countable dots you like and drops the fake axis for nothing. C is the one option this whole page argues against. The tension to weigh (Data Scientist ⚔ Dr. Chen): A is the only option that shows the ~9-min pile-up — and the only one that can invite a timing over-read the evidence can't support (§9.2: timing is anamnesis, not a regurgitation-vs-vomiting differentiator). B sidesteps both.
