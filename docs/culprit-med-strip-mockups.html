Notes for the decision

  • A vs B/C is the real conflict. The trial card's ratified rule says "logging is the FAB — a second door to the same room is not a feature" (that's A). The briefing's Care-due row was adopted with a one-tap Log dose in it (that's B/C). Picking an option resolves which text governs medication.
  • The progress bar is day progress, never compliance. A bar bound to doses-given is the exact defect the trial card rebuild deleted. Ongoing and ad-hoc meds get no bar at all — nothing false to draw.
  • "No dose logged yet today" only renders for meds with a known daily cadence (a regimen with doses-per-day). For ad-hoc meds the app doesn't know a dose was expected, so it states recency and stops.
  • With D3 (card per med), option C's weight compounds. If C wins, a slimming pass is available without changing the ruling: the Log dose button could appear only on the card whose day is incomplete, with logged-today cards collapsing to one line.
  • Day counters wait on the B-441 day-math fix; nothing here needs new schema.
PM decision (D1): A (context only, tap-through) · B (today's action + one-tap) · C (both)? Team lean: C for a fixed course, A's quietness for everything else — i.e. the one-tap earns its place where a course is live and today is incomplete, and stays out of the way otherwise.
