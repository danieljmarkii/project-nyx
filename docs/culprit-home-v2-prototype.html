What you are looking at

The spine the system owns: the instrument, Signals with any safety finding, the FAB. The tray the owner can arrange: the door, the trial cells, the medication row, the Patterns index.

The rules the prototype keeps: the safety card stays plain and never hides; a folded card comes back when the record moves, never on a timer; a note is quoted, never counted; nothing green about a quiet day; the cap shows only inside the sheet; no haptic on anything safety-shaped (the arrival beat is silent on a deflection).

Not in this prototype: real data, the Foods and Pet tabs, the trial screen, the full History filters, Patterns.
