§3 · Your question: what else belongs in the AI export

Almost everything Nyx's record holds belongs in it. What makes it work is the layout, not the size.

Nyx's record since May 14 is about 1,360 entries. That's long for a person and ordinary for an assistant. The Oct 11 study of AI readers found the weak spot is recounting a long list, so the file leads with numbers the app already counted, then gives every entry its own line with date, year, time zone and source. Nothing in the file speaks to the AI; it describes itself.

  • Stool (Nyx has none on file)

  • Changes at home: new litter, travel, a new pet
  • What the vet asked you to watch for, and when to stop a test
