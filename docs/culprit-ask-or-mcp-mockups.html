Ask or MCP

Neither, as asked. Ask and a connector are two doors onto the same missing thing: a facts layer that computes what the chicken read built by hand, with every number carrying its window, its denominator and what it can't tell. Build that layer, open it first as a free export any assistant can read, keep Ask on facts and triage, and keep judgment about one pet out of Culprit's voice. Before any of it, put the weight row where owners look: the read's most important finding already sits in Nyx's stored Signal, and Home has no row to draw it.
