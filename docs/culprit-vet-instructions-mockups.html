Signs that mean call today
For a cat. Call your vet now if you see:
  • Breathing fast or open mouthed while resting
  • Straining or crying in the tray with little or nothing coming, going in and out (especially a male cat)
  • Can't stand or walk, collapsed, wobbling
  • A fit or seizure
  • Pale, white or blue gums
And call today if she's subdued and not eating, vomiting or hiding, or hasn't eaten for a day.

6 · Decision 4 · for you

"When the course ends", if the course changes

The owner picks When prednisolone ends; the app stores the day after the last dose, Oct 10. Then the vet stops it early (last dose Oct 2) or extends it by phone (last dose Oct 24). The design pass sharpened this: the real risk is not opening early, it's closing early. On Oct 10 a cough can still be hidden by a steroid that's still running.
