The vet's "call me if", held and shown on its date

At Nyx's visit on Sep 16 her vet said to call if she was still coughing after the full strength prednisolone. She was, and the app never noticed. This round draws the feature end to end with her case, then draws the three decisions you asked to see. Every count on these screens is illustrative, not her real record.

10 · When nothing was logged current

The door still opens. A zero is never written as "no coughing", and a guard in the code holds that wording.

The answersI've called and Not needed are stored on the instruction. Booked a visit opens booking. Not yet writes nothing: the door leaves Home that night and the visit page shows "Open: call about the cough". Never asked again.
Why "I've called" is new storageThe care state's call record needs a logged symptom to hang from, and this call answers the vet, not a log. So the answer lives on the instruction. A team call; reversible.
What never happensThe app never checks the vet's condition against the logs, never says Nyx is better, and never lets the instruction quiet a safety warning. A cough that turns into breathing trouble still gets the direct words, the same day.

4 · Decision 2 · open

Things the vet says that the app can't count

Vets tell cat owners "call if she's hiding", "if she's straining in the litter box", "if she's eating less". None of those is a log the app can count. The choice is whether the row accepts them at all, and how.

Designer and SamHalf of what a cat owner is told doesn't fit six chips. A short label shown back word for word, with no count, makes the row useful for cats.
Trust & Safety and Dr. ChenFixed choices with no typed text is the privacy design. And "not eating" is an emergency signal the app already handles directly; counting meals would treat eating as a preference.

5 · Decision 4 · open

"When the course ends", if the course changes

The owner picks When prednisolone ends and the app stores Oct 10. Then life happens: the vet stops the course early for side effects, or extends it by phone. Which date should the door use?

Case 1 · stopped early

The owner marks prednisolone Stopped on Oct 2. The vet's question ("is the cough still there after the course?") is now due on Oct 3, not Oct 10.

Case 2 · extended

The vet adds two weeks by phone; the course now ends Oct 24. The vet's question is now due Oct 25. But a late door is the risky direction.

A · moves earlier, never laterA stop pulls the door forward. An extension leaves it on Oct 10, so the owner may be asked a little early. Early is the safe mistake. The visit page always shows the current date and the owner can change it.
B · fixed at saveSimplest, and matches an existing rule that a link never moves a date. But a course stopped on Oct 2 leaves the vet's question unasked for eight days.
C · follows both waysAlways "right" if the course record is right. An extension logged by mistake, or a course never marked ended, pushes the door late or forever.

To react to: is asking a little early (A) acceptable to you, or would you rather the date follow the course exactly (C)?

6 · Decision 5 · open

Does the vet's instruction pause the "worth booking" ask?

When a sign keeps coming back, Home already says something like "Cough on 9 days in 3 weeks. Worth booking a visit." But Nyx just saw the vet, and the vet set a date to check back. Should Home keep asking her owner to book a visit the vet effectively already planned?

Dr. ChenThe vet knows about the cough and chose the date. Asking the owner to book sooner contradicts the vet and teaches them to ignore the app.
Data ScientistAgreed it should pause, but only if the owner says so. One tap shouldn't silently write a second rule with its own expiry. Make it a visible checkbox.

7 · Out of v1

What this round deliberately leaves out

"If it gets worse" or "more than twice a day"A condition the owner judges every day, not a date. The app's existing safety warnings cover the dangerous end.
Reading the discharge sheetNo AI or photo reading of the vet's notes in v1. A later version could suggest the row from a photo, with the owner confirming.
The vet reportThe instruction stays off the report and out of Ask for now. "You asked us to watch the cough; here is the log since" is a strong later candidate.
Medication or ER instructions"Stop the pred if…" or "go to the ER if…" are never modelled. A dated ER instruction would mean "wait until Tuesday".
