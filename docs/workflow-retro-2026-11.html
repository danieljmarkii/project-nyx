The filing rule is followed (99 of 117 PRs) and absorbs about one find in five, but the underlying rate did not move.

  • Fixes spawn findings in chains. Four chains were traced, each three to five generations deep. Two had not converged. The two pass cap limits a PR, not a chain.
  • Reviewers audit the whole surface. At least 14 of 41 reviewer findings were already on main, not caused by the change under review.
  • "Live harm" is never challenged. 308 such findings in 60 days: zero duplicates, one cancellation, fixed in a median of one day.
  • Outside evidence agrees. In one small study a second review pass in sequence added 0.37 real findings and 3.24 false ones per item. Parallel reviewers with a step that tries to refute each finding did better.
Fix · D3 and D8

Build the ship bar you ruled on 22 October. It is in no file a session reads. Replayed on this sample, it and four filing fixes reroute 20 to 24 of the 29 low value findings and none of the 25 real ones.

10 · Looked at and rejected

What not to do

Move to Claude's native ProjectsIts threads do not load our repo hooks, where the production gate lives.
A merge queue, or an org move to get oneThe problem it prevents happened once in 304 merges.
More reviewers or more passesExtra passes add false findings faster than real ones.
Defaults that expire into "approved"One first hand account saw a veto window become rubber stamping in three weeks.
A "PM twin" that rules for youPredicting one person's judgments reaches about 72% to 75%.
Trust an agent tester's passAnthropic's own write up: it finds real problems, then approves anyway.
Larger rowsConflicts and cost both rise with size.
Delete instructions on a study's say soThose studies measured task success, not whether a rule prevents harm. Prune against a replay of past rows.

What to trust less. Every figure for your minutes is an estimate. Test time savings are arithmetic on measured step times and are untested. The session sample covers ten days. Outside reliability claims for agent testing all come from vendors. Reddit was read through one archive route, about 65 threads.

Never measured, and worth starting: your minutes per ruling and per week, who performed each merge, how often you accept a recommendation, defects that reach the phone, and cost per merged PR that survives two weeks.
