The pet's page and its forms, three ways

The Pet tab is where a pet's identity lives: the photo, the name, the facts, what they have been diagnosed with and what they take, and the doors to the vet. Two forms open from it, Edit profile and Add condition. Every state is drawn as it ships today, as a faithful move onto the shared parts, and as a design pass under Principles 3, 5, 8 and 9. You rule per screen or per element in the ledger at the foot. Issue: CUL-1989; the build that follows your picks is CUL-1911.

Waiting on your ruling

Nothing on this page is ruled yet. The faithful briefs (F) are team calls you can veto; the design pass briefs (P) and the two scope calls (S) are yours.

Medicines are the revamp's

The medication revamp (CUL-1903, CUL-1902) ruled that medicine lives with the pet and that setup is redrawn from the label (GC-5 A), which replaces AddMedicationModal in all three of its hosts (this tab, a vet call, after a vet visit). So, as the issue asked, this round does not draw that modal, and the Current medications card is drawn as it ships plus its faithful token moves only; its design pass column says "the revamp's". Brief S1.

One defect found, filed live

Switching pets on this tab while a read fails leaves the previous pet's conditions and medicines on screen, and "Log a dose" then writes against the other pet's course (§05, D-1). Filed as one class, Live harm: CUL-2174. Every design pass frame draws the fix.

D-1defect · live

A failed read after a pet switch shows the previous pet's rows. The name is the switcher and the page changes in place; when the new pet's conditions or medicines read fails, neither list is cleared, so Biscuit's rows sit under "Mochi". "Log a dose" then writes a dose for Mochi linked to Biscuit's course (a local first write, so it succeeds offline), and "Resolve" resolves Biscuit's condition. A first load failure shows the empty state over a read that never answered (C-12). Filed as one class, Live harm: CUL-2174.

Options: (A) Key every list to the pet it was read for (clear on switch, like the vet visits card) and give a failed read error plus retry. (B) (A) plus a server same pet guard on a dose's course (a migration, its own PR). Recommended (A) now, in its own fix row (no ruling needed); (B) as the backstop. Direction: QUIET today; (A) adds nothing quieter.
