Culprit Round 1 · current

App polish — the first mock round

Out of the 2026-08-22 design session: four lenses walked every screen — Jordan (capture, the 10-second test), Sam (two cats, multi-med), the Designer (periphery + consistency), and the Product Owner (what's already filed in Linear). The verdict: the foundations are genuinely strong — the token system, the night register, the one-tap meal path, the honest empty states on new surfaces. The gaps are felt gaps: chrome that never got an identity pass, one moment the principles promised and we never built, six different ways of saying "logged," and an app that mostly doesn't render in its own typeface.

This round mocks the six opportunities the PM greenlit. Frames are day-register depictions with real copy; motion and touch are annotated where they can't be drawn. React prompts are at the end (R-1 … R-7).

Tracked as CUL-580 · defect fallout filed as CUL-574–579 · frames use the demo pet Biscuit

§06 · The feel track

A sense of touch

There is not a single haptic in the product — expo-haptics isn't installed. For a one-handed, in-the-dark, glance-free app, touch is the cheapest premium signal we're not sending. The vocabulary below is tone-aware by rule: it follows the existing celebrate/calm split, and it is deliberately silent where the record is worrying — a haptic must never make bad news feel like a reward.

MomentHapticWhy
Meal / dose / routine log commitssuccess (soft double)the earned beat — pairs with the gold moment
Symptom log commitssingle soft tapconfirmation without celebration — the calm tone's twin
Chip select, intake / adherenceselection tickchips are the app's hands; they should click
FAB open · pet switchlight impactmode changes you should feel, not watch
Pull-to-refresh thresholdlight impactthe night band gets a physical edge
Destructive confirm (Remove, End trial)rigidweight where weight is due
Safety card arrival · red-flag readsnone — deliberateplainness is the severity signal (S1); silence is part of it

One lib/haptics.ts with these seven verbs, consumed at the moment stores — so a new log path inherits its haptic the same way it inherits its card. iOS system haptic settings are respected automatically; Engineer sizes this S.

React

Calls to make on this round

R-1Tab bar shape

Deciding: the bar's permanent identity. Options: A refined wordmarks · B glyphs + labels (team rec — discoverability plus one icon language) · C = B with the pet-avatar Pet tab (Designer's favorite; slightly louder chrome). Consequence: B or C commissions the four glyphs into CUL-493's family; C also answers Sam's whose-app-is-this at zero cost.

R-2Arrival treatment

Deciding: how the first insight arrives. Options: 2a dawn sweep (team rec — quiet, no new anatomy) · 2b whorl-drawn (more branded, borrows loading anatomy for a non-loading moment). Consequence: either ships with the once-ever + reduced-motion + never-for-safety rules; 2b adds a dependency on the whorl's draw path.

R-3Completion convergence

Deciding: adopt the two-register system (named card + in-place beat), retire the white takeover, beat carries the sentence, Undo armed, capture screens route through the real cards. Options: adopt whole (team rec) · adopt minus Undo (if undo semantics need their own pass) · keep takeover for weight only. Consequence: closes CUL-368's class and the D1 register sprawl; Undo touches soft-delete flows so it gets its own small spec inside the PR.

R-4Trend v2 register

Deciding: whether the Trend sublabel drops arrows/verdicts for the count-anchored pair. Options: adopt, routed through Dr. Chen with CUL-568/571 (team rec) · AA-fixes only for now. Consequence: AA repoints (CUL-578) ship either way; the register half completes the Change-Contract voice on Home.

R-5Typeface rollout approach (B-061)

Deciding: how Geist reaches the other ~70% of the app. Options: ThemedText wrapper, swept tab-by-tab (team rec — no magic, honors the house convention; churn is the accepted cost) · default-Text weight shim (one file, but exactly the "magic" the conventions ban). Consequence: unblocks CUL-364; each tab's sweep is a small, verifiable PR with on-device before/after.

R-6Haptics vocabulary

Deciding: greenlight the seven-verb vocabulary as specced. Options: yes, shipped inside the R-3 PR so beats and taps land together (team rec) · trim to log-commits only first. Consequence: adds expo-haptics (managed-workflow safe); the silence-for-safety rule becomes a written invariant beside S1.

R-7Header pulse

Deciding: the standing Open Question — ring-train vs dot-breathe on the header mark. Options: dot-breathe only (team rec — matches the Landing's retirement of rings; kills the stutter shape) · keep rings. Consequence: one CulpritMark change; ratifying means the §3 pulse-contract Tier-2 edit gets written.
