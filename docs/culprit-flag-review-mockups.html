Today: a flag is easy to raise and hard to answer

The Home red flag card (B-340) fires on a single flagged photo, and its design record gives the reason: "false positives are cheap for the owner to clear by editing the structured field, which clears the card by construction." That holds in the database. On the phone, clearing one costs the four frames below. And nothing anywhere records the opposite answer: an owner who looks and agrees has no way to say so.

T4 · After SaveThe grid takes the owner's word. The verdict above it does not (the read is "dismissible, never editable"), and Home keeps its card until the Signal next rebuilds: a pull to refresh, the next log, or up to 24 hours.

One screen, two answers: "Worth a call" over "Blood: None visible" (CUL-409).
Three defects, not one. There is no door from the warning to the photo; the answer is an edit form rather than an answer; and once given, the answer is honoured by Home (late) and by the vet report (silently) while the record's own verdict, the Design v2 day spine and a Re-run analysis all keep contradicting it. Replacing the photo is the sharpest case: the old correction silently applies to the new photo on Home and the report.
