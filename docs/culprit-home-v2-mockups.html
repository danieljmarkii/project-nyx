DC-3 — Full screen, and chat history

Deciding: whether the sheet grows to the built full screen (a layout call) and whether conversations persist (a T&S ruling).

Options: the sheet grows to full-screen /ask on drag, expand, or a second turn; no durable history (Ask D8 as ruled; B-375 stays parked) — recommended · full screen plus a persisted, deletable history behind the title (reopens D8, the §10 T&S posture, the export and deletion cascade).

Consequence: persistence is a schema-and-privacy track of its own; the layout here does not depend on it.

DC-4 — All logging through the FAB (re-rules B-614 D1)

Deciding: whether Home keeps any one-tap write outside the FAB.

Options: FAB only — the med row becomes context (as drawn), the "Given now" confirm retires · keep the one confirmation the spec already carved out — a control that writes a row the app could already describe (the med dose) — and add no others — recommended by Principle 2 and Sam's 9pm; the Designer is fine either way.

Consequence: "FAB only" is a Tier-2 edit to the med-strip spec (D1 C → A) and removes a shipped write path; "keep" leaves Home with exactly one confirm and zero forms.

DC-5 — Arrange Home

Deciding: whether Home v2 ships any owner configuration.

Options: none (curation only, the health-app answer) · reorder + hide over a fixed spine (Signals with safety, Today, the FAB never move or hide), device-local, behind the same flag — recommended as a v2.1 rung once the modules exist · a module gallery with add / settings (the resource-intensive version).

Consequence: the module registry the hybrid needs is the same refactor the drawn Home needs anyway; the cost of configurability is mostly in the spec (every module's empty / thin / safety states) and the QA matrix, not the code.

DC-6 — The name

Deciding: what the two-verb surface is called on the pill, the door, and the sheet.

Options: (a) keep "Ask" as the AI verb's name; the composer is unnamed and titled by its object, "Nyx's record"; the note is a plain verb, "Save note", like every other free write — recommended: the CTA you liked already is the name, the note never reads as AI input, and D7's constraint holds by construction · (b) rename the surface "Notes" and demote Ask to a verb inside it (diary drift; hides the delightful half) · (c) "Record" as the pill (collides with History; reads as a verb).

Two rules for the spec when D7 closes: the note verb never gains a brand or a noun of its own (that is the diary), and the word "AI" appears as a chip and a receipt, never as a name.

Consequence: one string on the header pill today; the door's copy; the sheet title; any store copy before public exposure (the D7 constraint).

Still open: D1 (is note capture the right feature — every frame here treats the note as the door's default verb) · D6 (verbatim-only; report by default vs toggle) · DB-3 (rose marks at display size, adversarial-gated) · DB-4 (the free class amendment to the Ask spec).
