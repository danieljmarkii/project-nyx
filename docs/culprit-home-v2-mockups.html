04

Six decisions, each pointing at frames

Rule from the briefs alone; the frames are the evidence. D1 first — it decides whether the Tell row exists in any direction.

D1 — Is note capture the right feature? (the PM's own question)

Deciding: whether Home gains a place to tell the record something in free text, and how far the text goes.

Options: (a) Yes, as a plain dated note row on Home — no AI, no schema — recommended. Every lens wanted the note (Jordan: "half of what I'd tell the vet isn't a vomit or a meal"; Sam: "the note comes before the decision"; Dr. Chen: "the context a vomit row lacks"); none wanted a box as the hero. It costs `other` + `notes` + a source stamp, rides the events queue, undo, wipe and RLS for free (Engineering, Data). · (b) (a) plus the AI proposal (N1's second state) as PR 2, behind consent D10 and caps — the photo-extraction shape extended to text. · (c) No — notes stay in the log form's optional field.

Consequence: (a) ships in B′ or D without waiting on the taxonomy wave, the consent ruling, or the `ask` redeploy; (b) is one new Edge Function and a T&S review; (c) leaves the in-between observations where they are today — unwritten. Frames N1, N2, T1.

D2 — The direction

Deciding: which Home mock round 2 refines.

Options: B′ "Since you last looked" carrying the Tell row — recommended: it answers the staleness complaint structurally (the first line is about the record since the last open), keeps safety leading by position, and v1's fold is already its Standing register. · D "The Open Page" — capture first; drawn for Sam's evening, carries the Designer's own Principle-3 dissent. · E "Ask-forward" — drawn at the PM's request; every lens objects to it as the hero (frame E). · C "The Briefing" — survives as B′'s first line, not as a screen.

Consequence: B′ needs a Tier-2 edit to design-principles §3 (D5); D needs the same components in a different order; E needs a Principle-7 ruling first (D3); C needs a composition layer and an adversarial pass per template. Frames §01, §02.

D3 — Ask on Home: how prominent?

Deciding: where Ask sits on Home and what the free tier permits there.

Options: (a) Recall chips under the strips when the record can answer them, plus the Ask verb on the Tell row — never a box as the lead — recommended. (Sam moved to this: "a chip, not a box, never the lead"; Jordan: "Ask-on-Home = the chips.") · (b) A prominent field (frame E) — honest only if the deterministic answers (recall, counts, the rundown) are free on Home and only synthesis is Premium, the entry never shows a meter or a lock, and a symptom-shaped question never gets a Premium sentence. · (c) The header pill only (today).

Consequence: (a) is client-only and needs nothing from the `ask` redeploy chain; (b) reopens the Ask spec's D3 (the 3-conversation teaser) and D5 ("chrome, not a card") and raises Dr. Chen's deflection bar ("should I worry" becomes the daily question); (c) changes nothing. Frames E, B′, N1.

D4 — Patterns on Home

Deciding: what of the Patterns dashboard reaches Home.

Options: (a) "Home speaks the delta; Patterns shows the level" — no card migrates; the Trend zone becomes an index of ≤3 level strips, each a doorway — recommended (Designer; the research's split by horizon, merge by promotion finding; Strava's ungated promotion as the foil). · (b) Migrate two or three cards (symptom counts, weight, intake) onto Home. · (c) Nothing; the "All patterns" doorway as today.

Consequence: either (a) or (b) is gated on CUL-805 (Patterns' calm colour on a falling count has no density check — tier 2 reassures where tier 1 may not) and on one rule: every Home number is the same exported function Patterns calls, same window, window spoken. Frame P1.

D5 — The 3-section rule

Deciding: what replaces "Home has three zones" in design-principles §3 (Tier-2, PM-approved edit).

Options: (a) A height-and-register budget — exactly one element at display size per open (a safety card always takes it when present); the first viewport holds the display element + the standing strips + the Tell row; standing is never capped and never dropped, height is — recommended. Four registers: Moved · Standing · Today (with Tell) · Trend. Nobody in the research documents a fixed section count; the budget has support. · (b) Keep the cap and fit the registers into it. · (c) No rule — trust each mock round.

Consequence: (a) is the proposed §3 wording plus "Home is for understanding and telling"; keep verbatim: safety leads and is never dropped, no feed, no nav menu, no upsell. Frames §01.

D6 — What the app may do with a note

Deciding: two conflicts the lenses could not close among themselves.

Read-back: verbatim only, event-scoped, quoted when asked — recommended (Dr. Chen, Data, T&S: "quoted, never counted"; synthesis over free text is diagnosis by paraphrase) vs. the Designer's warmer "this week you noticed…" template that joins two notes. · The report: Dr. Chen wants an "Owner observations" section by default, verbatim and dated; the Designer and T&S want notes to reach the report only behind an explicit toggle.

Consequence: verbatim-only keeps the engine blind to text by construction and makes N2 buildable now; the report choice decides whether a note's field says "goes on the report" (default) or "you choose what the vet sees" (toggle). Frames N2, §02 9:15pm.

Cross-reference, not a seventh brief: CUL-552 D10 (consent scope) now has two dependents. If it lands whole-boundary, every text-to-model path here inherits one gate; if photos-only, the Tell row's PR 2 needs its own. The T&S position is on that issue.
