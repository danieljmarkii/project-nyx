Booked, Today, Visited. One page, every door lands on it, the clock does the rest.

B · The vet area — one card, one page, one add

Report, visits and files were three cards. They are one place now.

The PM's ruling: the vet experience must be cohesive. The Pet tab carries one Vet card; it opens one page that holds the next visit, the report, every visit and every document. A document from a visit sits under that visit; one that came from nowhere in particular sits beneath. Vet Files' rules do not change — a document never mints or re-dates a visit (D7), a link is set from the document or from the visit — only the doors merge.

E · Motion and moments — what "delightful" means here

Three moments, on the vocabulary the app already has.

Delight in this app is a moment that says something true, once, and gets out of the way — the Signal fold, the incident read's arrival, the completion cards. The visit gets three: the page turning its face, the tick that opens a place for the answer, and the plan arriving on the saved moment. Every one reuses a shipped module; none touches a safety rule.

F · Ruled, open, and deliberately not drawn

What this page settles, and what it leaves for an issue

Ruled · the one page. The visit is one page with three faces (§A). Spec §4.1 B–D become one section; the C–D frames are archive.
Ruled · the verdict's home. A per-visit verdict row (the team, by the PM's delegation). Own schema PR, Migration Safety Pre-flight, guards/visitReaders gains the columns.
Ruled · the vet area. One card, one page, one add (§B). Vet Files' model unchanged; the surface merges.
Ruled · the window. Governs Home's strip only; gates no door, no field.
Open · the intake row's owner (CUL-950). The record's count wins when both fire, the Signal's sentence beneath or dropped; the local read un-gated from the trial. Needs adversarial-reviewer on the fallback never going silent.
Open · two phones, one login. The attend write refuses an already-attended appointment (one clause, in the verdict PR); the notes clobber sits against the last-write-wins hard constraint and is the PM's call.
Option · AI over the visit (§D). Waits on the D2-class ruling and CUL-848's disclosure.
Not drawn · the recording. Punted to v1.x as ruled (G5); its frames stay in the archive (C2, C3).
Not drawn · a recurring care task. "Clean the ears every 4 weeks" has no home in the medication model; named on the issue when filed.
Not drawn · live suggestions. Punted, again; the dissent stays recorded in the spec.
