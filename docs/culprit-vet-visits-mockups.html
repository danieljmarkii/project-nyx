The Pet tab holds visits. Home carries the one that is close.

D · After the visit — the plan becomes records

The rows read the record first. Confirm what exists; add only what is new.

The PM's question — "if we have a medication logged, how do we start that med" — is answered by not asking. Each plan row looks at the record before it speaks: an existing course is confirmed with one chip, a running trial is kept, ended or switched, and Add appears only for something the record does not hold. Every door is a pushed route with the visit as its origin; it pops back reading Linked. Nothing is stored twice.

F · Open decisions and what is deliberately not drawn

Four open items, each decided on its issue at build

Delete a visit while the report is held (VV-1). The Product Owner's catch: `vet_visits.deleted_at` reaches the report's window reader inside `generate-report`, which sits under the CUL-19 deploy hold. Until it runs, Home says "since last visit" moved back while the production report still keys off the deleted row. Options: the delete confirm names the lag; visit delete waits for the redeploy; CUL-19 names this reader as a third rider. Decide on VV-1.
The window default (VV-3). Drawn at five days; the PM is fine with three to seven. A constant, changed in one place.
The spoken announcement (v1.x, VV-5). T&S: default-on and locked. Designer: tap-only. Dr. Chen: it is what makes the staff comfortable. Unchanged; decided when v1.x is picked up.
Files and Visits as two cards (tabled by the PM). "It's now weird to have files as well as visits." Not v1; a later question about whether Vet Files becomes a section inside Visits.
Live suggestions in the room. Punted by the PM; the team's dissent stands recorded in the spec §9.
The microphone in the submission binary. A toggle hides a screen; it cannot hide a purpose string. VV-5 never merges before the submission build is cut.
