Culprit Round 1 · CUL-618
Nothing here is built. This is the decision round for CUL-618 — the fork the issue names, plus the fourth option the Designer added on the CUL-600 comment. Three briefs at the bottom; the frames exist so the choice is looked at rather than described. No app code changed on this branch.

The Pet tab shows a switcher that doesn’t switch

In this app a pet’s face has always meant “open the switcher” — the Home header, the FAB’s “Logging for…” chip, the log sheet’s title. CUL-599 put that same face in the tab bar, where it navigates to the profile instead — and the profile has no switcher at all. On History and Foods it is now the only pet control on screen, and it answers a question nobody asked.

Filed from the pm-feature-review pass on CUL-599 · sharpened by the Designer’s CUL-600 comment · related: CUL-678, CUL-680

The constraint · read this before ruling

The switcher sheet is also the only door to “Add a pet”

Verified in the code, not assumed: router.push('/add-pet') and router.push('/archived-pets') are each called from exactly one file — PetSwitcherSheet.tsx. There is no entry in Settings, none on the Pet tab, none anywhere else. That single fact is what ranks the options, and it is what makes option (d) conditional rather than free.

Two consequences fall out of the table. For a one-pet household the only route to a second pet is a silent, chevron-less tap on the Home header — an affordance that deliberately shows no cue (multi-pet spec §3.1: single-pet households see no multi-pet chrome). And CUL-678 cannot be resolved by deletion: pulling the management rows out of the capture sheet needs somewhere for them to go first.

React · three rulings, ~4 lines each

What we need from you

R1Should the Pet tab be able to switch pets?

Deciding: whether the tab-bar avatar’s destination fulfils the promise its face makes, or whether Home stays the only switcher.

Options: (a) put the switcher on the Pet tab — team rec: it is the only option that closes the gap on History/Foods and gives “Add a pet” a second door, which unblocks CUL-678 and (d) · (b) long-press the tab — fastest once known, but invisible at rest, which is the actual complaint · (c) accept it — free, and honest that Home is one tab away.

Consequence: (a) is one screen, no schema, no new component, and turns two other open issues from forks into moves. (c) closes this issue as a known limit and leaves the single silent “Add a pet” door standing.

R2If (a) — which shape?

Deciding: how the switcher appears on a screen that today has no header row.

Options: a2 — the name gains the chevron — team rec: no new row, no third avatar, and it reuses the chevron’s existing meaning · a1 — Home’s header row, re-used — most consistent, but puts the pet’s face on screen three times · a3 — an explicit “Switch to Luna” row — shortest switch at two pets, but a second idiom that degrades past two and reads as settings.

Consequence: picks what the build PR renders. a2 is the smallest diff and the only one that adds nothing new for a one-pet household.

R3Is (d) in scope now, or a follow-on?

Deciding: whether the single-pet Home header stops opening the switcher in the same change.

Options: a follow-on issue, ruled after (a) ships — team rec: (d) is only safe once the Pet tab carries “Add a pet”, and shipping both at once means one PR changes the switcher’s reach in two directions before either is on a device · same PR — one coherent story, one device pass · drop it — accept the silent single-pet tap.

Consequence: a follow-on keeps the App-Store-window diff small and testable; same-PR risks a one-pet account with no route to a second pet if the ordering is got wrong.
