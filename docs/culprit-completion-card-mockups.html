Why it should still feel good the fiftieth time

The card shows about seven times a day, so the motion has to delight once and then get out of the way.

  • Cover the words and it still reads. A mark gets written onto the card: something was just recorded. Gold says it was a good moment; no gold says it was noted. From the + button, the food you tapped visibly becomes the record.
  • It is short and identical every time. At rest by 0.3s on a symptom and 0.4s on a meal from the log screen. The sentence is readable by about 0.2s, and the controls never wait.
  • A touch finishes it. Any touch on the card jumps straight to the end. It never replays and never shows gold on the way to a refusal.
  • It speaks the app's existing motion. The house spring, the fold's timings and the FAB's flight, imported rather than retyped.
No bounce on the recordThe FAB's bounce answers a finger on a button. The record's mark settles.

The dim, in plain words

When you log a symptom or a weight, the card appears and the screen behind it darkens. The darkening is only paint: it doesn't catch taps. A tap on the dark area goes straight through to whatever is underneath, usually Home.

Most of what's under there is harmless. The + button opens the menu and closes the card; a tab switches screens. One thing isn't: the look chips at the top of Home (Herself, Quiet, Off) save a look on a single tap. So a stray tap on the dark area while the card is up can save a look you didn't mean to.

  1. Leave it as it is. The stray tap is possible; nobody has reported it.
  2. Dimmed means inactive (recommended). While the dim is up, the look chips under it ignore taps. Everything else keeps working exactly as today.
  3. A tap on the dim closes the card, like a sheet. Round 3 recommended this; withdrawn. The dark area also covers the + button and the tabs, so tapping + would only close the card and you'd have to tap again, and closing early ends the Undo window.
