What is black today

An inventory of every large dark ground in the shipped app. The PM's read holds.

The completion cards

Near black (#0A0A0A), full width, about a quarter of the screen, roughly 7 times a day. The symptom card also dims Home by 35%, which makes it the darkest routine moment in the app. No ruling ever chose this ground: it came in as a Gmail-style "Undo send" snackbar and was carried forward.

The snackbar

Same ground, same slot, and its own file says it is meant to read as one family with the meal card. It shows rarely (a restored food, a saved password). It goes with the cards.

The day screen

Not black: brand night indigo (#13112E), chosen on purpose for the 9pm recap you open. It is a ruled register and it stays. The other dark grounds (splash, camera, photo viewer, a one-time paywall) are intentional and rare.

Decisions

1 · Move the card to daylight

RulePolish spec §5 R1 (2026-08-22): "a warm dark bottom card over a dimmed Home (never a white flash)". It protected the 2am owner from the retired full-screen white takeover.

BetterA white card in the day system. The protection still holds: the card is a bounded inset, Home is already near white, and the symptom card keeps its dim.

RecommendApprove, with §5 R1 reworded to "a daylight bottom card over a dimmed Home (never a full-screen white takeover)". One app-only PR, shipped over the air. CUL-1697's exception must then stand out by rail, hold and no mark, not by ground.

2 · The mark on white

DecidingWhich teal the confirm disc becomes once the bright teal fails on white.

OptionsDeeper teal with a white gap before the halo (recommended): the brightest teal that clears 3:1, and the gap keeps it clear in the celebrate state. Or the teal ink disc: strongest, but the ink you called drab on the FAB.

ConsequenceCloses the cards' part of the CUL-1666 glyph sweep.

3 · A tap on the symptom card's dim

DecidingWhat happens when the owner taps the dimmed area around the symptom card.

OptionsIt closes the card (recommended): a white card over a dim reads as a sheet, and sheets close that way. Today the tap passes through to Home, where a look chip writes on one tap. Or keep the pass-through. Or drop the dim.

ConsequenceUnder the dim, Home is one tap away rather than live. The meal and dose cards have no dim and are unchanged.
