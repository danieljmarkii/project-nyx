The rules every part keeps

  1. Only a tap that changes the tab plays. The trigger is the bar's own onPress when the tab is not focused, never a change in state.index. A deep link, a widget link, the recovery redirect or a pet switch moves the dot and the ink with no motion.
  2. A second tap on the tab you are on plays nothing. The press still fires tabPress for the screen's scroll to top.
  3. A tap mid-gesture springs the dot from wherever it is to the new tab, never back to a tab centre first. The tab you left jumps to its end state; the new tab's gesture starts.
  4. The app leaves mid-gesture: everything jumps to its end state (the fold's blur rule). Nobody comes back to a half-drawn ring.
  5. A layout change (fold, unfold, rotation) places the dot under the focused tab without animating it.
  6. Silent. The tab bar never imports lib/haptics.ts.
  7. Reduce Motion (hooks/useReducedMotion): every part takes its end state at once.
  8. Never a loop. Each part plays once and stops. The tick stays the app's only loop.
