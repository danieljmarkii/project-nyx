# Event-Taxonomy Hard Review — 2026-08-26 (post-ratification pass on spec v1.2, CUL-509)

**What this is.** The PM asked for a hard, hole-finding review of the ratified track before build ("I'd much rather us find holes in this spec/implementation plan now than find holes later"). The same-day design review (`docs/event-taxonomy-design-review-2026-08.md`) was the *direction-level* pass and returned unanimous-GO plus the 13 CE closures now in §9a — so this pass deliberately attacked what that one did not: **the §9a closures themselves, the W1 PR chain (§13a), the spec's factual claims about the codebase, and the evidence base W2's thresholds stand on.** Three isolated reviewers ran (a second `adversarial-reviewer` pass on §9a + W1 + the engine handshake; a spec-vs-code audit; an external fact-check of the W2 clinical anchors against primary literature), plus the in-context lens convening and a live-record re-query. Every code-grounded claim below was verified at file:line; the three highest-consequence ones were independently re-verified in the main session.

**Verdict.** The spine holds and the direction is unchallenged — **no finding reverses D1–D12 or the §9a intent, and the W2 clinical anchors survive a primary-literature audit essentially intact** (rare, and worth saying). But the pass found **30 findings**, including: **five that make W1-PR-3 unshippable as scoped** (the engine's per-lane membership mechanism does not exist; the client symptom mirrors are missing from the chain and ship at App Store cadence; `generate-report` shares the detection module and would print a safety flag about a sign its own table never counts), **two §9a closures that fail as written** (rule 2's chosen mechanism goes quietest on the sickest cat; rule 13's suppression has no evaluation point), and **two mandatory rule edits from the fact-check** (rule 11 must be sleep-anchored; rule 9's decline-for-dogs arm is clinically unsupported). Everything is a rule, a sentence, or a named build step — nothing is a redesign.

**Severity legend:** `W1-GATE` — must be closed in/before the W1 PR chain · `SPEC-EDIT-NOW` — a v1.3 text change, cheap now and structural later · `W2-GATE` — binds the W2 build/calibration · `W3-GATE` — binds W3 · `MINOR` — hygiene. ## §2 Findings

### A. The W1 plan (the chain as specced would not ship clean)

**HR-1 · W1-GATE · The per-lane membership mechanism does not exist — one list drives the fetch and every lane.** *(adv2 + code, independently)*
`CORRELATION_SYMPTOM_TYPES` (`detection.ts:104–111`) scopes the DB fetch (`index.ts:796`) **and** is iterated by all five per-type lanes (① `:2898` · ③/④ `:3501` · ⑦ `:3891` · ⑤ `:5052` · ⑥ `:5177`). Adding `cough` so ⑦ can see it auto-enrolls it in ① — which can emit a `food_symptom_correlation` card naming a protein as associated with coughing, the claim §9 forbids by name — and in ⑤, whose shipped template would render "…9 could be timed against a recent feeding" about a respiratory sign (the regurgitation-vs-cough confusion the never-hairball rule exists to prevent). The ① window fallback silently hands an unlisted type 12h (`:2899–2900`). Chronicity config is also global — no per-type floor slot (`minEpisodes: 6` for everything, `:2151–2158`), so "the owned cough floor calibration" has nowhere to land.
**Closure:** W1-PR-3 first lands a per-lane membership map (fetch = the union) + per-type chronicity config, behavior-neutral for the existing five types, **with a negative fixture per lane that must NOT fire for cough**; only then does cough join ⑦'s row. §9's table gains a cell per lane; the checklist says "decide every lane, including the ones that must not fire."

**HR-2 · W1-GATE · The client symptom mirrors are missing from the chain, and the release-order asymmetry makes it an owner-visible defect on the safety rail.** *(adv2 + code)*
The Signal card face is server-phrased, but the client re-derives copy from `SignalSymptomType` — a closed five-key union (`lib/signal.ts:47`) mirrored in `lib/signalCopy.ts:68` — feeding the cross-pet safety banner, the expanded evidence, and what-to-tell-the-vet. A cough chronicity finding renders **"Nyx has had recurring undefined since July"** (`signalCopy.ts:1897–1898`). The G10 unknown-*type* pin cannot see this (the type is known; the symptom is new), the cache is an unvalidated cast (`lib/signal.ts:563`), and the Signal surface is not gated by `event_types_v2` at all. The asymmetry: the Edge Function reaches 100% of devices in seconds; the client mirror ships at App Store cadence — and the next cut must be A-Native.
**Closure:** split PR-3 into **3a (client mirrors first: `SignalSymptomType`, both `SYMPTOM_LABEL` maps, `SYMPTOM_NOUN`, `WIDGET_SYMPTOM_LABELS`)** and **3b (engine + redeploy), gated on "a build carrying 3a is the minimum live build"** — the client-first precedent already in `deploy-manifest.json` (delete-account).

**HR-3 · W1-GATE · `generate-report` shares the detection module: the lane change is unavoidable report work, on a held function.** *(adv2)*
`report.ts:56` imports `CORRELATION_SYMPTOM_TYPES`; `:2365` filters the report's detection input on it; `:2487–2491` runs `detectSignals` and extracts `chronicity`; `render.ts:1387` renders the chronicity safety flag and `:1548` adds "Ongoing pattern — see the safety flags above." to the headline. `REPORT_SYMPTOM_TYPES` is a separate list that will not gain cough — so whenever the held redeploy (CUL-19) clears, the report prints an ongoing-pattern safety flag about a sign its own §3.5 frequency table never counts. That defeats §10.1's "no wrong data," and it is the B-494 class (a zone that advertises, then goes incoherent). Mechanically sooner: the deploy manifest fingerprints `generate-report`'s whole import closure including `detection.ts`, so W1-PR-3 must re-acknowledge a **held** function's fingerprint — §13a doesn't mention it.
**Closure:** W1-PR-3 adds cough to `REPORT_SYMPTOM_TYPES` in the same PR (report already fetches all types) **or** excludes cough from the report's `detectSignals` input; either way the PR carries the ledger re-ack, and §10 gains the rule: **a lane-membership change is report work.**

**HR-4 · SPEC-EDIT-NOW · FL-1 ("flag-off byte-identical") is impossible as stated, and as stated it drives the implementer into the bug §12 forbids.** *(adv2 + code)*
The flag-off flat grid derives its tiles from `EVENT_TYPES` itself (`EventTypePicker.tsx:197–203`, pinned to "exactly eight tiles" + snapshot). Add `cough` unconditionally → the flag-off grid grows (feature leaks past the flag; pin fails). Gate the `EVENT_TYPES` entry instead → a swapped row on the owner's flag-off iPad renders "Event" over 14 cough rows while her beta phone says "Cough" — exactly the outcome §12's read-not-gated paragraph forbids.
**Closure:** restate FL-1: byte-identical **for the capture surfaces** (grid tile set, confirm); read surfaces are deliberately not byte-identical and the §8 audit is why. `EVENT_TYPES` is never flag-gated; the flag gates the grid's tile list (a `v2Only` field or a gated grid list — a real PR-2 design item). The snapshot pin moves to the picker surface. §13a PR-2's note is rewritten accordingly; the entry-record type also needs the checklist's fields (`hasPhoto`, `species`, family group — none exist today, and groups currently live in the picker component, not constants, contra §3).

**HR-5 · W1-GATE · The swap script's preconditions are one short of safe, and its "no `updated_at` semantics" line points a builder at the exact mistake that would orphan devices.** *(panel + adv2)*
(a) **Step 0 is missing:** a server-side `UPDATE` reaches every device at the next hydration, but the §8-audit build reaches devices at release cadence — §11 must require "every device on the account carries the audited build (PR-2), verified not assumed" (same class as HR-2). (b) **The `updated_at` bump is load-bearing:** hydration pulls are watermark-incremental on `updated_at` (`lib/hydration.ts:105–114`) and `trg_events_updated_at` bumps on every UPDATE (`001_schema.sql:278`) — that bump is *how the re-key reaches other devices*. The spec's "no `updated_at` semantics beyond the write itself" reads as license to suppress the trigger, which would leave every other device holding `other` rows forever. Reword: the trigger bump is required and is the propagation mechanism; sync quiescence remains the push-side guard. (c) **Step 4's predicted ⑦ firing is a clock, not a fact:** the window start, the 14-day recency floor, and the 42-day firm boundary are all evaluated at run time — if W1 lands months from now and the cat recovered, verification "fails" on a *recovering* cat, and 14 notes ≠ 14 episodes after the 3h collapse. Step 4 computes all four floors on swap day and records "does not fire, for recency" as an acceptable, predicted outcome — not a defect to debug.

### B. The §9a escalation contract — closures that need re-closing (bind W2; edit the spec now)

**HR-11 · Rule 2's chosen mechanism fails on physiology: the collapse CHAINS, so the sicker the cat, the fewer trips it counts.** *(adv2; the highest-severity finding of this pass)*
The module rule 2 re-parameterises is chaining by documented design — "a continuous drip of events each ≤ gap apart stays ONE episode however long it runs" (`lib/symptomEpisodes.ts:61–66`, `lib/mealTiming.ts:229–233`). An obstructed male cat straining every ~8 minutes for two hours collapses to **one trip** at any gap ≥ 8 min; a less-sick cat every 25 minutes counts 5 and fires. The predicate is anti-correlated with severity — CE-1's defect at a new constant. And rule 2's one constant carries two conflicting jobs: the dedup window must *exceed* caregiver latency (two phones, one trip) while the trip window must be *shorter than* the sickest cat's inter-trip interval — the ranges overlap.
**Closure (rewrite rule 2):** split the jobs. Dedup = **non-chaining fixed-window row de-dup** (the shape `generate-report` already uses — `DEDUP_OBSERVATION_TYPES`, `report.ts:249–258`); trip count = qualifying rows after dedup, **no chaining collapse**. State the chaining property as the reason the generic module is not the mechanism here. (The one-collapse guard doesn't object: it requires a gap assignment *and* a chaining cursor, so a plain row count trips nothing — verified.)

**HR-12 · Rules 3/4/5 define three predicates over one window and never compose.** *(adv2)*
The mixed-evidence cluster (two "No", one "Yes", one unanswered) satisfies neither the band nor the ask — silence on a cat that strained four times. The n=1 lower-register line can fire *after* the band once the window rolls (non-monotone register: three interruptions ending on the calmest). And nothing retracts: chips answered "Yes" after the ask still leave a durable straining-cluster flag the report would print.
**Closure:** one predicate, three tiers, one evaluated window — an arbitration table over (marked-No, marked-Yes, unresolved) → {silence | n=1 line | ask | band}; a marked "Yes" resets the window; the register is monotone within a cluster; the **durable** Signal/report flag is recomputed from current chip state (a late "Yes" clears it) while the capture-time band is never un-said.

**HR-13 · Rule 13's mechanism cannot pin the rule: the guard is structurally blind, and the suppression predicate has no evaluation point.** *(adv2)*
The haptic at band time is the *beat's* `commitSymptom()` (`SheetLogBeat.tsx:87–88`) — a non-safety sibling the marker-scan guard never reads; adding the band's marker asserts the band doesn't import haptics, which it never would. And the beat mounts synchronously in `handleLogged` while the band decision is an async post-save SQLite read — "will a band render" is unanswerable at haptic time.
**Closure:** the band decision becomes an **input to the stage transition** — `handleLogged` awaits detection and selects a `band` stage, so the beat (and its haptic) is never mounted on a band path. Pin with a component test on `EventTypeSheet` ("a band-qualifying save plays no haptic"), not the import guard; keep the marker addition but say it guards a different hole.

**HR-14 · Rule 1 stops the timer but not the two dismissals the host already has.** *(adv2)*
A scrim tap or Android back falls straight through to `onClose()` on every non-confirm stage (`EventTypeSheet.tsx:104–114, 160–162`) — an emergency band dismissed silently by a touch above the sheet, and rule 3's once-per-cluster suppression means it never comes back.
**Closure:** on the band stage the scrim is inert and `onRequestClose` is a no-op; the only exit is the labelled control (the confirm stage's guarded treatment, not the done stage's pass-through).

**HR-15 · Rule 3's "fails toward firing across restarts" re-creates CE-8 for the leaf's modal user.** *(adv2)*
Foreground re-evaluation (rule 6) + fail-open suppression = the band re-fires on every app launch for the chronic-FIC cat whose cluster stays satisfied all day — alarm wallpaper via the closure that was written to prevent it. Where the state lives is also unspecified (device-local ⇒ once per device per household — fine, but say it; persisted ⇒ it's account state and belongs on the wipe path).
**Closure:** suppression is a **derived** fact — a cluster is identified by its first qualifying row id; the band fires iff no band-shown marker exists for that cluster id; the marker is persisted per pet, per device by design, joins `wipeLocalSession`. "Fails toward firing" applies only to an unidentifiable cluster, never to a restart.

**HR-16 · Rules 1+6 imply two hosts and only one is specified.** *(adv2)*
On the second caregiver's phone the cluster completes at *hydration* — no sheet is open; D4's "capture-time band" has no capture moment.
**Closure:** rule 6 names the presentation per entry point. Recommended: post-save renders the band; hydration/foreground route to the Signal safety card (with the B-661 Part 2 push as the later additional channel), and the consequence — device-A silence at completion-by-sync — is recorded, not discovered.

**HR-17 · Rule 11 must be sleep-anchored — the fact-check's one over-fire risk.** *(facts)*
All four primary studies support <30/min **sleeping** in both species; but 6–14% of verified non-decompensating animals cross 30 *awake at rest* (Ohad 12/190; Porciello 7/51 dogs + 1/22 cats, ranges to 44–45), and feline awake bands run to 35 in practice summaries. A >30 count the owner couldn't confirm as sleep must route to **"count again while she's asleep"** — never into the sustained-finding state. Copy exclusions the studies require: adults only; never while panting (dogs); not within ~30 min of play; not during dream-sleep twitching; purring (cats, practical). Attribution: the numbers cite the Rishniw-group studies and practice guidance, **never ACVIM** — the 2019 consensus is deliberately numberless (baseline-relative), which is precisely the authority *behind* rule 11's "if your vet has given you a number for {pet}, use theirs" line. Keep that line verbatim.

**HR-18 · Rule 9's "explicitly decline for dogs" arm is clinically unsupported — delete it.** *(facts)*
Unproductive urinary straining in a dog is the same emergency pattern (obstructive urolithiasis, male-skewed, fatal in 48–72h via hyperkalemia — ACVS/PetMD), not UTI-grade. Rule 9 collapses to one arm: **fire, with dog wording and a dog floor** ("in the yard," not "litter tray"); straining *with* urine passing is the prompt-24–48h tier, which the unproductive predicate already separates. (The female lead sentence and male-intensifier shape are confirmed verbatim by the 2025 iCatCare/ISFM consensus — "occurs almost exclusively in male cats" belongs in the evidence pack so the sex branches cite something.)

**HR-19 · The RRR trend claim has no rule: a rising under-30 series renders wordlessly.** *(adv2)*
18→22→26→28 over four nights — rule 11's two states both key on an over-reference count; nothing fires; a chart with no words reads as "nothing to say," which is reassurance by layout on a record trending toward the threshold. §9 promises "trend beats single readings" and §14 frame 10 exists precisely for this series.
**Closure:** an escalate-only monotone-rise rule (the shipped `detectGapShortening` shape — fires on a strictly-shortening run, silent otherwise) — or drop the trend claim and cut frame 10. The series surface never ships without a rule attached.

**HR-20 · Rule 11's state machine must be derived, not stored.** *(adv2)*
A stored "already asked" boolean lets two high counts 170 days apart (with 40 normal nights between) state a sustained finding, and renders differently after reinstall or on the second caregiver's phone. Derive on each evaluation: sustained = |{over-reference counts within `maxAge`, separated ≥ `minSeparation`}| ≥ 2; ask at exactly 1. Restart, reinstall, and device 2 then agree by construction.

**HR-21 · Open-mouth copy nuance.** *(facts)* The n=1 escalation stands (textbook; safe direction), but the band copy should anchor "at rest" — a brief post-play pant in an otherwise normal cat resolves in a minute or two, and the copy shouldn't imply the pant itself is the emergency. Dr. Chen's call at W2 copy time.
