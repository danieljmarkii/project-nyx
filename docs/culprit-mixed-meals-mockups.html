§6 · Held back on purpose

What we won't animate

No celebration at all-rabbitNothing in the app ends a switch, so nothing celebrates one. No confetti, no bloom.
No gold on doorsGold marks a saved record. The MIX pill and the mix line stay plain.
No direction hintsBoth stepper sides look, move and buzz the same. No long-press repeat.
No rolling numbersThe split changes whole. A roll would show splits nobody fed.
No motion in History on scrollRows draw in once with the day. A run breaking is shown, never animated.
No spinner for a saveA mix saves on the phone first. The tick is only for waiting on the network.
