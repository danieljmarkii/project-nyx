Mixed meals: one bowl, two foods, a ratio

Every frame uses one story: Pixel, a cat, moving from Chicken Pâté to Rabbit Pâté. On day one the owner mixes ¼ can of chicken into ½ can of rabbit. The record should show that as one bowl, about ⅓ chicken and ⅔ rabbit, and keep showing it truthfully as the mix changes. The app never says what the next ratio should be.

§4 · P-4 · the open conflict

Old food in the bowl just after Day 1

In plain words. Pixel's trial says Day 1 is Oct 1. On Oct 3 the owner logs a bowl that still has some chicken in it, because the switch isn't actually finished. Either the app (a) counts it as a slip and shows the usual heads-up, or (b) also says, once, that if the switch is still going, Day 1 can move to when it ends. Both count the bowl. Neither hides it.

§6 · P-6 · the vet report

What the vet reads about the switch

Nothing here is new data entry. The report reads the bowls you already logged and says what they show: when the trial food first appeared, how many days it was mixed, and the first day the bowls were trial food only. Day 1 stays what you entered. Highlighted text is new.
