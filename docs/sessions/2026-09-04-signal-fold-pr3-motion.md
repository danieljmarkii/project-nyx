## What shipped

The owner-caused fold / unfold choreography on the Signal card — `docs/nyx-signal-fold-requirements.md` §12 v1.2 (DF-10, the PM's "design delight … feel free to even go a bit more aggressive"), built to the beats verbatim: **fold** = the body fades and drifts 8pt up over 180ms (ease-out quad), then the box eases shut over 300ms with the strip arriving as one node and every sibling below rising in the same native transaction, **the rail shortening last** (220ms, 80ms behind the box) to the strip's 16pt tick; **unfold** = **the rail grows first** (160ms, ease-out cubic), the box follows 80ms later with one felt settle (iOS `LayoutAnimation` spring at damping 0.7 — ~4pt overshoot, never a second bounce; Android ease-in-out, its spring is coarse), the face fading in over 370ms and landing −8 → 0 over 300ms a beat after the box. Identical on every class. No haptic, no loop, no wash, no bounce on close, no scale of the card, no sideways travel. Reduced motion = a `durationFast` crossfade of the incoming content and nothing else. An automatic re-open never enters the machine. Nothing folded ⇒ the shipped tree to the byte (the PR 1 snapshot is unchanged).

- **`components/home/foldMotion.ts`** — `useFoldMotion`, the state machine (`idle` · `leaving` · `closing` · `opening` · `crossfade`), the `FOLD_MOTION` beat table, and the two `LayoutAnimationConfig` objects (`FOLD_LAYOUT`: 300ms easeInEaseOut; `UNFOLD_LAYOUT`: the platform-split spring/ease). Every phase change writes `phaseRef` eagerly (a mocked native driver completes in the same tick); native-driver completions pin their end value (`setValue`) so a later commit never re-applies a seed; a blur **finishes** the transition (values to their end state, the owed host commit issued un-animated — the arrival moment's rule); a re-keyed row (a pet switch under a transition) **abandons** it without committing anything; presses mid-transition are no-ops; timers are cleared on idle and on unmount.
- **`components/home/InsightCard.tsx`** — the card renders **both states**: `folded` + `onUnfold` are new props and the strip renders inside the same row beside the **same rail node**; `FoldedStrip` is now the strip's inner pressable (name · count · chevron; it carries PR 1's `paddingVertical: space1` and the 44pt floor itself, the folded row carries no padding, so the folded row's geometry is PR 1's to the point). In flight: the rail is an `Animated.View` (the same node through the animated commit), steps out of the row's flow (absolute, explicit height, `transformOrigin: 'top'`, `scaleY` + `translateY`) once it holds a height, the content column takes the rail's width + the row gap as margin so nothing moves sideways, and the row clips (`overflow: hidden`, the mock's `.mrow`) so the trailing rail never paints over the card below. The face and strip stages (`Animated.View`, opacity + drift) mount only while their content is arriving or leaving. `expanded` closes with the fold (a re-open lands on the face, §3.2). The strip's resting rail is the mock's **16pt centred tick** (`railStrip`).
- **`components/home/SignalZone.tsx`** — `LiveStack` no longer swaps `InsightCard` ⇄ `FoldedStrip`; it passes `folded` and `onUnfold`.
- **Tests** — `InsightCard.motion.test.tsx` (19): the beats in order with every value at its end state; the two configs fire on the two press paths only — never on a `cached` change, the reopened path, a stored fold on first paint, or under reduced motion; the idle tree adds nothing (no stage, plain rail, no layout handler, no `overflow`); the degrade paths (no face measurement ⇒ the rail rides the box; a stored fold with no strip measurement ⇒ the box opens at once); blur settles in both directions; a re-keyed row abandons; mid-transition presses are no-ops; unmount leaves no timer; the rail keeps colour / 0.85 / 3pt at every phase. PR 1's suites updated where the anatomy moved (the host commit now rides the leave beat; the rail is the row's, not the strip's; the folded row is an `insight-row` holding the strip).

## Decisions made in the build (Designer's call under DF-10; flagged on CUL-788)

1. **One row, one rail.** PR 1 had the host swap two components, which unmounts and re-creates the rail on every fold — it cannot be "the same node before, during and after" (§12's principle). Unifying the states inside `InsightCard` is what makes the continuity real rather than approximate. Cost: `FoldedStrip` loses its own row and rail; three PR 1 assertions were re-pointed.
2. **`LayoutAnimation` for geometry only; `Animated` (native driver) for every fade and drift — the spec's `create: { opacity }` configs are not used.** On Fabric a layout keyframe re-applies a view's *committed* props when it ends, so a view that is both layout-animated and carrying an in-flight native-driver transform snaps back to the transform it was committed with. The rail therefore leaves the row's flow (absolute + explicit height) for exactly the commits that animate layout — no keyframe ever touches it — and the strip / face fades are `Animated` wrappers, which also makes the reduced-motion crossfade the same mechanism with the drift and the rail switched off. Same beats, durations and easings as §12; a different engine split. **Proposed §12.4 edit** (Tier 2): replace the `create: { easeInEaseOut, opacity }` lines with "geometry only; the fades are `Animated`", and note the rail's absolute step-out.
3. **The strip's resting rail is a 16pt centred tick,** per §12.1 / §12.4 ("the strip's 16pt") and the mock's `.foldrow .rail` — not PR 1's full-row stretch, which followed §3.1's v1.0 table row. **Proposed §3.1 edit** (Tier 2): Rail → "3pt, `RAIL_COLOR[priorityClass]`, opacity 0.85, a **16pt tick centred on the strip** (the fold's end state — §12); never dims on fold." Dr. Chen's condition (rail at full opacity) is untouched; FS-3 ("the strip keeps the rail") holds.
4. **The rail's height is rendered as `scaleY` about its top edge**, not an animated layout height — so it runs on the UI thread with the box's native transaction instead of a JS-thread layout pass per frame on a cold Home. §12.3 forbids "scale" for the *card* (shrinking into the distance reads as dismissal); a 3pt line shortening from its top is the line being drawn back, which is exactly the beat. The one artefact: at 16/180 the tick's 2pt end-caps are squashed for the ~30ms before the idle tick replaces it — sub-point at 3pt wide.
5. **Blur uses `useAppActive`** (§12.3 said "not imported — nothing to pause"). The beats are sequenced by a JS callback and two 80ms timers, and JS timers do not run in the background, so a fold cut by a blur would otherwise *resume* on return — the mid-collapse the finish-on-blur rule exists to prevent. The subscription is the shared one; nothing loops.

## Process

- The plan-gate was posted to the issue and the build proceeded without waiting: the session ran unattended, the issue is build-ready with the PM's DF-10 ruling deferring the details to the Designer, and the draft PR is the redirect point.
- Two jest facts were probed before the design was fixed, not assumed: `Animated.View` adds `collapsable={false}` to the host tree (so the idle rail must stay a plain `View`, swapped only while in flight), and a mocked native-driver animation completes on the next frame whatever its duration, with the JS value untouched (so the tests read the live `Animated.Value`s off the composite and the beats advance by one frame + the real 80ms lags).

## Residuals

- **On-device is the real test of this PR.** The Fabric keyframe/native-driver interaction the engine split is designed around cannot be exercised in jest; the QA script on the PR walks fold, unfold, reduced motion, blur mid-fold, and a fold on the lead (Newsreader) canvas on the PM's own record.
- The spring is `LayoutAnimation`'s, so its overshoot is the platform's ~4pt at damping 0.7, not a tuned curve; if it reads as more than "one felt settle" on device, the one knob is `springDamping` (0.7 → 0.8) in `foldMotion.ts`.
- Tier-2 edits 2 and 3 above await the Designer / PM (on the issue).
- PR 2 (CUL-785) is in: the motion runs on the safety strips unchanged; nothing here is class-keyed. On-device, the fold on the chronicity lead (the Newsreader canvas) is now testable on the PM's own record.
