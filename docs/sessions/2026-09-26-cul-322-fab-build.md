**PM prompt:** "Session 2: Build CUL-322 (D3 C, D5 a): the indigo FAB with the teal plus, the fan choreography, and the three accessibility fixes. Read the issue and the mock §05–§06 first, then post a plan before coding." The plan went up in chat; the PM said "go ahead".

## What shipped

- **Colour (D3 = C).** The disc is `colorBrandNightElevated` and the plus `colorAccent`: 14.25:1 disc on `colorNeutralLight`, 14.87:1 over a white card, 6.57:1 plus on the disc, and the disc still clears 3:1 over its own open scrim. `constants/theme.contrast.test.ts` swaps the CUL-1063 ink rows for these, and keeps three failing rows (the bright teal as the disc on both grounds; a white plus on the bright teal) so a later tidy is a red build. The Tier-2 amendment to `docs/culprit-in-app-brand-requirements.md` §1 rule 3 (v1.2) names the FAB as the rule's one exception; the theme's night-token comment says the same.
- **Motion (D5 = a, beats 1–4 and 6–8).** `components/log/FAB.tsx`, RN `Animated` on the native driver only. The disc is now a `Pressable` and scales to 0.9 on touch-down; the plus springs 135° with an overshoot (tension 90 / friction 7); an indigo scrim (`colorScrimNight`, the brand night at 34%) fades up over the whole tabs area; the panel became a fan of pills, one `Animated.Value` per slot, staggered 38ms nearest first, recent foods lowest and newest nearest the thumb. Close runs in reverse, farthest first, in about 180ms, and a tap mid-close re-opens rather than double-closing. The completion beat is unchanged (quick meals still route through `showMeal`; the symptom pills open the log sheet).
- **Reduce Motion.** One crossfade: no scale, no turn, no fan. The plus and a still × crossfade on the menu's fade, so the open state still reads as close. `useReducedMotion` decides the render; `reducedMotionNow()` decides the handlers, and each handler writes the end state either way, so a setting flipped mid-open still renders a correct frame. An unknown setting reads as still (C-43).
- **BRK-37.** The disc has `accessibilityRole="button"` and `accessibilityState.expanded`. The open layer (scrim, fan and disc together, so the way out is inside it) is `accessibilityViewIsModal` with `onAccessibilityEscape`, and Android back closes it. The tabs layout wraps the banner and the tabs in the new `HiddenUnderFabMenu` host, which reads `uiStore.fabMenuOpen` and hides its descendants (C-14).
- **Defensive close.** The CUL-871 stand-down un-renders the FAB but keeps the instance mounted, so a capture overlay opening under an open menu would have left Home hidden from assistive tech with no disc to close it. Unreachable by today's callers; the menu now closes on it anyway (the code-reviewer's observation).

## Calls made in the plan

- No blur: `expo-blur` is not a dependency, and the issue said a scrim alone without a PM call. Home's settle to 97% (mock beat 3) is not built: the FAB is a sibling of the tabs and cannot reach Home's view, and the issue's scope did not name it.
- The "Recent foods" header and the "No foods logged yet" line left with the panel: a header has no ground between floating pills, and text on the translucent scrim fails contrast. `Log food` holds the thumb's slot for a pet with no foods. CUL-723's second test was rewritten to what it can still discriminate (the in-flight frame says nothing about the incoming pet's foods).
- The mock's "usual 6 AM" meta is filed as **CUL-1280**, since it needs a read and a floor.
