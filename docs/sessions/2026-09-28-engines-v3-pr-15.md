# Engines v3 PR-15 — the synthetic corpus of pets with known truth

**Date:** 2026-09-28

Engines v3, Wave 2, Lane D (CUL-508). Shipped via #972. Server-side test data only: no migration, no Edge Function change, nothing an owner sees. Unblocks PR-16 (CUL-1131, EN-1) together with PR-11b and Lane C.

## What shipped

- **A trajectory simulator** (`supabase/functions/_shared/engineCorpus/trajectory/`). A scenario is truth (what the pet did), logging (what the owner wrote, the only layer an engine sees) and response (what the owner did after an evening's cards). `simulate(scenario, seed, observer)` walks the days; the observer is the one seam to an engine. Streams are named per day and per effect, and effects add episodes over an untouched background, so two engines over one seed are a paired comparison.
- **38 hand-authored scenarios** (null, injected, weight, owner response), each with its rationale and a machine-readable answer key (`falseCards`, `detect`, and a `paired` / `both_acknowledged` scoring rule).
- **Committed as code and seeds (D-1),** pinned per scenario by a SHA-256 digest; the owner answer is a corpus-local dated fact per sign (D-2).
- **Tests:** 49 Deno tests. Every truth claim is measured from generated rows within about ±10%. There are checks on what the observer can resolve each evening, the response layer, common random numbers and the answer key's consistency, plus the floor over the PR-15 row's list. Separate tests cover DST and the shape of one evening fed to the shipped `detectSignals`.

## Decisions

- **D-1 / D-2** as recommended (PM, 2026-09-28).
- **Scoring rule:** an effect anchored to the owner's acknowledgement makes the truth depend on the engine that asked, so it is never an E-6 proof; `own-visit-doubling-fixed` is its paired sibling.
- **FCEAI:** chronic pets vomit ten a month (moderate is 2 to 3 a week, evidence pack §4).
- **The symptom-list guard:** the corpus's `SIGNS` carries a `symptom-list-ok` marker rather than a registration (C-32). The visit-reader guard needed nothing: the synthetic link column is spelled `visitId`.

## Review

- **adversarial-reviewer: FAIL on the first draft, all fixed.**
  - The acknowledgement-anchored truth differed by arm.
  - The food list was empty at every evening, which blinded the food lanes in both arms.
  - The bands passed effects 25% weaker than stated. Each such weakening is now red by mutation.
  - The FCEAI band was misquoted.
  - Cause labels overstated what was injected.
  - Night vomits were always witnessed.
  - The red flag had no truth record.
  - Some weight numbers were wrong.
- **code-reviewer: all fixed.** The visit guard, the DST conversion and its test, the wander test, a vacuous weight test, and weigh-in order on shared days.
- **The shape check** caught blood written as yes/no instead of 013's `vomit_blood` vocabulary.
