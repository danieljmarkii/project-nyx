## Teach

### The strategy kernel: the diagnosis was the wrong list (P1, L1)
A good plan has three parts, and the first is the one people skip: a diagnosis, meaning what is actually in the way. Then comes a guiding policy, the approach the diagnosis calls for, and then actions that follow from it. A wrong diagnosis makes every later step efficient at the wrong thing.

**Like:** a car that won't start. You can put in a new battery perfectly, but if the tank is empty the car still won't start. The work was fine. The diagnosis wasn't.
