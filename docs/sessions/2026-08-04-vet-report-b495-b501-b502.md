## What shipped

- **B-495 — the weight tile states % of body mass alongside the absolute.** `-0.3 kg` was
  species-blind: identical for a 32 kg Labrador and a 4.4 kg cat, where on the cat it is ~7% of body
  mass and, on a refusing animal, the single most action-forcing number on the page — yet no
  percentage appeared anywhere in the report. The At-a-glance `weightTile` now renders the percent,
  computed against the earliest in-window weigh-in (`seriesKg[0]`) by a new **shared
  `weightDeltaPct(deltaKg, firstKg)`** that the trial-scoped `weightDuringTrial` sentence now reads
  too — so a fall can never render one magnitude on the tile and another in the sentence (the diet-
  trial §5.3 one-predicate rule, applied to weight). The `weightDuringTrial` refactor is
  behaviour-preserving (the old inline 0.15 kg / whole-percent / sub-1% guards are exactly what the
  helper encodes). The guardrails are the **instrument's, not clinical** — a sub-0.15 kg (one
  home-scale tick) delta or a sub-1% result states no percent (the tile falls back to the
  absolute-only label), and the figure rounds to a whole percent. `-0.3 kg` now reads ≈7% on the cat
  and ≈1% on the Lab. Cold read: *"species-correct … no false precision, whole-number %, weigh-in
  count disclosed, home-scale caveat present."*

- **B-501 — already resolved by B-532 (#516); formally closed.** `signalmentBlock` already renders
  the active problem list beside the signalment (`Recorded conditions: Chronic vomiting (since Apr 10,
  2026) … owner-recorded history, not a finding in this window`, filtered to `status === 'active'`) —
  exactly the "cheapest fix" (a conditions line beside the signalment) B-501 asked for. B-532 built it
  with a dedicated regression test ("B-532 COLD⑦ — the active problem list reaches page 1"); its code
  comment even names the same Westie-atopic-dermatitis cold-read case B-501 describes. Confirmed
  against both artifacts; cold read: *"exactly where I expect the problem list."* No code change. The
  safety-band-weighting residual B-501 parked stays with B-479.

- **B-502 (partial — scope valve) — the empty vomit block collapses to a line.** With no incident
  photographed, `vomitCharacteristics` said "no photo" three ways (a photo-read lead describing a read
  that never happened, a grey "no legible read yet" bar, and the Blood & foreign block — plus the
  "Automated photo analysis" tag over a section that analysed nothing): ~100 words and a quarter page
  for one fact. It now collapses to a single note — the denominator (which already discloses the
  without-a-photo count, §5.10) plus the caveat. The **B-494 "not a clearance" caveat is preserved
  word for word** (absence of a photo is not absence of blood — it is the only thing keeping the
  section's silence from reading as a negative result). Refused-cat page 1: **1302 → 1223 words**; the
  vomit block ~115 → ~55.

## Scope valve — the B-502 caveat de-duplication was re-filed (B-686)

B-502's title is the caveat crowding; its two named remedies are (a) de-duplicate the repeated caveats
to one canonical each, and (b) let the empty blocks collapse to a line. **(b) shipped. (a) was
re-filed as B-686** because it is the "editorial pass that runs long" the task's scope valve
anticipated:

- The measured repetitions (`worth confirming against the bag` ×3, `intake not directly observed` ×4,
  the "a floor, not a total" tile+prose pair) are each **load-bearing *inline* from a prior cold-read
  fix**: B-600 put the antigen-tally caveat on its own row *"because the reader who lifts this number
  has already stopped reading prose"*; the exposure tile's "a floor" is B-600 pass-3 ⑤ (the tile is
  scanned without its prose); the vomit caveat is B-494. A mechanical "one canonical each" would
  orphan a claim's provenance where a reader may scan only that element.
- The real fix is a **Designer/Dr. Chen editorial pass** (which claims may share one provenance
  statement; whether to shorten the qualifier), which is exactly what the B-502 row already said it
  "wants". Rushing it into a two-fix PR risked regressing the prior cold-read fixes.
- Decisive evidence for treating it as polish: `vet-report-cold-read` graded **every remaining
  repetition a non-blocking nit that "displaces nothing / does not crowd out any clinical datum"** and
  returned CLINIC-READY on both artifacts.
