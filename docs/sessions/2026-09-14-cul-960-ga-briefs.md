## Brief 4 was not the question it was written as

The brief asked whether to accept, disclose, or wait, on the grounds that "the deployed report prints no cough/sneeze while ⑦'s Signal card says tell your vet". The PM's steer was that data integrity should be the priority, which is the instinct the framing invites — and the framing was wrong in three places, all verified before the ruling:

1. **Live `generate-report` is v14 (2026-07-30), not v13 (Jul 18).**
2. **Cough and sneeze are already in `REPORT_SYMPTOM_TYPES` on `main`** (`supabase/functions/generate-report/report.ts:280-289`). No code is missing. The record itself is intact — the rows write correctly, appear on every in-app read surface, and feed lane ⑦ on `generate-signal` v34, which is live and account-agnostic. This is **deploy currency**, not data integrity, and saying so precisely is what let the real problem surface.
3. **CUL-557 explicitly deploys `generate-report`** — its order is analyze-vomit → analyze-stool → generate-report, on App Store milestone M3, before the cut. The brief cited CUL-557 as grounds to *accept* the gap. It is the work that *closes* it.

That third correction is the one that mattered. If CUL-557 deploys `generate-report`, and `supabase/functions/deploy-manifest.json` holds `generate-report` at `status: "hold"` with the standing instruction that the hold "must not be read as progress toward clearing it" — then two live tracks are planning opposite things about the same function, and the store build's report depends on which is right. Queued behind that hold is genuinely unfinished safety work: **CUL-50** (Urgent, Backlog) and **CUL-60** (Urgent, Todo), the refusal lane's identity blind spots, plus **CUL-59** (High). Deploying per CUL-557 ships a half-finished refusal lane; holding per the ledger ships a report six weeks stale.

Filed as **CUL-965** (Urgent, `Waiting on PM`, App Store Launch). The ruling: resolve it first, **GA-2 does not wait on it, the 1.2.0 cut does.** Dr. Chen's dissent from the 2026-09-13 product read is satisfied by the deploy rather than by a disclosure line, so no report-copy PR and no extra cold read join this track.

The generalisable part: **a brief's recommendation is only as good as the premises in its own bullet list, and the bullet supporting "accept" was the one that disproved it.** The check that found it was reading CUL-557's description rather than trusting the brief's one-line characterisation of it.

### A correction I made mid-session

I first wrote on CUL-663 that check 25 (picker flag off → byte-identical) is "asserted by nothing automated". That is wrong: `components/log/EventTypePicker.test.tsx` pins it — *"renders the shipped flat grid unchanged"* and *"never renders a `v2Only` tile … at any flag state"*. What is genuinely absent is a **tree-equivalence guard of the C-36 / `vetVisitsFlagOff` shape** for this flag. Content pinned, byte-identity not. Corrected on the issue, because the wrong version would have had the PM treat a pinned check as unpinned.

The lesson is narrow but real: **"no guard file exists" and "nothing asserts this" are different claims**, and the first does not imply the second. `guards/` is one home for an invariant; a component suite is another.
