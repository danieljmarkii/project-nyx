## Appendix A — the product read, verbatim (`pm-feature-review`, the four round-4 changes, run on the page as drawn; every finding folded)

**Summary (quotable).** Round 4 does what the PM asked in all four places, and three of the four changes introduce a new seam that a walk-through finds before a device does. The selected state now reads unmistakably *chosen* and — the thing that mattered most — still does not move the next tap target; but the deeper wash appears to have cost the accent ink its contrast (my arithmetic puts `#0B7B6C` on `#CDF5EC` at ≈4.40:1, under the 4.5:1 the spec promises to pin, and the selected chip's gloss at 75% opacity at ≈2.90:1), so the "ramp it up 20 percent" ruling needs one more token pass, not a redesign. The way back moved to the top and the Done bar pinned to the bottom, which solves the PM's complaint at the bottom and re-creates it at the top: once Jordan has scrolled to the Toilet family, the only visible exit is a Done that *commits a look*, and the two renderings of "‹ Show fewer words" (its own row in the prototype, a fourth chip in the safety-critical first row in the still frame) disagree about where it even lives. ### Broken (product-visible)
- **(a) The accent ink appears to fail on the new wash.** `.chip.on` sets `color: var(--d-teal-ink)` (`#0B7B6C`) on `background: #CDF5EC` (`docs/culprit-daily-look-mockups.html:135`). By my arithmetic that is **≈4.40:1** — under 4.5:1 for 12 px / weight-500 text. The shipped `colorAccentLight` (`#E0FBF7`) gives ≈4.80:1, so the round-4 deepening is precisely what crossed the line. Spec §3.1a (`docs/nyx-daily-look-requirements.md:133`) promises "a new token beside `colorAccentLight`, tuned so the accent ink still clears 4.5:1 on it — pinned in `constants/theme.contrast.test.ts`"; the hex in the design authority does not clear it. Fix is one notch on either side (a lighter wash ≈`#D3F6EE`, or a darker ink), then the pin. Defer the exact value to the token pass — but the mock's hex is what an engineer will lift.
- **(a) The selected chip's gloss is worse, and round 4 made it worse.** `.chip.on small.gloss { color: var(--d-teal-ink); opacity: 0.75 }` (`:143`) composites to ≈`#3C9A8C` on the new wash — **≈2.90:1**. This is owner-facing text on the unfolded grid (*Off**, not getting up for the usual things***), and §4.1 rule 12 makes the gloss load-bearing ("the gloss is never dropped from the grid"). It was already failing (≈3.10:1) on the old wash; the promised contrast pin only covers the ink, not the ink-at-75%.
- **(b) The design authority contradicts itself on where the way back renders.** The still frame puts `‹ Show fewer words` **inside the first row** as a fourth chip (`:528`); the prototype renders it in **its own row** beneath the first row (`:1116`). The ledger and T-21 say it "stays where it was tapped", which is true of neither — the door that was tapped (`Something else ›`) sits at the *end of the head-word row*, and that row is gone once the grid opens. Two builders reading this build two different cards.

### PM decisions (only you can call these)
- **The link's verb.** `Add a note ›` (matches the record's column, History's row, the report's Appendix G) versus `Say more ›` (matches the placeholder, and is the register the rest of the card speaks in). Recommendation: **`Say more ›`** on the card, "note" as the object's name everywhere it is stored or printed — the link is an invitation, the column is a noun.
- **Does an older entry keep the note link on Home?** Options: (i) newest-entry-only, as built — cleanest card, silent loss of the affordance; (ii) every entry in the today list keeps it — one more teal affordance per row, and the card grows; (iii) newest-only plus one line on the record-screen route. Recommendation: **(i) with (iii)'s cue**, because the density cost of (ii) lands on the card that has to survive months.
- **Does a *saved* note render on non-newest entries in the today list, or only its newest?** The prototype renders all (`:1099`); T-22 is silent. This is the difference between a 6-line and a 9-line card on an engaged day.
- **Does the FAB hide while the grid is open and the Done bar is pinned?** Hiding it removes the collision and the ambiguity about which bottom-right control saves; keeping it preserves the "logging happens at the FAB" habit mid-look.
- **During an active diet trial, does the intake router pre-fill the most recent food or the trial's target food?** The wedge user's answer and the general user's answer differ, and the wrong default is invisible in the record afterwards.
