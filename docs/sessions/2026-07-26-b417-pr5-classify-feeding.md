# B-417 PR 5 — `lib/dietTrial.ts`, the one shared off-diet predicate

**Date:** 2026-07-26 · shipped via #459

## What this closed

The wedge feature had **three** answers to *"was this feeding off the trial diet"*, one of them shipped: `generate-report/report.ts:2246`'s `confounderFeedings` counts every treat and every human food, never consults the trial, lists the vet-**permitted** treat as a contaminant, and cannot see a different-brand kibble fed as a meal at all. §5.3 rules that `classifyFeeding` lives in `lib/dietTrial.ts` as *"one implementation, not one documented intention"*. That module now exists, `lib/trialContaminant.ts` is a consumer of it rather than a fourth definition (**B-438**), and PR 3's single-food stopgap is deleted (**B-453**).

Four rungs, closed-world, with the allowed set as the only permit path: the dated `diet_trial_foods` membership resolved on case-folded brand+product (§5.4, with the id checked first so an un-hydrated food row does not turn the trial diet into its own exposure) · full-**array** protein comparison against the union of every protein of every `primary_diet` food · `off_diet_unrecognised` with designed first-class copy, because it is the modal case on a real library · and C3's oral route. Plus D-A (the standing contamination fact, extended to permitted extras and compared against each food's **own** designated primary) and D-B (a permitted food keeps its verdict and still records its antigen).

Pure and dependency-free by hard constraint — only `./protein.ts` and `./utils.ts`, `.ts` extensions — so PR 7 can import it into `generate-report`. `lib/trialContaminant.ts` cannot be imported from Deno at all, and that is precisely how the third contradictory definition came to be written.

**The §5.1 metric is pinned, and only then the floor.** Coverage is distinct local days with ≥1 non-treat feeding over days elapsed, both halves on one overlap range and one clock (B-421's `localDayIndexOf`). Over that: `COVERAGE_SUPPORTS 0.8` / `COVERAGE_FLOOR 0.5` / `MIN_INTERPRETABLE_DAYS 7`, gating §7.2's interpretability sentence only. Filed as **P-3**, provisional pending Dr. Chen in the shape §0.4 uses for P-1/P-2.

The card also gets real numbers for the first time: `dietTrialFacts` supplies `exposures` and `belowCoverageFloor`, so states 3 and 4 are reachable.

## The `adversarial-reviewer` FAILED the first cut, with eight breaks, and its structural finding was the valuable part

Not one break was in the rung logic. **`computeTrialFacts` returns five disclosure channels — `unclassifiable`, `oralRoute`, `arrangementExposures`, `antigenTally`, `byRung` — and exactly one number, `offDiet`, reached a surface.** Everything the module got right about *"a floor, never a total"* was discarded one call later, and the unqualified sentence rendered anyway. The reviewer ran real scratch suites against the real modules rather than reasoning about them, which is what let it produce literal card output.

The four it would not ship:

1. **§5.2's own worked example survived end-to-end.** The spec says the composition with intake is *structural* — a live `IntakeDeclineFlag` **replaces** the adherence line — and the replacement genuinely is structural on the card. But the detector behind it, `detectIntakeDecline`, is a **relative**-decline detector. Fed the spec's own example (a cat refusing the new hydrolyzed diet twice a day for 14 days, every bowl logged and rated `refused`) it returns `{status: 'none'}`: trigger A needs recent days *below* a higher baseline and a diet refused from day 1 is uniformly low, not declining; trigger B needs a prior mean *for that food* and a never-eaten diet has none. Worse, the chronic case **decays into** the clean case — a pet that ate normally then refused from trial start fires for ~3 days and goes quiet, so the card *upgrades* from the safety state to the clean state exactly as the anorexia becomes chronic. Measured output: *"Meals logged on 14 of 14 days. 14 feedings in total — all 14 matched the trial diet or a permitted food."* over a cat seven times past the feline 48h hepatic-lipidosis window. **Fixed** by giving the trial the second, non-clinical path §6.5 explicitly sanctions: `TrialFeeding` now carries `intakeRating`, `trialDietRefusal` is computed over trial-diet feedings (floors: 3 rated samples, 2 distinct days, ≥50% refused), and it (a) makes the affirmative claim unsayable and (b) supplies a trial-viability line — health register, never "picky", pointing at the vet, and **only when the clinical detector is silent**. Coverage is deliberately unchanged: a bowl put down and refused is a day the owner kept the record, and scoring it as a gap would punish the most diligent owner in the app for the pet's illness.

2. **The free-choice bowl.** §5.6's standing off-diet arrangement was computed correctly and then never read by the wiring, so the card rendered *"12 bowl top-ups and wet meals logged; all 12 were the trial diet"* over a bowl of Aldi Chicken Dry — the single most important exposure in that household, computed and discarded, with the card affirming the opposite. Separately, `loggedFeedings` counted **raw** meal rows while `offDiet` counted **classified** ones, so the two halves of that sentence were on different denominators. **Fixed**: the replacement count is now the classified total, and an off-list arrangement withholds the claim.

3. **The un-hydrated allowed set → a 100% false accusation.** The guard was half-applied: `primaryCount === 0` catches a set that has not arrived, but not one whose `food_items_cache` rows have not, which reports a positive count with null keys and empty arrays. With a re-photographed bag (§5.4's documented, *encouraged* action) the ids no longer match and the keys are null, so **40 feedings of the prescribed diet** classified `off_diet_protein` naming **duck** — the trial's own protein — three lines under a standing note reading *"the trial food hasn't loaded on this device yet, so there's nothing to compare against"*. **Fixed**: the gate now honours `primaryResolved < primaryCount`, the same distinction `trialDietNote` already drew.

4. **A trial food with proteins but no designated primary disabled both halves at once.** `sanctionedProteinsOn` unioned its whole array, so a duck food listing chicken put **chicken into the sanctioned set** — the contaminant sanctioning itself, and every chicken chew for eight weeks classifying with `antigens: []`. Meanwhile `trialContamination` *skipped* the same food for lack of a comparator, so D-A went silent too, and nothing said the check was off. B-351 shape ① — the entire reason the feature exists — silently disabled. The suite even asserted the silence as correct. **Fixed**: a `primary_diet` food with no designated primary contributes **nothing** to the sanctioned set, which makes the two sets agree and lets the set come back empty — which disables the protein arm and fires `trialDietNote`'s B9 disclosure. And an empty sanctioned set now makes rung 2 **dark rather than universal**: under a naive set difference every protein is unsanctioned, so every food would be reported as carrying a contaminant against a diet nobody has characterised. Detection is unaffected (rung 3 still records); attribution goes quiet, which is §5.3's own "a dark rung 2 costs attribution, not detection".

The rest: rung 4 counted `missed`/`refused` doses (a second, contradictory definition of "the drug went in" against the one already live in `generate-signal/detection.ts`) and counted a pill hidden in the **prescribed diet** — 56 oral-route exposures across a 56-day trial, C2's alarm-fatigue reasoning applied to rungs 1–3 and forgotten at rung 4. Both fixed; `readDoses` now selects `adherence` and joins the vehicle's identity. `unclassifiable` and `oralRoute` were absorbed at the card boundary under a qualifier telling the reader flavoured products *"aren't visible here"* — so the one that **was** visible was the one dropped. And §5.2's floor caveat was appended only when `offDiet > 0`, i.e. stated on every card **except** the clean one, the only sentence a reader can mistake for a total.

All of it is now behind one exported rule, `mayClaimAllMatched`, which is one-directional by construction — there is no input that makes it turn a claim **on**.

**What held**, each with the input tried: the §5.5 named counterexample (same-day, backwards and beyond-window pairings all rejected; dog 14d / cat 7d / `other` 14d — the wider window, because a missed flare and a false pairing are not symmetric costs) · a permitted treat × 168 feedings across 56 days → one verdict, no alarm fatigue · the duck-that-lists-chicken with `proteins[0]` sanctioned → rung 2 fires · a permitted extra as a loophole → sanctioned set unwidened · D-B's tally with no leak into compliance · C2 by construction · dated membership in both directions · the re-captured duplicate with a hydrated set · the blank-key collision · one clock / one range against B-421's oracle · two-sidedness below the minimum span · the terminal-state refusal · the greppable negative-claim guard.

## Decisions taken

- **P-3 — the coverage floor** (0.8 / 0.5 / 7 days), provisional pending Dr. Chen. Two anchors, both about the **record** rather than the pet: ≥0.8 is the conventional documentation-adequacy cut-point in adherence measurement, and below 0.5 the modal day in the window has no data at all. The reviewer's caveat rides with the flag: the metric is day-granular and **saturates on the first meal of the day**, so "supports interpreting it" is affirmable for a once-a-day logger whose partner slips an unlogged jerky nightly — whether 0.8 over a saturating metric is the right bar is a clinical question, not an arithmetic one.
- **§10 S3 implemented**: coverage reports from `max(trial start, first log)` and names the pre-adoption span as untracked. The vet-directed setup — handed the diet at the clinic, back-date to the vet's start day, log from home — was scoring the owner 1 of 15 days for days before the app was on their phone. The clip is bounded by the first log, so it cannot hide a gap in the middle or the tail.
- **B-456 deliberately NOT ruled.** `allowed_from` back-dating on permitted extras biases the exposure floor down; the alternative over-counts and flags a food the vet may genuinely have permitted from day one. Both directions carry a real clinical cost, the backlog routes it to Dr. Chen, and the ruling is a change to the **write** in `lib/dietTrialSetup.ts` — so the predicate reads the column as PR 3 writes it and the trade-off is documented at `membershipOn`.
