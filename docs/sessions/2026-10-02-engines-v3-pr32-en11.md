## What shipped

- **Early food tier retired** (D5 = B). ① emits Established only.

- **Reversed-in-time control on ⑤.** It withholds when a meal follows a vomit at least as often as one precedes it, with long-band episodes excluded. A withheld ⑤ still suppresses ⑥.

  - None in the 48 h after a GI episode.
  - Matched without replacement.
  - At most 7 days from the case.

## Falsification record

**Adversarial pass 1: BROKEN.**
- **D1:** a failed flag read during an incomplete read dropped carried safety cards for every account.
- **D2:** a staple skipped on one day read Established.
- **D3:** a withheld ⑤ unmasked ⑥ and split the timing story.
- **D4:** a floor of 4 silenced 3 days of diarrhea, and the headline read "finished 47 of 47 meals".

**Pass 2:** D1, D3 and D4 held. D2 still broke: matching walked back past a diet change. Fixed with the 7-day cap.

**Pass 3: HOLDS.** It tried staples started 0–8 days before onset, a staple swapped mid-run, and bursts. A Monte Carlo null (vomiting independent of food, 600–4,000 runs per arm) put EN-11's false-Established rate within noise of flag off (at most 0.88% against 0.80%).

Every fix has a test, and each was mutation-checked (C-18).

**`vet-report-cold-read`:** NOT READY under EN-11. The report never renders the burden card, so a new sign has no dated line on page 1. Filed as CUL-1487. The cold read endorsed dropping "Rising frequency" on 2 vs 0.

## Lessons

- Putting a key in `SIGNAL_ENGINE_KEYS` moved more than the stand-down mint. The same gate guarded the CUL-989 carry, so a "withhold a sentence" gate became "drop warnings". Before adding a key, check every consumer of a gate.
- Two fixtures had to be rebuilt, because each passed for the wrong rule: the bland-diet case only reached Early, and the rice case leaked into the next day's controls. Isolate the rule under test.
