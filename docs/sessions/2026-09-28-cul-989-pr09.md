## What this taught

- **A subset argument covers counts, not comparisons or tiers.** "At least N" is true of any subset. A tier derived from a subset can still soften, and a card can vanish. The ruling's "never resolve" needed memory of what the owner was already told: the prior row.
- **Carrying memory needs a date and a shape check.** The first carry re-emitted a sentence whose truth depended on the day it was written, from jsonb the owner can write.
- **Changing the order rows arrive in is a behaviour change until proven otherwise.** Newest-first exposed a detector that read "the last row" under ties.

## Also in this PR: `/dispatch` may merge a green PR (PM, 2026-09-29)

The PM asked this session to merge its own PR, and it couldn't: `/dispatch` v1 launched it with "Never deploy, merge…" in its instructions (the 2026-09-28 ruling). At the PM's direction, `.claude/commands/dispatch.md` step 5 now lets a dispatched session squash-merge **its own** PR, and only when, on a fresh read immediately before the merge, all of these hold:

- it is not a draft;
- every check on its head commit has passed, Claude Approvals included;
- there is no conflict;
- the issue's Definition of Done passes;
- the PR holds no migration.

A migration row never merges itself. Step 6 flags the rows that will merge themselves and which functions that deploys.

Since the never-line no longer forbids merging, a child still gets the permission, but the merge conditions ride only in its prompt. The PM makes that edit, or allows it in settings.
