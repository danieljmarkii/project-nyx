### Wedge & brand
This serves the wedge. It is the one surface that captures the thing a diet-trial owner is actually sent home to watch and cannot currently record, it lands in the existing event vocabulary rather than a mood table, and it hands Dr. Chen "off on 3 of 42 looked days, first Sep 2" — a line he says he currently extracts with four questions. `Pets > $` is held explicitly (floor item 13) and nothing in §01–§08 gates any of it. The brand risk is not monetisation, it is **register drift into a habit app**: the recommended name ("What you noticed"), the receipt's "**you've** looked", and the option-a coverage line all centre the *owner's act* rather than the dog. The team already killed the coverage line for exactly this reason; the same pressure survives in the receipt's grammar and in the naming board's winner, and nobody has named it as one pattern.

### 🐞 Broken (product-visible)

- **The card rearranges under her thumb on every tap.** `render()`'s compact branch builds `var order = state.sel.slice(); HEAD.forEach(…)` — a selected chip **jumps to the front of the row**, and `commit()` FLIP-animates every chip whose rect moved. Tap "Clingy" (7th) and it flies to position 1; every other chip shifts. On a multi-select surface the PM explicitly loves, the second word's target has relocated between taps. This is the literal answer to "does it read as a card that keeps changing shape under her thumb" — yes, and not at the unfold, at the *selection*. Fix: chips hold position while the card is open; reordering happens only across the fold boundary.
- **"One tap, one Undo" — and no frame anywhere contains an Undo.** The string ships in three frames; the save is silent, the card collapses to its answered row, and no frame in §02, §03 or §04 draws a reversal control. Spec §3.6 says "a look has no photo, so its Undo needs no confirm — under i the confirm-free Undo reverses both rows and names both". Under C-21 a write with no confirm owes a way back, and here the copy *promises* it by name. Where does she tap it?
- **The compound write is promised, then not confirmed on the surface that made it.** After Done the answered row reads `**Looked** this morning · off · 7:12`. The low-energy row it also wrote is confirmed only on a *different card* ("1 low energy logged"). This is the regimen-vs-dose shape: she wrote two things and the card confirms one. Add the second fact to the row or the receipt.
- **The disclosure line is missing from the expanded storyboard.** Beat 2 renders a Done bar with "Off" selected and no `.writes` line, while the prototype's expanded branch does render it. A word picked from deep in the grid must not lose the disclosure; the static frame currently shows it doing so.
- **"one tap" is false as soon as two pip chips are selected.** `recLine()` generates "Off and Sleeping more also **go** in Mochi's symptom record as **low energy** — one tap, one Undo." That was two taps, and it writes one row, not two. Replace `one tap` with `one row`.
- **Sheet title contradicts its own first line.** The emergency sheet is titled "Signs that mean call today" and its first block is "**Call your vet now:** breathing hard…". Keep the *door* label as-is (§3.7's reasoning about not self-selecting the already-alarmed is right); rename the **sheet** to `When to call the vet`.

### 🤔 Works as built, but a real owner wouldn't get it — highest-value bucket

- **The pip is an unexplained red dot at rest.** On the resting card two chips carry a rose pip and nothing on screen says what it means — the `.writes` line only exists once something is selected. Rose is the app's safety colour. A first-time Jordan reads "Off" and "Sleeping more" as *the app's two flagged answers* before she has answered anything, which is the instrument leading the witness Dr. Chen ruled against in T-1. The caption's defence — "the same rose the record's symptom pips wear, so the meaning is learned once" — assumes she has already seen the record. On day 1 she has not.
- **Answering the PM's question directly: the pip line is understood *after* the tap, before the save — not before the tap.** §03's caption claims "the line under the Done bar says, **before the tap**, that the word also lands in the symptom record", and the prototype cannot do that: `recLine()` only renders once a `rec` chip is selected. That is defensible (nothing is written until Done) but the mock states it wrongly, and the line is 11px ink-2, *below* the primary button, in the position where fine print lives. Jordan taps Off → Done in three seconds and will not read it. **Move it above the Done bar** and it becomes true-in-practice; leave it where it is and option i will surprise people.
- **Three chips in the first row, three different consequences, two identical chevrons.** "Nothing unusual — I looked" saves a word; "Didn't eat ›" leaves the look entirely for a meal sheet with its own Save; "Not himself ›" selects *and* silently explodes the card to full height. Two of the three wear the same `ghost`+`›` treatment for a route-away and an in-place unfold. Cold Jordan taps "Didn't eat" expecting to record that he didn't eat.
- **The head words change their text under her once she unfolds.** Compact uses `WORDS[k].h`, expanded uses `WORDS[k].l`, so the chip she already selected mutates from "Off" to "Off, not getting up for the usual things" *while its box translates*. "Did it pick a different one?" Fix: keep the head label on a selected chip and render the gloss as a second line, or don't re-label chips that are already `on`.
- **"Something else ›" / "Show fewer ‹" are not a pair.** One is a promise of more words, the other is an instruction about display. Cold, "Show fewer" sounds like it will permanently trim the list. Propose `Something else ›` (keep — it is the owner's phrase) / `‹ Show fewer words`.
- **Sam cannot tell what was saved when the words are withheld.** Under a live intake concern the row reads `**Looked** this morning · 7:12` with the hint "Recorded — it's on Pixel's report, beside her meals." Every other frame prints her words back; this one doesn't, and the hint says *where it went*, not *that it took*. Propose: **"Saved — your words are on Pixel's report, beside her meals."** (Also: the spec's own version of this row carries the pet name and the mock drops it. C-9/CUL-660 says a record row should say whose record it is.)
- **The Patterns doorway only exists after you answer today.** `Patterns ›` sits in the seclabel of the *answered* card and not on the resting card. An owner who wants to look at her accumulated looks before answering today has no door to them.
- **"Nothing unusual — I looked" switches grammatical subject mid-row.** Every other chip has the pet as the implied subject; this one has the owner in the first person. It is also *not* how the row reads it back ("**Looked** … nothing unusual"). Propose **"Looked — nothing unusual"** so the chip and its receipt are the same sentence.

## Adversarial review — Daily Look spec v0.2 (`docs/nyx-daily-look-requirements.md`), second pass

### 1 · L-9 option i — the compound write into the engine

**HELD:** the `check_in` row itself never enters the engine. `supabase/functions/generate-signal/index.ts:805–807` selects only `.in('event_type', [...CORRELATION_SYMPTOM_TYPES])`, so a look is unfetchable by construction; §4.7's walk rows are belt-and-braces on top of that. Floor 5 holds *for the look row*.

**BROKE — the symptom row it writes. Three counterexamples.**

**(a) `lethargy` — the premise is factually wrong.** `grep -c lethargy supabase/functions/generate-signal/detection.ts` → **0**. `lethargy` is not in `SYMPTOM_TYPE_UNIVERSE` (`detection.ts:145`), not in `CORRELATION_SYMPTOM_TYPES` (`:167`), and in no `LANE_SYMPTOM_TYPES` cell (`:186–203`) — including chronicity ⑦. So §6.13's "the engine's lanes read the leaf as they always have" and §3.6 i's Data note are wrong: the engine reads it **not at all**. The rise lands instead on `REPORT_SYMPTOM_TYPES` (`supabase/functions/generate-report/report.ts:249–258`), `SYMPTOM_EVENT_TYPES` (`lib/analytics.ts:58–71`), `TREND_SYMPTOM_TYPES` (`lib/trendSummary.ts:42–51`), `ASK_SYMPTOM_TYPES` (`supabase/functions/ask/tools.ts:76–90`), the day-summary count chips (`lib/daySummary.ts:360`) and the widget's today tile (`lib/widgetSnapshot.ts:79`, `:493–520`). §6.13 names only "Patterns and the report."

**(b) `scratching_more → itch` — the reassuring failure. This is the blocker.** `LOOK_WORD_PROPOSES` (§4.7) carries `scratching_more: 'itch'`, and `itch` is in `PRE_TAXONOMY_LANE_TYPES` (`detection.ts:176–182`) — therefore in **every** lane cell, including `LANE_SYMPTOM_TYPES.symptomDelta`, which *is* `COMPARISON_GATE_SYMPTOM_SET` (`:257–259`).

Counterexample, traced: a dog with intermittent vomiting. Prior week — owner logs on 6 days (meals + a vomit). Current week — she is travelling, logs meals on 2 days, but taps the look every morning and taps *Scratching more* on 5 of them (allergy season, unrelated to the GI question). `loggingDaysInWindow` (`:3810–3839`) counts a day if any symptom passes `countsTowardComparisonGate` — itch passes — so `currentLoggingDays = 7`, `priorLoggingDays = 6`. `computeReflectionDensity` (`:4022–4041`): `7 >= 6 × 0.7` → **`comparable = true`** → the reflection lane publishes *"3 this week, down from 8 last week."* Without the look, `currentLoggingDays = 2`, `2 < 4.2`, comparable false, the comparison **withheld**.

The app becomes *more* confident that vomiting is improving on the strength of rows it solicited itself. The module already litigated this shape for cough (`detection.ts:232–241`: "12 cough-only days inside a trial era flipped `densityComparable` false→true and published a 5× apparent vomiting reduction the guard had correctly withheld"), and the ruled principle at `:215–219` is verbatim: *"A GATE WHOSE FAILURE DIRECTION IS REASSURANCE READS ITS LANE'S CELL; A GATE WHOSE FAILURE DIRECTION IS ESCALATION KEEPS FULL COVERAGE."* Look-written itch is **worse than cough**: cough is at least pet-state-dependent; a prompted chip is *prompt*-dependent, and under R1 the prompt fires on every account every morning. The same flip reaches `trialLoggingFraction` (`:5331`, `:5411`) and `computeChronicityCompare`'s receipt denominator (`:4088–4098`) — the latter being the exact number CUL-787's `alsoCounts` was added to make honest.

**(c) `itch` into lane ① — pseudoreplicated case-crossover.** `correlationWindowHoursByType.itch = 72` (`:2323`), `symptomEpisodeGapHours = 3` (`:2329`). Daily 7:12am taps are 24h apart → **each morning is its own episode**; nothing collapses. Take a mildly itchy dog, 14 taps in 8 weeks. `earlyMinMatchedPairs: 3` (`:2331`) is met five times over. Control selection (`:3311–3323`) rejects any day in `symptomDays` and any day within 72h of the case, and — critically — has **no used-control set**: `bestCtrl` is chosen independently per onset, so one surviving control day can back all 14 pairs. Exact McNemar (`:2749`, `:988`) then sees b+c=14 discordant "pairs" that are 14 cases against ~1 control. `pValue <= correctedAlpha` (`:3531`) becomes reachable → **Established**, on a food, from prompt artifacts. The control-reuse weakness pre-dates the look; the look is what multiplies the case count by 5× and converts it from latent to live. Secondary effect: `symptomDays` grows, control days become scarce and non-random, so on a heavy looker `bestCtrl` goes null and ① silently stops firing for the owner using the app most.

**(d) Floor 5 is overridden, not re-opened.** §2 item 5 — "A look never enters a count, a floor, a verdict, a trial verdict or the coverage line" — is a "re-open only with a named counterexample" floor. Under L-9 i the look transitively enters: a density/coverage gate (b), a Bonferroni family (c), the diagnostics floor (`countSymptomEpisodes`, `:5711`), the report's §3.5 frequency table, the widget tile, the Trend zone, Ask's counts. §6.13's mitigation covers only two-half comparisons on Patterns and the report. The floor is not re-opened anywhere in §0.

**Minimal fix (G1, blocker).** Either (i) drop `scratching_more` from the pip set in v1 — the two remaining pips (`lethargy`) are engine-invisible, which contains the damage to the render surfaces in (a) — or (ii) hold L-9 i until the engine can see provenance: `logged_via` added to `index.ts:805`'s select, a `loggedVia` field on `SymptomEvent`, and a look-written row excluded from `countsTowardComparisonGate` / `loggingDaysInWindow` / `LANE_SYMPTOM_TYPES.correlation` while remaining fully counted everywhere coverage or escalation is the question — i.e. the exact seam `:215–252` already cuts. Option (ii) rides the held **CUL-557/CUL-19** redeploy chain. Either way §6.13's "errs toward escalation, the safe direction" must be struck and Q-10 answered as *no*.

### 2 · The compound write and its Undo

**BROKE, four ways.**

**(a) The guard named cannot do the job.** §3.6 i and §5.5 say "`guards/reversePath.test.ts` extends to the compound." That file (`guards/reversePath.test.ts:20–30`) is a syntactic scan for call sites of `softDeleteEvent`, and says so in its own header: *"A syntactic scan proves a file does not call the primitive — not that its reversal is correct, nor that `reverseLoggedEvent` settles everything it should. That is review's job."* It cannot express a cascade. The compound needs the cascade inside `reverseLoggedEvent` (`lib/undoLog.ts:118–149`) plus its own test.

**(b) The cascade does not exist and its side-effects don't compose.** `reverseLoggedEvent` soft-deletes exactly one `events` row. The symptom row is a **sibling** `events` row, not a child of the `check_in` event, so `ON DELETE SET NULL` on `looks.wrote_event_id` (§5.2) can never fire — the house rule is soft delete, never `DELETE`. If the cascade is implemented as two sequential `reverseLoggedEvent` calls you get two `reconcileWeightSnapshotAfterDelete` passes and two `triggerSignalRegenDebounced` fires (`lib/undoLog.ts:126`, `:138`); the regen must fire once, after both deletes, or it recomputes over a still-live itch row.

**(c) The symptom row reversed alone from History breaks the C-4 partition.** Counterexample: owner taps *Off* Monday; Wednesday she opens the lethargy row from History and Removes it. `events.deleted_at` is set; `looks.wrote_event_id` still points at it; the look row still holds `words: ['subdued']` and Home still says *"Looked · off · 7:12"*. Now §6.13's provenance split has two possible implementations and they disagree: counted from the `looks` side it prints *"low energy on 5 days · 4 from the look"* over 3 live look-written rows — **two counts over one population that do not partition it** (CLAUDE.md C-4), on a vet-facing surface. Counted from the events side, the look row is orphaned and Patterns' *"off on 6 mornings"* silently exceeds *"low energy from the look"* with nothing explaining it. **The spec specifies both mechanisms (`wrote_event_id` *and* `logged_via`) and never says which is authoritative.** Fix: the split is derived **only** from the symptom row's own `logged_via`; `wrote_event_id` serves the Undo cascade and nothing else; reversing the symptom row NULLs it.

**(d) Sync-ordering hazard, and it is not hypothetical.** `logged_via` is a Postgres enum (`supabase/migrations/038_logged_via.sql:101–110`) whose values are `app · notification · reconciled · widget · intent · watch · device` — **there is no `'look'`**, and §5.5's build checklist and DL-1 don't add one. `lib/loggedVia.ts:6–13` mirrors it. Also `insertSimpleEvent` (`lib/simpleEvent.ts:96–110`) does not write the column at all, and `lib/sync.ts:2110` pushes `e.logged_via ?? 'app'`. So on the sequence as specified, either the provenance is never stamped, or — if the enum add is forgotten in the same deploy — the symptom event's push is rejected server-side and quarantined while the `check_in` event and the `looks` child push cleanly. The server then holds a look whose `wrote_event_id` FK cannot resolve, so the `looks` insert is rejected too, leaving a **parentless `check_in` server-side**. `generate-report` runs server-side; its page-1 denominator ("Looked on 118 of 128 days") is computed from `looks`, while Patterns computes from local SQLite where every row exists. **The report and Patterns disagree on the denominator** — a G5-parity defect on the surface §7's always-printed denominator exists to protect.

**Minimal fix (G4, high).** Add the `logged_via` enum value to DL-1's migration with its own irreversibility note (an enum value cannot be dropped — same caveat `check_in` already carries); thread `loggedVia` through `insertSimpleEvent` + the local INSERT + the sync push; put the cascade in `reverseLoggedEvent` with one regen at the end; state that reversing either row NULLs `wrote_event_id`; state that the provenance split reads `logged_via` only; add a fixture pinning report/Patterns denominator parity when the child push fails.

Head word = tap + Done (or one hold). Non-head word = **unfold + tap + Done** (or unfold + hold) — strictly one gesture more. T-8 says "Every answer costs the same." It is true of 7 words and false of 19–21.

§3.1a states the objection against families-first in exactly these words — *"a word costs one tap more than Nothing unusual does, which is the asymmetry Door D was rejected for, now inside the grid"* — and then recommends a shape with the same property for three-quarters of the vocabulary. **The difference between the two options is quantitative (which 7 words are exempt), not categorical, and the brief presents it as categorical.** L-8's decision brief is therefore mis-framed: the real question is *which words are worth exempting*, not *head-words-vs-families*.
