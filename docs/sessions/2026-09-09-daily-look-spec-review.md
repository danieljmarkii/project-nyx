## Method — the premises first

Round 4's own recorded lesson was that three of its rules rested on surfaces the tree does not have, every one checkable in a grep and none checked. So this review began by re-verifying **every premise the spec borrows from the tree** — 47 claims across three isolated sweeps (file:line consumers; schema, sync and local storage; the app surfaces the card lands on) — before any persona weighed in. The results are the errata table below. Then each lens walked the spec against its own routing-table responsibilities (`docs/personas.md` § Persona Routing Table), and every open item was triaged by the gate it sits on.

## What is working well

1. **The decision record is complete and traceable.** R1–R16, L-1–L-17, T-1–T-22, each with its origin (the PM line or the pass that produced it), its consequence and its enforcement. A build session never has to reconstruct *why*; the "text that left lives in git at `117eb59` / `5d420dc`" convention means nothing survives by omission and nothing is lost.
2. **The engine boundary held four adversarial passes and holds in the tree.** A look never enters a count, a floor, a verdict or another surface's coverage line (T-5, floor 5). Verified: `check_in` appears nowhere under `supabase/functions/`; `lethargy` appears nowhere in `detection.ts`; every counting consumer in §5.1 is real and reads by an explicit list. The exhaustive-switch plan on `EventTintCategory` plus the §13a walk rows is the right two-part guard for a fail-open exclusion.
3. **The day model is the right unit and is stored once.** `local_day` written on the device (T-19), every count keyed on it, one grouping module (`lib/looks.ts`) before there are two consumers — the diet-trial §5.3 lesson applied preemptively. The two-clock premise is true: the client buckets in the device zone (`lib/utils.ts` `localDayIndex`), the report in `user_profiles.timezone` with a UTC fallback (`report.ts` `localDayKey`).
4. **The n=1 rules are on every owner surface, not only the clinical ones.** Receipts earned by symptom-class words only and absent below the floor; the pairing with both sides answered days, positive margin only, one per card; the withheld state on a positive intake fact, never on ignorance; the footer's three forms with the saturation case named. Each rule carries the counterexample that produced it.
5. **Shape A has a near-exact shipped template.** `weight_checks` (024) is the same 1:1 child: denormalized `pet_id`, the pet-scoped policy, the parent-gated drain (`drainWeightChecksQueue` joins `events` on `synced = 1`), `hydrateWeightChecks` after `hydrateEvents`, deletedness through the parent at read time. DL-2 is a transcription of a pattern that already survives offline → reconnect → flush.
6. **Privacy is in the schema, not in a review note.** The note on `looks.notes` only, the parent's `notes` closed by CHECK, the editor gate, the same-pet trigger (the 023/041 mechanism, verified), the two-audiences rule for the share link, the export gap named (CUL-232). The one wider problem it found is filed where it belongs (CUL-848).
7. **Every new rule names its guard and its mutation proof.** `homeWrites` by closure and effect with three named mutants; `lookNotes` keyed on the column; `completionCard` extended by one row in an existing `RULES` table (verified shape); the haptics scan split with the withheld entry in `ALWAYS_SCANNED`. This is the C-18 discipline applied before the code exists.
8. **The copy is verbatim and the interaction is prototyped.** Nearly every owner-facing string is in the spec or the mock; §02 of the mock is a working tap-through of the unfold and the way back. The `nyx-voice` pass per PR is bounded, not a rewrite.
9. **Scope discipline: everything deferred has a home.** The FAB tile (CUL-841), the notification (CUL-842), the AI read (CUL-843), the symptom link (CUL-845), the family correction (CUL-849), the shipped notes field (CUL-848). Nothing is "later" without an issue.
10. **The floor (§2) and the vocabulary (§4) are stable across four rounds.** Fifty-five words passed the diagnosis / verdict / preference tests on the first pass and were never re-litigated; the per-word `Never beside` column is the tool a copy pass actually uses.
