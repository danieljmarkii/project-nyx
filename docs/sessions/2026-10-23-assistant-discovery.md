What I think could be really appealing as well is the ability to use that assistant to jot down notes like on their pets behavior. What else might this assistant be able to help w/? Ensure that our consultants are brought in from day 1. Especially the design / motion consultants. As you're getting ramped up here.. let me know what questions you have."*

The stimulus was ThePawcess's "AI DAILY LOG" card: a sent-text bubble ("gave a dental chew around 3pm, paws a bit itchier tonight"), a divider "AI STRUCTURED IT", then "✓ Logged to Day 24", a "24-DAY STREAK" pill, Status → "SLIP FLAGGED", Exposure → "dental chew · 3:00 PM", Itch severity → "3 / 5 ▲", and a seven-day itch sparkline.

"Ask" is absorbed as a *door* from the composer to the built `/ask`, not merged as a function: two Edge Functions, one shared gate chain (consent, ownership, caps, the crisis gate), two contracts (Data, Engineering); "one field is survivable, one verb is not" (Dr. Chen).

- **"Same as yesterday" is not a button.** A day copy fabricates observations nobody made and clears a coverage floor with no observation (Data, Dr. Chen); the honest shortcut for a meal already ships as the FAB's three recent foods, and a repeat dose goes through the medication write path (Home's med strip and its confirm retired at Design v2 GA). - **Behaviour words: a jot may propose one Noticed chip, from the frozen set only, after the note is saved, with the owner's sentence kept whole beneath; never a new word, never a count, never a why the app wrote** (Jordan, Sam, Dr. Chen, Data, the Designer).
