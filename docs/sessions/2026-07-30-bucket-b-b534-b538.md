## B-534 — the freshness pair

**The race.** Every local write is local-first with a fire-and-forget push; `generate-report` reads live Supabase. So "end the trial → tap Open vet report" lost the race on any weak signal and the artifact rendered the server's still-`active` row — an ended trial reading to the vet as ongoing, the B-455 harm arriving via timing. The shipped gate (`flushBeforeReport` + `reportFreshnessLine`, `lib/pdf.ts`) is the **second** design — the first was rebuilt by the adversarial pass, and the rebuild is most of what this session is worth recording (below). Shape: before generating, read `getSyncStatus()`; if anything is pending, **await one `syncNow()` cycle** — the documented flush with the FK ordering and the in-flight guard, covering *every* queue — then re-count. Whatever still holds out is disclosed beside "Send to vet" under the B-398 two-state rule (quarantine leads, and never gets the connection advice). Send stays available — at the clinic on bad reception, a report honest about being a step behind beats no report.

**The stale strip.** Home and the Pet tab are two independent `useDietTrial` instances and only the writing screen got a `reload()`. The hook already re-reads on `hydrationTick`; the writes just never bumped it. All three write paths (`startDietTrial` / `endActiveTrial` / `extendTrial`) now end in `notifyTrialChanged()` — in the lib, not the call sites, because the next surface to call one of these will not know the Home strip exists. Pinned in `dietTrialSetup.test.ts`, including the negative case (a refused extension does not bump).

## B-535 — the round trip that stranded the form

`StartTrialModal` is kept mounted so the half-filled form survives "Snap a new food" → `/food-capture`; the picker's own docstring calls that the *common* path (the trial food is a bag the owner was handed ten minutes ago). But food-capture's save ends in `router.dismissAll()` and nothing ever re-opened the modal — the owner landed back on the tab modal-less, concluding the trial saved or the work was lost (`pm-feature-review`, BROKEN class). The host now owns the whole trip: `onAddFood` arms a `resumeTrialModalOnFocus` ref, and the tab's `useFocusEffect` re-opens the modal when focus returns — whichever way the capture ended, save or back-out, because the half-filled form is the owner's to finish or cancel either way. The modal's visible-effect re-runs its active-trial pre-flight on re-open, so the resume lands on the form step with every field intact and the new food one picker-tap away.

## B-536 — the guard that counted a different series than it rendered

`densityLine`'s before-half was guarded on `beforeTracked` (ANY event in the stretch) while the number it renders counts MEAL days. The pre-ship adversarial pass executed the gap: an owner who tracked symptoms for weeks before the trial but logged no food — the ordinary install-for-symptoms story — rendered *"Days you logged any food: 0 of 56 before, 52 of 56 during"*, a fabricated zero that reads as "logging went from nothing to daily" and lends the symptom comparison above a credibility check it never passed. Flattering direction, on the screen that ends the intervention — and R4 makes that screen the verdict-bearing one. The guard now keys on the series it renders: a zero meal-day before-half renders the during-only count plus *"There's no food logging from before the trial to compare that with."* One meal-day is deliberately still a comparison — the guard is "no before-series exists", never an invented floor. Tests include an every-string sweep of the built sheet (the instrument the untracked case already used, because a guard that holds in `densityLine` and leaks through another field is the exact shape the original bug had).

**R4's copy half.** The outcome question was always un-gated on Save; R4 flips that from filed bug (B-508) to design, and the copy now makes the design legible: both question notes and the facts-unreadable fallback carry *"Answering is optional — the counts/record go on the report either way."* The claim was verified before it was written: `render.ts` renders the owner line only `if (t.outcome)`, so the counts genuinely ride the report unanswered. The Save button carries the R4 comment forbidding a future `disabled={!outcome}`.
