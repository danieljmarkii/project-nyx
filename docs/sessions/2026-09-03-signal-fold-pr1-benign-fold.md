# Signal fold PR 1 — the fold primitive + the benign fold (CUL-784)

**Date:** 2026-09-03
**Outcome:** shipped via #796 (draft) — Home v1, PR 1 of 3. Unblocks PR 2 (CUL-785, the safety strips) and PR 3 (CUL-788, the motion).

## What shipped

The first rung of the Signal fold (spec `docs/nyx-signal-fold-requirements.md` v1.2, CUL-695 direction F3): a benign Signal card folds to a one-line strip in place, at rank, persists on the device per pet per finding, and comes back when the **record** moves the finding — never when the calendar does. All accounts, no flag. No engine change, no detection / ranking / threshold delta, nothing under `supabase/functions/`.

- **`lib/signalFold.ts`** — the pure core + the AsyncStorage shell. `foldIdentity` (type + the noun the sentence is about; a correlation keys on its sorted cluster so a member joining is a new identity; a lone `postprandial_timing` that becomes a `timing_story` renders open). `MATERIAL_FIELDS` is the §5.3 table as *data* — increase-only counts, one decrease-only field (chronicity's `daysSinceLastEpisode`, the net-zero day), one turn-on flag (the cough↔vomit adjacency), any-change tiers / booleans / directions — for all eleven types, so `materialChange` is total and PR 2 only flips the class gate. `reconcileFolds` implements the release rules: an absent key deletes the entry (the improving-then-relapsing course renders as a full card), a material change writes `reopened` with its reason, a reopened entry clears on the next fingerprint change of any kind. One addition to the spec's rules, found while writing the property test: **a folded entry whose fingerprint moved but not materially has its stored fingerprint follow the record down.** Fold at 8 timed episodes; the window ages to 6; a new episode makes 7 — against the fold-day 8 that is a decrease and the card would stay shut on a real new episode; against the record it is the increase it is. The shell is one key (`nyx.signalFold`), the `signalArrival` clear-epoch guard on the read-modify-write, `readFoldEntries` returning `null` (not `{}`) on an unreadable read, a prune that refuses an empty keep-list, and `clearSignalFold` wired into `wipeLocalSession` by name with the `session.test.ts` assertion.
- **`hooks/useSignalFold.ts`** + **`useSignal.answered`.** The reconcile deletes any fold whose key is absent from the set, so it must only ever run against a set that was actually read. `useSignal` gains `answered` — true once `readSignalCache` resolved for this pet (with a row or with none), false while in flight and after a throw, reset on a pet switch. The hook reconciles on `answered`, keys its snapshot on the pet the findings belong to, and writes are plain side effects of the tap (never inside a state updater).
- **`components/home/InsightCard.tsx`** — the face `Pressable` narrows to the sentence + evidence; the control row is a **sibling** (`Why we're showing this` / `Hide details` as a real button; `Keep it compact` beside it, benign cards only via `canFold`); the expanded caption *It comes back on its own when the picture changes.*; the Back-because line inside the face and prefixed to its a11y label; `FoldedStrip` (rail · name · count · chevron, `minHeight 44`, each line its own `Text` with no `numberOfLines`); every `LayoutAnimation.configureNext` gated on `useReducedMotion` (the shipped toggle animated regardless — corrected in passing).
- **`SignalZone` / `LiveStack`** — strip / re-opened face / face per entry; order = server rank; `isLead` stays `i === 0` on rank (a benign card is never promoted to the Newsreader canvas because the card above it folded); an all-folded zone is label + strips + doorway.
- **`lib/signalCopy.ts`** — `stripNameLine` / `stripCountLine` for the seven benign types, `backBecauseCopy`, the fold strings.

## Decisions made in the build (flagged on CUL-784 for the Designer)

1. **§3.3 geometry vs C-5.** The spec's control row — a 28pt line reached upward by `hitSlop {top:16, bottom:0}` — puts 16pt of each control's hit area over the face's meta row. Under `Keep it compact` a tap on the sample line's right end would fold the card: the shared hit area C-5 exists to forbid. A compact row has 33pt of exclusive vertical reach under the meta row (8 gap + 16 text + 8 padding + 1 hairline + 8 next padding − 8 next slop), so the 44pt floor cannot be met without the card growing. Built: control row `marginTop 8`, control box `minHeight 28` (text centred), `hitSlop 8` all round. The 8pt up-slop exactly fills the 8pt gap (face bottom slop 0); the 8pt down-slop meets the next face at the hairline (17 ≥ 16 on a compact row, 25 on a lead); 8 + 28 + 8 = 44. Every relation is asserted off the flattened style, including across cards in `SignalZone.fold.test.tsx`. Cost: the card grows ~12pt. A real control costs its floor.
2. **FS-11 caps vs the §4 forms.** The spec's own verbatim `timing_story` name (`Vomiting soon or hours after eating`) is 35 characters and its correlation example is 34, against a stated ≤30. The binding requirement is one line at 375pt / default type; the strip's text column is ~282pt there, ~39 characters at 13pt medium and ~46 at 11pt. Caps pinned at **36 / 44** with the arithmetic in the test, every form at its realistic maximum (two-digit counts, the longest symptom word the type carries). The correlation name line is reshaped to `{Symptom} after {protein}` (the spec's form runs to 41 for `skin irritation`) — the same temporal shape as the timing strips, no attribution; the hedge stays in the sentence and the evidence. Proposed §3.1/§4 correction.
3. **One Back-because string added:** `Back because the timing changed.` — the timing types' structural flips (a clock band shifting, a last-two flag) are re-open triggers whose cause is not always a new episode, and "a new episode was logged" must not be a guess. Voice pass run: plain, count-neutral, no verdict.
4. **Under reduced motion PR 1 ships instant geometry**, not the §3.2 `durationFast` crossfade — §9 says "allowed", not required, and PR 3 owns the motion and its static swap.

## Process

- The plan-gate was posted to the issue and the build proceeded without waiting: the session ran unattended, the issue is marked build-ready with no PM fork, and the draft PR is the redirect point.
- The `SignalZone` nothing-folded snapshot was committed **first, against the shipped tree** (01f3075), then updated when the control row landed — so the byte-level change to the surface is one inspectable diff in git rather than a claim.
- **Proof by mutation (C-18):** inverting the increase-only comparison in `materialChange` reds 7 tests (the increase property, the decrease-only property, the intake day-4 fixture, the release, the baseline-follows-the-record case, the FS-7 isolation case); restored, 37/37 green.

- **`code-reviewer` (isolated, scratch-copied tree):** no correctness bug found in the reconcile / write paths, the `answered` gating, the pet-switch keying or the `commit()` closure; it re-proved three guards by mutation on its own (removing the `answered` gate reds the C-12 zone test; promoting the next row to `isLead` reds the DF-7 test; inverting the count asymmetry reds nine property tests). Verdict *fix-before-merge* on three items, all addressed: (1) the correlation name-line rewording and (2) the FS-11 cap change need a visible sign-off / Tier-2 flag rather than a code comment — carried as explicit persona lines in the DoD and as proposed spec edits on the issue and in the summary; (3) `useSignal.answered` had no direct test against the real hook — five cases added to `hooks/useSignal.test.ts` (in flight → false; resolved with rows / with no row → true; a throw → stays false; a later throw does not revoke an earlier answer; a pet switch resets it in the same render). Two nits recorded, not acted on: the `reopened` entry carries a `fingerprint` the spec's §5.1 type sketch omits (needed for release rule 2 — a spec correction), and the clear-epoch guard, reused verbatim from `signalArrival`, does not cover a `setItem` already past its epoch check racing a same-tick `removeItem` — inherited, not new.
