Should the diet trial get its own screen?

Today the trial strip on Home opens the Pet tab, scrolled past the photo, the weight and the medications to the trial card. Design v2 gave the Signal a screen of its own. The team met on whether the trial should get one too, and every lens said yes, with conditions. This page draws what they agreed, and draws side by side the choices only you can make.

Your ideaShould tapping the diet trial card from home also have a similar detail screen? That feels like it would be a significantly cleaner experience.
Asked once beforeDraft PR #631 (Aug 12) proposed a /trial room opened from the strip. It was never ruled. Since then the Signal screen, trial window changes, Get ready, History v2 and "The trial so far" have all shipped, so this round starts from today's app, not from August's.
Who read itFive isolated reads, none of which saw the others: Jordan; Sam; Dr. Chen with the Data Scientist; the Designer with the Mobile Information Architect and the Data Visualization Designer; Engineering with QA, the Product Owner and Trust & Safety.
The short answerYes. A route of its own, built on the Signal screen's physics (the rise, Back, the pet in the link), not on its sections. It computes nothing new, safety leads it, and nothing about symptoms ships before CUL-1216.
What you ruleSix briefs in §07. Two are drawn side by side: the first frame (§03) and what the screen says about vomiting mid-trial (§05).
§01

Where the tap lands today

The strip is the only place a trial owner meets the trial every day, and it does its job. The problem is the tap. It opens a card that lives deep on a page built for a different job.

Jordan: "Home says three vomits in the trial against eleven before. I tap to understand it, and the card I land on never mentions vomiting. That's the worst one."
Sam: "On a bad day I don't want to scroll past her photo, weight and meds."
§02

What every lens agreed

It is a route.Back gesture, a deep link and VoiceOver focus for free. The pet comes from the link, never from whichever pet is active (C-9).
Borrow the Signal's physics, not its sections.The Signal screen presents one finding: title, chart, compare, why. Copied onto a trial, that order brings the mid-trial compare with it.
It computes nothing.Every number comes from the trial's existing facts and one resolver. If the strip withholds a number, the screen withholds it too.
Safety leads, and replaces the top.The fact with its numbers, then the ask, then the record, then the doors. Keep going hides while a refusal is live.
It only shows.Logging stays on the +. No new write, and no new predicate.
Starting a trial stays on the Pet tab.The spec says so (§4.1), and food capture's exit would pop a pushed screen and lose a half-filled form.
The recheck has one home: Get ready.August's "For the recheck" folds into it. The trial screen carries a door.
Nothing about symptoms before CUL-1216.The Signal screen, one tap away, draws a trial compare the strip deliberately withholds.
One motion.The Signal's rise, reversed on Back. No flight, since a 4pt bar has nothing to fly. The ledger draws in once, with no per-day stagger: 56 marks popping in would read as a celebration.

§07

Decision briefs

D1 · The first frame conflict

Deciding: what an owner sees the moment the screen rises (§03).

Designer (with the Mobile IA and Data Viz): lead with the trial (frame A). The shipped card is over 400pt tall; put it on top and the first frame is the Pet tab with a back button.

Engineering + Jordan: host the shipped card unchanged (frame B). One component, no second layout to keep in step across twelve states, the cheapest v1. Jordan: "The card I already know sits at the top. Don't give me a new version."

PM decision needed: A · B · or B as v1, with A's layout from the same resolver in v1.1.

Consequence: A adds a layout PR and the ledger, which needs a new row in the §05 chart standard. B fits the 4–5 PR skeleton in §09.

D2 · The Pet tab once the screen exists recommendation

Deciding: whether the Pet tab keeps the full card (§06).

Options: a one-row door while a trial runs or has just ended, and the start card unchanged when there is none (recommended by the Designer, Mobile IA, Data Viz and Jordan: every action in one place) · keep the full card, fed by the same resolver.

Consequence: a Tier-2 edit to the trial spec §4.1 and §4.2. The lifecycle sheets move out of the Pet tab into one shared host either way.

D3 · Vomiting on the screen mid-trial conflict

Deciding: what the screen says about symptoms before the trial ends (§05).

Shared floor: nothing before CUL-1216; never less than Home in the escalating direction; (a) is acceptable to every lens.

Dr. Chen: weekly counts with logged days and the adjacency line, no compare drawn (b). A mid-trial difference invites a verdict on a curve that a steroid, an antiemetic or a quiet week can move. He adds a brief of his own: GI trials usually read by about two weeks, so a text-only compare for GI trials from day 14, on a fixed baseline, with medications named, deserves your ruling.

Data Scientist: one compare, owned by one module, with its gate inside it (c), withheld whenever an antiemetic, steroid or antibiotic overlaps either window. "A door doesn't contain the leak." Weekly counts beside History's pre-trial weeks let the owner build the compare anyway, against memory, which is the worst baseline.

PM decision needed: (a) only in v1, then (b) or (c) once Dr. Chen rules the medication overlap.

Consequence: (b) and (c) each need a clinical ruling and an adversarial pass. (a) adds no number.

D4 · Where it lives, and what gates it recommendation

Deciding: the flag and the project.

Options: its own allowlist flag and its own project, built after the App Store cut (recommended by Engineering and the Product Owner: riding design_v2 either adds a surface to CUL-1071's GA or ships this one unreviewed) · ride design_v2 inside "Design v2 — the whole day", as the Signal screen's sibling.

Consequence: one flag-seed PR. Design v2's GA scope stays as ruled.

D5 · Where the recheck lives recommendation

Deciding: whether the trial screen grows a "For the recheck" section.

Options: Get ready is the one home; the trial screen carries a door, and Get ready's trial row answers the vet's recheck questions, with Dr. Chen's questions as its headings, in the order he asks them (recommended by Dr. Chen, the Data Scientist and Jordan) · a recheck section on the trial screen (August's T3).

Consequence: retires T3. It becomes a Get ready change whose row quotes the trial's numbers and never recounts them.

D6 · Vomiting when the app can't see her eat conflict

Deciding: whether a vomiting count shows on a live refusal or a topped-up bowl (§04, §05).

Dr. Chen: keep a raw vomiting total on a refusal. Vomiting on top of not eating raises the urgency.

Data Scientist: withhold it, as the strip does today (B-789). A falling count from a cat that isn't eating is false reassurance.

Sam adds: the strip still shows its vomiting line for a topped-up bowl, because the not-eating check leaves free-fed out.

PM decision needed: send it to Dr. Chen with CUL-51's refusal rulings and CUL-1294.

Consequence: the refusal frame in §04 draws the shipped rule (withheld) until then.

Better than the rule · the strip's tap rule, 2026-07-25

The rule: "Tapping opens the Pet tab card" (trial spec §4.2). §4.2 also scoped the whole feature at six screens, and §4.1 makes the Pet tab card the only way to start a trial.

What it protected: one card with one set of meanings. It was written to put the trial on Home because "the Pet tab is not a surface the wedge owner visits daily", which is the same argument for this screen.

The better thing: the tap opens the trial's own screen. The protection holds as long as one resolver feeds both places and each action lives in exactly one of them.

Recommendation: amend §4.2's routing and its screen count. Keep §4.1: starting stays on the Pet tab.

Better than the rule · trial weeks on the ledger rule, 2026-09-19

The rule: the Design v2 chart standard (§05) starts every week on Sunday, so rows and bars agree across screens.

The better thing: the ledger counts trial weeks, days 1 to 7, the unit the protocol and the vet count in (Data Viz).

Does the protection hold: yes, as long as Sunday-start weekly bars never share a screen with the ledger. The option (c) compare in §05 uses windows, not weeks.

Recommendation: trial weeks on the ledger.

§09

If you say go

Engineering's sequence for the screen itself, before any new section:

  1. The trial hooks take a pet. Every trial hook and list screen reads the active pet today. A screen whose pet comes from the link but whose doors follow the active pet would show the wrong patient's lists. A refactor, green before and after.
  2. The lifecycle sheets leave the Pet tab for one shared host, mounted by whichever places keep actions.
  3. The flag seed (D4).
  4. The route. The gate in the route, the drawing in its own namespace. With the flag off, the link still answers and points to the Pet tab card.
  5. The senders: Home's strip, the Day Summary recap, and the widget, re-pointed from the app's side because the widget is frozen.

Then one PR per new section: the ledger (D1), and symptoms after CUL-1216 (D3). Not in v1: the before-and-during compare, weekly symptom counts, a recheck section, any share, any way to start a trial, any new predicate or write.

QA's one risk: a read that hasn't answered must never print "No trial running". That would be a false all-clear on the app's main feature, and CUL-400 is this exact defect on the two trial list screens today.
