02

The GI glyphs, honestly

The reaction was right to poke here, and it deserves a straight answer. The droplet/circle abstractions were a deliberate MVP-era call — the code comment says they were chosen for "visual distinctness + clinical calm over literal fidelity" when the emoji (🤢 💩) were swept out. But the calm came at the cost of legibility: nobody can tell what a bare circle is, and a droplet for vomit is exactly the "metaphor that requires explanation" the iconography rule bans. That's the definition of getting fancy.

The fix isn't returning to emoji — it's literal subjects drawn in the house line style: 1.75px stroke, rounded caps, monochrome or category-tinted. What made the poop emoji feel wrong for a clinical tool was the cartoon face and the brown fill, not the shape. A line-drawn swirl with no face, tinted rose at 20px in a wash circle, reads like a pictogram on a vet chart, not a text message. These two would be the first custom glyphs in the product — drawn in-house as two small SVG paths (react-native-svg is already under Lucide), which quietly starts the B-746 icon family with its two hardest cases instead of waiting on a commission.

The distance from the text-message register comes from the stroke style, the absence of a face, and the clinical tint system around it. If that rhyme still feels wrong on the frames below, G1's fallback is round 1's abstractions (CircleDot + Droplet), which are systemically fine — just quieter than they are legible.

04

What removing the photo entry costs — the audit

  • Nothing is lost from capture. Photos attach inside every event flow today (the photo row in the detail steps) — that row is untouched. The grid tile only offered photo-first ordering: pick the image, then answer "which event is this for?". That ordering dies; the capability doesn't.
  • Dead code retires with it: the attachment banner on the type step, the attachmentUri-before-type state, and the dashed tile. The design-system README's dashed-border exception gets a Tier-2 cleanup note (the exception's one consumer is gone).
  • Vomit/stool AI reads are unaffected — they trigger off photos attached in the event flow, which is where owners were attaching them anyway.
  • One watch item for the on-device pass: if any owner habitually started from the camera roll (photo taken at the scene, logged later), their path is now event-first + attach inside — same taps, reversed order. Worth one line in the release QA script, nothing more.
