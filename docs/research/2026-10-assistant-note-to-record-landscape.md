# The assistant: note → record logging, the competitor that shipped it, and the best-of-breed parse moment

**Date:** 2026-10-23 · **Issue:** [CUL-2162](https://linear.app/projectnyx/issue/CUL-2162) · **Prepared for:** the assistant discovery (jot → record, behaviour notes, what else an assistant helps with, whether it absorbs Ask)
**Method:** three isolated research lanes run the same session (no app installed, every claim tagged VERIFIED / SECONDARY / UNVERIFIED with a link), each lane's load-bearing claims then handed to an adversarial verifier that fetched the cited source and tried to refute it (§V). Lane A: about 30 fetches, nine searches. Lane B: about 45 searches, 20 primary fetches, the iTunes Search and Lookup APIs over about 95 listings. Lane C: about 70 fetches and searches.
**Delta to:** `2026-07-ask-ai-ux-landscape.md` (the twelve ask-AI patterns; not repeated), `2026-08-home-screen-competitive-teardown.md`, `2026-09-medication-competitive-landscape.md`, `docs/culprit-competitive-landscape-2026-10.md` (which already names ThePawcess as a web-only $39-per-trial entrant with an AI-written report).
**Status:** point-in-time evidence capture, frozen. Each lane closes with the synthesis it offered the discovery; decisions live on CUL-2162 and in whatever spec follows, never here (the research-folder contract).

**The state of the art in one paragraph.** Free-text-to-record logging has arrived in the pet category, but only at the zero-rating edge: ThePawcess (web only, dogs only, about four months old, $39 per trial, the log locked after three free days) markets "type 'gave a dental chew, itchier tonight' and AI turns it into a structured entry"; VetGPT, Moomoo AI and XiaoBai ship typed-or-spoken notes becoming structured logs with 30, 0 and 0 ratings. The scaled players (Fi, Tractive, Whisker, PetDesk) read sensors and uploaded documents and answer questions; none takes an owner's observation into the record. Nobody in the pet category parses behaviour words, shows the parse as a reversible card with the time and the pet resolved, parses a note against a trial or a course, or states what a single worrying parsed note does. Outside pets, the strongest products (Linear's agent and Triage Intelligence, Apple's Writing Tools and Journal, Fantastical, Todoist, Granola) converge on one shape: the raw words are kept, the structure is a proposal shown with its provenance, the user corrects per field and commits, and automation is earned per field. The documented failures are the same everywhere: invented numbers, silently dropped modifiers, a note lost when the parse fails, a streak beside a symptom, reassurance from one sample, and AI output that looks like the record.

### 5. Read against Culprit's rules

**The three pointed questions.**
- *Does "SLIP FLAGGED" + "Itch severity 3/5 ▲" reassure or escalate on one sample?* The card does neither: a neutral record with a rising arrow and no verdict. The reassurance sits one layer up: slip triage says "breathe" on a single slip, and reintroduction calls a protein "safe for life" after one 7-day challenge without a reaction. The second is the absence-≠-wellness error **n=1 never reassures** forbids; the first is trial-integrity advice the owner will hear as "the dog is fine" [V].
- *Does the streak celebrate a symptom?* It celebrates logging, at equal weight beside "Slip flagged" and a rising itch; the pill is content-blind. Under Principles 3 (safety leads) and 4 (warm, not nagging) that is a celebration beat on a bad day, and a streak punishes the missed day Principle 2 forgives [V].
- *Is exposure → itch stated as cause?* Not on the card; the report says "suspected", "within 3 days", "CORRELATION ONLY". The marketing says "a returning symptom points to a single, named cause" and flare forensics "names the suspect" [V]. The hedge is in the PDF; the cause is in the pitch.
- *Intake is not preference*: moot; no intake, appetite or refusal channel exists, so a decline routes nowhere [V, absence].

**Strengths.** A complete loop: audit → log → slip triage → reintroduction → report. "Same as yesterday" is Principle 2 done right. Sentence → structured entry is a real friction cut. The fridge card targets the failure owners hit ("someone else's kindness"). Per-food label memory, the 1–7 day lookback and tiered emergency copy are honest. One-time pricing fits a bounded job. The privacy page is candid (names its AI broker).

**Weaknesses.** Care is paid: logging locks after three days, the inverse of Pets > $. Dog and web only; no offline, widget or native notification evident. No parse correction, ambiguity handling or confidence on the entry. "96% compliance" is days logged, coverage printed as adherence (Culprit's own version: [CUL-1779](https://linear.app/projectnyx/issue/CUL-1779), [CUL-1780](https://linear.app/projectnyx/issue/CUL-1780)). Contradictory slip semantics. "Safe for life" on n=1. Affiliate prescription-diet picks under "not medical advice". No named vet, legal entity or user anyone else has seen; one founder; an unnamed study behind the dose math.

### 6. Take, avoid, open

**Take.** (1) A sentence-in, structured-entry-out path for `app/log.tsx`, with "same as yesterday" as a first-class confirm and the thing they lack: an editable parse shown before save. (2) A read-only "is this on the trial?" caregiver surface over `diet_trial_foods`, revocable token, through `rls-privacy-reviewer`. (3) A pre-trial cupboard audit over the food cache and extractor. (4) The "1–7 days after the cause" lookback copy and a "Label read · N ingredients" transparency line.

**Avoid.** A streak pill on the same card as a symptom. Any "breathe" or "safe for life" verdict on one sample. Coverage printed as compliance. "Named cause" copy outside a finding with its counts. Affiliate food picks. A model-written report. Locking the log behind a paywall.

**Open questions.** (1) Which homepage the PM saw: `/how-it-works` copy promoted to a browser-only redesign, or an A/B variant? (2) Whether an owner can edit the parsed entry, and what the log does with an ambiguous note; only the $39 hands-on ([CUL-1777](https://linear.app/projectnyx/issue/CUL-1777)) answers it. (3) What the Household Guard link exposes and whether it is revocable, given the dog's identity and exclusion list sit on a public route.

### 2. Named patterns

1. **Draft → preview → save.** Moomoo "drafts… for you to preview before saving"; Cronometer's "Match Foods"; MyFitnessPal's reviewable matches; Sympta's pre-filled check-in; WHOOP's behaviour review. *Culprit:* the parse is a proposal rendered as the confirm cards the log sheet already uses (Principle 2), never a silent write.
2. **A receipt you can undo.** VetGPT: "Every save shows a receipt you can undo"; Cronometer flags Siri-logged rows for review; WHOOP edits any past day. *Culprit:* every assistant write lands marked "from your note" and reversible (C-20).
3. **The note survives the parse.** Health Diary's fix note; Digitail's diary stays free text; Memex keeps and searches the sentence (SECONDARY [Memex](https://www.memexlab.ai/blog/pet-journal-app)). *Culprit:* the note is the record, the structure derived; a failed or declined parse still saves the note, and the vet report can quote it.
4. **One bar, two verbs.** Guava's one "Ask Guava" bar logs, searches and analyses; WHOOP's W icon takes "habits, symptoms, emotions" and questions alike; VetGPT: "Start with a question, not a form." *Culprit:* absorbing Ask into the assistant is the field's majority shape, viable only if the write path (confirm card) and the read path (answer card) stay visibly different classes (C-33) and Ask's caps stay intact.
5. **A bounded schema, taught by example.** CareMind extracts exactly six fields; Guava's prompt text is literal examples; Lose It quotes a sentence. *Culprit:* parse targets are the existing taxonomy (event types, look words, foods, meds); an unknown becomes a look word or a question, never a new type (W2a/W2b stay unbuildable); the empty state teaches the grammar by example (Principle 5).
6. **One follow-up, then a check-back.** Voyage "asks the follow-up question a vet would ask before it answers" (VERIFIED [listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/voyage-ai-vet-for-dogs-cats/id6748588390)); VetGPT "offers to check back in a few days when something needs watching." *Culprit:* at most one clarifying question in the confirm card; a watch-item becomes a Signal card, not a chat thread.
7. **Observation, not conclusion.** PetNid: "Every AI insight is framed as an observation, not a conclusion"; HealthPilot: "for reflection… does not… establish cause and effect"; Fi: "It doesn't diagnose." *Culprit:* the house rule already; confirm copy stays descriptive, and one note may escalate on a red flag but never reassure (n=1).
8. **Anchor the note on the trace.** Maven's Context Markers put logged symptoms beneath the sensor charts. *Culprit:* a parsed note's time anchor goes onto the day ledger and the chart row (Principle 8), which makes "dental chew 3pm → itchier tonight" readable.

### 3. Anti-pattern casebook (AI logging and journaling specifically)

- **Silent modifier drop, low-confidence defaults.** Nutrola's March 2026 test (250 phrasings per app; competitor-run, arithmetic not fully reconciled): MyFitnessPal "defaults to the top user-entered match" and dropped modifiers "silently… in 63 of 100 modified phrasings"; Lose It returned "no matches" for typed free text. SECONDARY [Nutrola](https://nutrola.app/en/blog/same-meal-10-phrasings-nlp-robustness-data-report-2026). A pet record reading "no vomiting today" as a vomit event is a clinical error.
- **The note lost when the parse fails.** Health Diary 1.1.4 implies earlier builds discarded the owner's words when analysis was unavailable. VERIFIED [listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/health-diary-body-notes/id6755714195).
- **Conversational capture interjects.** Millard et al., JMIR mHealth 2023: 60.1% (357/588) of Alexa food entries matched the web diary; Alexa "interjected" when participants paused; the authors ask for less conversational interfaces. VERIFIED [JMIR](https://mhealth.jmir.org/2023/1/e41117/).
- **Auto-tagging the owner's words drew privacy pushback.** Pixel Journal's Mood ID infers an emoji with on-device Gemini Nano that also reads Health Connect, Photos, Calendar and location; a reviewer was "unsure how they felt about AI reading their thoughts." SECONDARY [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/apple-journal-vs-pixel-journal-3591852/); a cited Verge critique is UNVERIFIED [techbuzz](https://www.techbuzz.ai/articles/google-journal-ai-features-spark-privacy-backlash).
- **Consent retrofitted after launch.** Sympta 1.4.23: voice entries and AI summaries "now each ask for your permission." VERIFIED [listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/symptom-tracker-sympta/id6758482143). The assistant needs its own consent row and matching policy text (5.1.2(i), CUL-1836, CUL-1769).
- **Output at the wrong moment.** Tractive: "both alerts came while the pet was at the vet." SECONDARY [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tractive.com).
- **Date framing in a journal.** WHOOP members: "today" conflicts with logging yesterday; night questions sit under daytime. VERIFIED [1](https://www.community.whoop.com/t/just-got-a-new-whoop-band-life-the-journal-is-very-confusing/3223), [2](https://www.community.whoop.com/t/feature-request-smarter-whoop-journal-conditional-contextual-meds/13181). "Around 3pm" and "tonight" need a resolved timestamp in the confirm card (C-10, C-19).
- **Vision estimates read as ground truth.** NIH metabolic-kitchen test (NUTRITION 2026, preliminary): four photo apps under-counted by 250–345 kcal a meal. SECONDARY [HealthDay](https://www.healthday.com/health-news/nutrition/how-accurate-are-photo-based-calorie-apps-4-are-put-to-the-test). Photo→protein keeps the same confirm.
- **Reassurance as a feature.** Furbo's Health Checker is sold for "suggesting home remedies when visiting the vet is not an immediate option" (VERIFIED [Furbo](https://furbo.com/blog/en/article/ai-powered-furbo-nanny-updates)); Vetify promises "INSTANT AI DIAGNOSIS" and to "avoid unnecessary clinic visits" (VERIFIED [listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vetify-pet-symptom-checker/id6761506524)). Both are the n=1-reassures failure the house rule forbids (Ollie's "Looks Great!" is in the October brief); sycophancy compounds it: Cheng et al., *Science* 2026, AI affirmed users' actions 49% more often than humans (SECONDARY [Georgetown](https://www.law.georgetown.edu/tech-institute/insights/ai-sycophancy-impacts-harms-questions/)).

### 4. The whitespace, stated carefully

Verified absent across ~95 listings on 2026-10-22/23; the shelf adds ~140 pet-health listings a quarter, so "nobody" expires in weeks.

1. **No pet app with traction takes an owner's typed or spoken observation into the record.** The three that do (VetGPT 30 ratings, Moomoo and XiaoBai 0) are months old; the scaled players (Fi, Tractive, Whisker, PetDesk, Chewy: 42K–1.2M ratings) read sensors or documents and answer questions; Digitail's intake is clinic-gated and pre-visit.
2. **Nobody in the pet category parses behaviour words.** VetGPT, XiaoBai and PetCents name symptoms; Moomoo adds mood and energy fields; none describes "hid under the bed" becoming a vocabulary term. Human apps do it only for emotion.
3. **Nobody shows the parse as a countable, reversible card with time and pet resolved.** VetGPT has receipt and undo, Moomoo the preview; no one has both plus timestamp and pet (C-9).
4. **Nobody parses a note against a diet-trial object** (off-diet food in prose → coverage) **or a medication course** (a dose in prose → "Dose X of Y").
5. **Nobody states what a single worrying parsed note does.** Disclaimers are generic; a documented asymmetry (escalate on a red flag, never reassure on its absence) would be a first in either category.

## Lane C — Best-of-breed outside the pet category: the Linear agent, the parse moment, motion, trust

**Date:** 2026-10-23 · **Lens:** Sr. Product Designer, isolated · **Method:** ~70 fetches/searches, primary pages first. Does not repeat the 12 patterns in `docs/research/2026-07-ask-ai-ux-landscape.md`.
**Tags:** **V** = VERIFIED (primary page read) · **S** = SECONDARY (third-party, or quoted via search) · **U** = UNVERIFIED.
**Read against:** Principle 9 — motion is the record moving, chrome is still, the one loop is the tick (3pt, 1.4s, only while a request is in flight, becomes the rail of what it waited for); Reduce Motion's three-way rule.

### 1. The Linear agent — ten mechanics, and what transfers

1. **Delegate, never assignee.** "issues can only be assigned to humans, and only delegated to agents"; "an agent cannot be held accountable"; "Delegate issues, but not accountability." **V** [SDK blog](https://linear.app/blog/our-approach-to-building-the-agent-interaction-sdk), [linear.app/agents](https://linear.app/agents). *Transfers:* every record carries the owner as author; the AI proposes; the vet report never shows an AI-authored event. *Does not:* the two-role field.
2. **Invoked where the work is.** Assign, @mention in a comment, or `Cmd/Ctrl+J`; "Sessions are created automatically when an agent is mentioned or delegated an issue." **V** [docs](https://linear.app/docs/agents-in-linear), [Introducing Linear Agent](https://linear.app/changelog/2026-03-24-introducing-linear-agent), [dev docs](https://linear.app/developers/agents). *Transfers:* the jot box lives on the day it writes into, not a global bot tab. *Does not:* @-grammar.
3. **Named session states:** `pending · active · awaitingInput · error · complete · stale`, tracked "automatically based on the last emitted activity." **V** [agent-interaction](https://linear.app/developers/agent-interaction). *Transfers:* a parse is a stateful object, not a spinner; *awaiting input* is a first-class state. *Does not:* the 30-minute stale window.
4. **A latency contract.** First activity "within 10 seconds to avoid the session being marked as unresponsive"; "respond immediately with a `thought` activity." **V** [agent-interaction](https://linear.app/developers/agent-interaction), [best practices](https://linear.app/developers/agent-best-practices). *Transfers:* acknowledge the jot the instant it lands (saved, tick running, request named).
5. **Ephemeral status, replaced in place.** `thought`/`action` can be ephemeral, "displayed temporarily" and replaced by the next; `response`, `elicitation`, `error` each become a comment. **V** [agent-interaction](https://linear.app/developers/agent-interaction). *Transfers:* one status line that swaps, never a growing log. *Does not:* showing "thoughts"; the records *are* the response.
6. **Elicitation is a typed question**; "An agent cannot generate a `prompt` type activity." **V** [agent-interaction](https://linear.app/developers/agent-interaction). *Transfers:* one ambiguity → one question as chips ("3pm today / yesterday?"), answered inside the preview. Never a free-text follow-up.
7. **Contextual confirmation gates.** "pause mid-run and ask the user for confirmation before proceeding" on risky or hard-to-undo actions; may delete what it created in this conversation unasked, must confirm deleting an existing issue; "shouldn't take actions that substantially expand the scope of a user's request without requesting confirmation"; refusal preferred to error. **V** [How we built Linear Agent](https://linear.app/blog/how-we-built-linear-agent). *Transfers* verbatim: new records it just proposed → one commit; any edit to an existing record → confirm; never more records than the jot named; unsure → ask or decline.
8. **Draft is the default output.** Project updates: "writes an update draft for you to refine… then publish" **V** [changelog](https://linear.app/changelog/2026-06-18-agent-assisted-project-updates); coding sessions "return a new diff for review", iterated "in the same thread" **V** [2026-06-11 via releases.sh](https://releases.sh/release/rel_5RqWh_1vr6Obnw-PWhQdb-coding-sessions-now-available-with-linear-agent); steer by tapping a diff line to quote it **V** [mobile](https://linear.app/changelog/2026-07-30-coding-sessions-on-mobile). *Transfers:* the parse is a diff against the day; tapping a field addresses the correction to that field.
9. **Triage Intelligence — the closest analogue.** "Accept, decline, or view more information about why"; per property "appear, be hidden, or be auto-applied"; "Hovering over a suggestion reveals the model's reasoning in plain language"; alternatives shown; a "thinking state" with a timer; "you always know what came from the system and what came from your team"; "Automation will come gradually and with control." **V** [docs](https://linear.app/docs/triage-intelligence), [blog](https://linear.app/blog/how-we-built-triage-intelligence). *Transfers:* per-field chips with why-on-tap; typed vs inferred provenance; auto-apply earned per field. *Does not:* team guidance text; the module's density.
10. **Quiet chrome, earned attention.** "Don't compete for attention you haven't earned"; "Structure should be felt not seen"; the 2024 redesign set out to "reduce visual noise… increase the hierarchy and density"; Method: "Aim for clarity… Don't invent terms", "Simple first, then powerful." **V** [refresh](https://linear.app/now/behind-the-latest-design-refresh), [redesign](https://linear.app/blog/how-we-redesigned-the-linear-ui), [Method](https://linear.app/method/introduction). *Transfers* wholesale. Linear publishes no motion durations; the speed/keyboard lore is **S** [Sequoia](https://sequoiacap.com/article/linear-spotlight/).

**Failure:** `error` is a comment; "stale" recovers on the next activity **V** [best practices](https://linear.app/developers/agent-best-practices). *Transfers:* a failed parse leaves the jot saved and visibly unstructured. **Not found:** any doc describing a *stop* control (§5).

### 3. Motion and micro-interaction references worth copying

No source publishes durations (**U** for every ms value); sequences and what-moves/what-stays are quoted.

1. **Materialise, don't fade.** "Instead of fading, Liquid Glass objects materialize in and out by gradually modulating the light bending and lensing." **V** [WWDC25 219](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/219/). *Copy as:* the record card resolves soft → sharp in place (blur → 0); nothing translates. Reduce Motion: Apple "decreases the intensity of some effects and disables any elastic properties" — the crossfade-in-place leg.
2. **The menu pops from the trigger.** "the bubble simply pops open to reveal the content… keeps everything right where you just tapped." **V** (same). *Copy as:* the preview grows from the jot field's bottom edge; a question chip row pops from the field it questions.
3. **One element morphs on one plane.** "Liquid Glass dynamically morphs between the controls in each context… a singular floating plane." **V** (same). *Copy as:* composer and record card are one element with one settle, never a dismiss plus a new card.
4. **Chrome yields to the record.** "when users scroll, tab bars shrink… The moment users scroll back up, tab bars fluidly expand." **V** [Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/06/apple-introduces-a-delightful-and-elegant-new-software-design/). *Copy as:* the composer collapses to a pill while the owner reads the proposal.
5. **Processing shown on the text, not the chrome.** Writing Tools: chunked delivery, "animations to the text being processed to indicate the model is working." **V** [WWDC24](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2024/10168/). *Copy as:* the tick runs along the jot's left edge while the parse is in flight and becomes the rail of the first record it produced.
6. **Status line with an honest clock.** "the model's 'thinking state' along with a timer" at the module's foot; ephemeral activities replaced by the next. **V** [blog](https://linear.app/blog/how-we-built-triage-intelligence). *Copy as:* one secondary-ink line under the tick ("Reading your note · 2s"), crossfaded on change, never stacked.
7. **Why on tap, alternatives beside.** **V** (same). *Copy as:* long-press a field chip → the phrase that produced it lights up in the raw jot above (Granola's magnifying glass, inverted).

### 4. Trust evidence, and the recommendation

**AI output that looks like the record is the failure mode.** Apple paused News & Entertainment summaries in iOS 18.3 after the BBC complaint; the fix was *visual provenance*: "Updated style for summarized notifications better distinguishes them from other notifications" (italics plus glyph). **V** [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/01/27/apple-releases-iOS-18-3), **S** [Mediaite](https://www.mediaite.com/media/news/apple-suspends-ai-news-alerts-after-false-bbc-story-claimed-luigi-mangione-shot-himself).

**Transcription invents, more so around silence.** Whisper: "roughly 1% of audio transcriptions contained entire hallucinated phrases or sentences"; "38% of hallucinations include explicit harms"; worse with "longer shares of non-vocal durations." **V** [Koenecke et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.08021). An invented drug, "hyperactivated antibiotics." **S** [AP](https://www.adn.com/nation-world/2024/10/26/researchers-say-an-ai-powered-transcription-tool-used-in-hospitals-invents-things-no-one-ever-said/). Notion: "Sometimes a transcript includes words that weren't said." **V** [Notion](https://www.notion.com/help/ai-meeting-notes).

**Structuring errs by omission first.** Ambient clinical scribes, 356 notes / 31 physicians: omissions 18%, hallucinations 11.5%, accidental inclusions 9.3%, 2.5% serious risk; clinician review "remains necessary." **S** [JMIR preprint](https://preprints.jmir.org/preprint/85015/accepted). An adversarial audit found ~1 in 3 notes with verified errors. **S** [arXiv 2608.31017](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.31017). Consumer scale: Granola wrote $312.1M for $312,000 and dropped a digit from a code; with no audio "it removes your only way to check." **S** [HappyScribe](https://www.happyscribe.com/blog/granola-ai-review). Fantastical strips "evening" and sets 5pm; a venue named "Friday" moves the date. **S** [MacRumors forum](https://spy.macrumors.com/threads/problems-with-fantastical-calendar.2176681/).

**Hands-busy voice.** Parakeet (n=4): WER 16% seated indoors, 25% walking outdoors; *after user correction* 1% / 2%; 18 vs 13 WPM; when the confusion net could fix an error users used it 96% of the time. **V** [paper](https://www.keithv.com/pub/demoparakeet/demoparakeet.pdf). Dictating while driving: ~6× the errors of templated voice search. **S** [Ju & Paek](https://preview.aclanthology.org/author-url/P10-2058.pdf). Lesson: accuracy comes from the correction interface, not the recogniser.

**Suggestions are rejected often even where loved.** Copilot acceptance ≈30%. **S** [ITPro](https://itpro.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/github-30-of-copilot-coding-suggestions-are-accepted). Expect a third of fields to be touched.

**Auto-apply has a documented tail.** Google narrowed Gmail→Calendar auto-add to "When I respond to the invitation in email" after spam events. **S** [Android Police](https://www.androidpolice.com/latest-google-calendar-update-makes-ignoring-unwanted-event-invites-easier/). Automation-bias literature separates *commission* (rubber-stamping) from *omission* errors. **S** [Aether review](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Aether-Overreliance-on-AI-Review-Final-6.21.22.pdf), [UXmatters](https://www.uxmatters.com/mt/archives/2026/06/designing-for-doubt-how-to-prevent-automation-complacency-in-ai-workflows.php). HAX G8 "Support efficient dismissal", G9 "Support efficient correction", G10 "Scope services when in doubt", G11 "Make clear why." **V** [Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction-design/). Linear: "you need to see where they came from and believe in their accuracy"; automation "gradually and with control", opt-in per type. **V** [blog](https://linear.app/blog/how-we-built-triage-intelligence). **Gap:** no controlled study comparing confirm-first with auto-apply on correction rates.

#### Recommendation — save the words now, propose the records, commit on one tap

- **The raw jot is written instantly**, verbatim, as a note on the day: the write-then-correct half, and Principle 1. It never disappears, even when structuring fails.
- **Structured records are a proposal until one commit.** Anything that becomes evidence — symptom, dose, food, time — is confirm-before-write. One tap over a preview the owner reads anyway; the alternative puts an invented symptom into a diet-trial record and the vet report, and an *omitted* symptom reassures by silence (n=1 never reassures). Omission being the commonest scribe error, the preview also shows what it did *not* structure.
- **Per-field auto-apply, earned** (Linear's model): time = "now" or pet = the only pet may graduate when their correction rate nears zero; severity, symptom type and medication never do.
- **Provenance in ink** (typed black, inferred grey), **why-on-tap**, **token un-parse**, **one question as chips**, and **a failed parse is a saved note**, not a toast.
