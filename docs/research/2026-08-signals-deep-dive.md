## §0 Why now, and what question this answers

Step 10 (`generate-signal`) is live: seven deterministic detectors, a reflection layer, coverage diagnostics, and per-incident red-flag surfacing, all behind clinically-conservative floors, with the Haiku layer phrasing only. The Signal/Home design uplift (B-721) is build-ready, which makes *what the cards can say* the next frontier — and three prior research briefs (2026-06 Opus PoC, Fable rerun, vet council) each found the same shape of gap: the engine is rigorous per-lane but blind across lanes, and the strongest clinical signals in the dogfood record were the ones no detector owned. Two of those (worsening ④, time-of-day ⑥) have since shipped; chronicity ⑦ shipped and is now the top live card.

The question this brief answers: **with the engine as-built and three more months of live record, where does the evidence say the next unit of engineering effort buys the most honest clinical signal?** Answered from four directions: what the engine can already say (§1), what the live record contains that it cannot yet say (§2), what methods exist that stay honest at this data scale (§3–§4), and what everyone else does (§5–§6).

## §1 The engine as-built (verified against source, 2026-08-13)

Seven detectors plus three auxiliary layers, composed in `detectSignals` (`supabase/functions/generate-signal/detection.ts`, ~4,700 lines; 829 Deno test cases enforced in CI). All floors below are the shipped `DEFAULT_CONFIG` values.

| # | Detector | Core mechanism | Key floors (shipped values) |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Food↔symptom correlation | Case/control matched-pair design per protein; per-symptom attribution windows (vomit 12h, diarrhea 24h, itch/scratch/skin 72h); episodes collapse at 3h gaps | Early: ≥3 matched pairs, ≥2 case-only discordant, risk difference ≥0.2. Established: ≥5 pairs + familywise α=0.05 (Bonferroni across the candidate family) |
| ② | Intake decline | Per-food rated-meal baseline → consecutive-days-below | ≥4 rated meals for baseline over 14d; 2 consecutive days below (cats: 1 day at concern ceiling); refusal recency 2d |
| ③ | Reflection (descriptive trend) | Week-over-week episode counts, `improving`/`flat` only | ≥3 episodes in the busier window; ≥3 actively-logged days in BOTH windows (a logging gap can't masquerade as improvement); worsening NEVER stated here (suppressed → owned by ④) |
| ④ | Worsening (safety) | Fires on the exact predicate ③ suppresses (shared `isWorsening` — the "one-way valve into silence" fix from the Fable brief) | worseningMinEpisodes 2; firm tier at symptoms on ≥4 of 7 days (density-anchored, not raw count) |
| ⑤ | Post-prandial timing | Fraction of confidence-eligible vomit episodes ≤30 min after a feeding, with a grazing guard (observed rapid must be ≥2× chance-expected given the pet's feeding rate) | ≥3 rapid, ≥6 eligible, fraction ≥0.25, recency ≤14d, 60d window. Vomit only |
| ⑥ | Time-of-day clustering | 4h sliding clock window over episode onsets; calibrated against the 24-position multiple-comparison problem | ≥6 eligible, ≥5 in cluster, fraction ≥0.6 (calibrated up from spec's 4/0.5, which fired at ~21.6% on uniform-random onsets; shipped floors ≈3.3% pooled) |
| ⑦ | Chronicity (safety) | Span + sustained burden + distribution + still-ongoing, orthogonal to ④'s week-over-week delta | 56d window; span ≥21d; ≥6 episodes (raised from spec's 4 after a ~9.9% noise-gate failure; 6 → ~1.3%); ≥3 active weekly buckets; episode within 14d. Firm tier at span ≥42d |

Auxiliary layers: **coverage diagnostics** (B-053: explanation-only cards for reduced engine power — sparse logging, single-protein diets; fully suppressed on trial pets); **per-incident red flags** (deterministic surfacing of `event_ai_analysis` blood/foreign-material findings — the only place the engine touches the photo-AI's structured output); **priority classes** (safety > insight > reflection; safety findings never dropped for layout).

Composition rules that matter for §2:
- **⑤ suppresses ⑥ for the same symptom** (`suppressTimeOfDayWhenPostprandial`, §4.4) — rationale: a pet fed on a schedule that vomits post-prandially clusters by clock trivially, so the clock card would restate the meal-adjacency card.
- **⑦ suppresses ④ same-symptom with tier inheritance** (chronicity owns the course; worsening owns the delta).
- **Medication confounding is identity-agnostic by design** (`detection.ts:335`): ANY drug on board during a symptom's matched pairs suppresses (case-enriched) or Early-caps (balanced) that correlation. `medication_item_id` is carried for audit only; route is not read.
- **Trial pets:** correlations lead (band 1) and staple washout is disabled for the trial staple; coverage diagnostics fully suppressed.
- **Soft-deleted rows are invisible** to every query (`.is('deleted_at', null)`, enforced by `detectionSoftDelete.test.ts` reading the function source in CI — B-071's contract).

Everything phrases as counts; the Haiku layer rephrases but cannot add claims; templates are the no-API-key fallback. The n=1/no-reassurance invariants hold at every layer (verified in the G2 "two-sided rule" form: no negative claims about the record at any coverage).

## §2 The live record (production SQL audit, 2026-08-13)

Four pets. **One is real: Nyx** (cat, the dogfood subject of all three prior briefs). **Cooper (dog, added 2026-07-22) is a demo account — synthetic data populated for App Store review** (PM correction, 2026-08-14; v1 of this brief misread him as the first real typical-density logger). All queries filtered `deleted_at IS NULL` exactly as the engine does, except F6 which audits the deletions themselves. Owner timezone `America/Chicago` (all local-time claims below use it).

**The headline numbers (Nyx, live rows):** 831 events — 731 meals, 37 medication doses, 36 vomits, 23 `other`, 3 itch, 1 lethargy. Zero stool events, zero weight checks. Meal density 60–80/week through 2026-07-20, halving to 29–40/week after — which F5 resolves into a *feeding-structure* change, not logging fatigue. Intake ratings: 431 `all` / 28 `most` / 36 `some` / 21 `picked` / 11 `refused` / 204 unrated (72% rated). Active elimination trial: Royal Canin Selected Protein PR (rabbit), started 2026-07-26, 56-day target; trial-era adherence 87 rabbit meals vs 2 chicken.

What the engine currently shows for Nyx (cache read 2026-08-13): two cards — ⑦ chronicity, firm tier ("vomiting across 6 of the last 8 weeks — 18 episodes since June… worth booking a vet visit") and ⑤ post-prandial ("7 of the 10 vomiting episodes we could time happened within 30 minutes of eating, including the last two"; median 5 minutes). No correlation card (chicken staple washes out pre-trial; single-protein diet in-trial), no coverage diagnostics (trial suppression).

### F1 — The vomit record decomposes into two phenotypes, and the engine can only speak about one of them

All 36 live vomits, bucketed by time-since-last-meal (24h lookback, raw rows — note this denominator differs from ⑤'s confidence-filtered episode set):

| Phenotype | n | Local-time shape | Photo-AI content (completed reads) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rapid post-prandial (≤30 min) | 14 | Any hour | Undigested/partially-digested food; hair in several; one bile+liquid |
| Mid (30 min–4h) | 9 | Mixed | Mixed, incl. hair, foam |
| **Empty-stomach (≥4h since food)** | **10** | **Almost all 2–8am** | **Bile + partially-digested food — i.e. food still present ≥4–7h post-meal** |
| No meal logged within 24h | 3 | Early morning | Sparse |

17 of 36 vomits (47%) fall in the 2–8am band — a quarter of the clock. The empty-stomach cluster is the classic bilious/overnight phenotype the vet-council brief hypothesized from 21 episodes; at 36 it is unambiguous, and the photo-AI reads corroborate it (bile present, and — notable in its own right — *partially-digested food retained ≥4h after the last meal*, a gastric-emptying observation).

The engine's composition hides this: **⑤ fired, so ⑥ is suppressed for vomit** (`suppressTimeOfDayWhenPostprandial`). The suppression rationale assumes the clock cluster *restates* the meal-adjacency cluster — true for a schedule-fed pet with one phenotype, false here, where the 2–8am cluster is composed of *different episodes* than the ≤30-min set. A mixed-phenotype pet loses its second signal exactly when having two phenotypes is the clinically-interesting fact (the vet council: bile-timing ≠ overnight-timing → ≥2 overlapping processes). The suppression is phenotype-blind where it needs to be episode-set-aware.

Corollary: no detector reads *time-since-last-meal* as a first-class dimension. ⑤ computes it and then keeps only the ≤30-min tail. The ≥4h tail — the empty-stomach signature — is computed, then discarded.

### F2 — The recent era is a phenotype shift the engine has no lane for

*(Nyx-only, like F1 — unaffected by the Cooper correction.)* Since 2026-07-09, 9 of 10 vomits are ≤10 minutes post-meal (the tenth: 257 min). Before July, the record mixes both phenotypes roughly evenly. The dominant phenotype *changed* mid-record — from mixed/empty-stomach toward almost-pure rapid — and ⑤'s 60-day pooled fraction (7/10) understates what the last five weeks look like (9/10). Nothing in the engine states composition change over time; ④ would catch a *count* worsening, not a *character* shift at flat counts. F5's re-run sharpens this: the shift's back half is the trial era, in which the empty-stomach phenotype stops entirely.

### F3 — A six-week respiratory course is invisible because it lives in `other` notes

Nyx's 23 `other` events + 3 itch events decode (from their free-text notes) as: **coughing ×14 (2026-07-01 → 2026-08-13, ongoing as of the audit date)**, sneezing ×7, ear-specific itching ×3 (concurrent with a treated ear infection, otic med 07-16 → 08-09), "not as playful" ×1. The owner adapted to a missing event type by typing into the note field — consistently enough that the course is fully reconstructible.

Checked against ⑦'s shipped floors as if `cough` were a detectable symptom type: span 43d ≥ 42 (firm), 14 episodes ≥ 6, 7 active weekly buckets ≥ 3, last episode = audit day. **It would fire the firm chronicity tier today.** It fires nothing, because `other` events are not fetched by the engine (`DETECTION_SYMPTOM_TYPES` = vomit/diarrhea/itch/scratch/skin_reaction) and free text is not a detection input under the current (correct) reading of the LLM boundary. A six-week ongoing cough in a cat is exactly the class of finding the chronicity lane was built to escalate — and in a cat, chronic cough is never-normal in the same register as the vomiting-frequency literature the lane already leans on (see §4).

The capture-taxonomy reading: detection scope is bounded by the event enum, the enum is bounded by what the log screen offers, and the highest-signal missing types are now *empirically named by the owner's own workaround* — cough and sneeze first among them.

### F5 — The trial era, re-run Nyx-only (rewritten 2026-08-14): a phenotype-specific response the pooled counts hide

v1 of this finding said the trial-era vomit rate was "flat" — a sloppy windows comparison, corrected here after the PM's lived observation ("I have felt like she's vomiting less") prompted a proper re-run. Nyx-only, seven pre-trial weeks (2026-06-07 → 07-25, 50 logged days) against the trial era (07-26 → 08-13, 19 logged days):

| | Pre-trial (7 wks) | Trial era (19 days) |
|---|---|---|
| Vomits | 20 (≈2.8/wk) | 4 (≈1.5/wk) |
| — rapid (≤30 min post-meal) | 8 (≈1.1/wk) | 4 (≈1.5/wk) — all 4, each ≤5 min |
| — **empty-stomach (≥4h)** | **7 (≈1/wk)** | **0** |
| Treat feedings | 369 of 461 (80%) | 4 of 89 (4.5%) |
| Non-treat meals/day | 1.8 | 4.5 |

Four things changed together at trial start, and one didn't:
1. **The empty-stomach phenotype stopped.** 7 episodes in 7 pre-trial weeks; zero in 19 trial days. The current 21+-day empty-stomach-free run is the longest in the record (previous max gap: 18 days). At the pre-trial rate, 0-in-19-days has one-sided p≈0.07 (Poisson) — *suggestive, not conclusive*, stated per §3's honesty rules.
2. **The pooled rate roughly halved** (2.8→1.5/wk) — but the §3 C-test on the totals (20 events/50 days vs 4/19) gives one-sided p≈0.17: **not yet distinguishable from natural variability.** The PM's felt improvement is real in the record and not yet statistically bankable — both true at once, which is exactly the sentence the engine should be able to say.
3. **The diet context transformed**: treats collapsed 80%→4.5% of feedings, real meals per day went 1.8→4.5, and the protein changed — three simultaneous changes, so attribution is impossible by construction (the §3 RTM + confound literature applies in full).
4. **A mechanism note the vet would want**: at 4.5 real meals/day (including near-daily evening meals), the long-fasted overnight state that defines the empty-stomach phenotype is itself rarer — the cessation may be a *feeding-structure* response (the classic management response in the §4 bilious literature) rather than a protein response. The engine states the counts and the structure change; the vet interprets.
5. **The rapid phenotype persists unchanged** (all four trial-era vomits ≤5 min post-meal) — consistent with a mechanical scarf-and-barf process indifferent to protein identity.

The product lesson is the sharpest in this brief: **today's live surface shows the pooled chronicity card ("18 episodes across 6 of 8 weeks — worth booking a vet visit") while the decomposed record shows one phenotype stopped, treats collapsed, and meals doubled.** The owner's lived sense ("she's doing better") and the card's register ("book a vet visit") disagree *because the engine pools what the record separates* — the strongest single argument for C1 (phenotype lanes) and C3 (trial-relative context) now on file. Structural facts from v1 that stand: ① is silenced by the trial's own success (an adherent single-protein diet leaves nothing to pair — the detector the trial band promotes is the one the trial silences); no lane reads the `diet_trials` timeline against symptom trajectory; the §4 GI assessment points pass unmarked; and the meal-density drop that looked like logging fatigue is now legible as the feeding-structure change itself.

### F6 — The soft-delete under-count is real but historical, not ongoing

47 vomits soft-deleted vs 36 live — but 35 of the 47 deletions date to May 2026 (the import/duplicate cleanup era the Opus brief caught), 8 to June, 4 to July. The recent record is clean. The engine's hard `deleted_at IS NULL` contract remains correct; what the era argues for is nothing more than honesty in any surface that renders pre-June history.

### F7 — Structured photo-AI content and event timing are two rich sources that never compose

31 of 36 live vomits carry completed `event_ai_analysis` reads with structured fields (contents[], colour, bile/blood/foreign tristates, hair). The engine reads exactly two bits of this (blood, foreign material → red flags). Unread today: **bile presence** (corroborates F1's empty-stomach phenotype from a second, independent source), **hair** (the "hairballs" folk-attribution the vomiting-frequency literature warns against — countable, and cross-tabulable against timing), **undigested vs digested content × time-since-meal** (the retention/regurgitation axis — undigested food at ≥4h is a different clinical object than digested food at 4h, and the vomit-vs-regurgitation capture gap was a named vet-council finding). All deterministic joins over rows that already exist.

### F8 — Medication data is course-shaped and route-annotated, but confounding is route-blind

Nyx's otic (topical ear) medication course (07-16 → 08-09) overlapped five weeks of the vomit record. Under the identity-agnostic v1 rule any on-board drug caps food→vomit correlations at Early — including a topical ear drop with negligible GI plausibility. The `route` column exists on `medications` and the diet-trial work already ruled route-awareness in scope for trial contamination (C3: oral-route detection, zero new schema). The same distinction — *systemic/oral confounds GI; topical/otic does not* — is sitting unread in the confounder path. (The med-response direction — "did symptom rate change after the course started" — is a §7 candidate with its own literature in §3/§4.)

## §3 Statistical prior art & methods (research lane 1)

External literature sweep for methods that stay honest at Nyx's data scale — every method below is deterministic (closed-form or seeded), phrases naturally as counts, and carries its floor. Full agent sweep 2026-08-13; URLs inline.

**The two strongest architectural anchors are retrofits, not builds.** The engine's case/control day-pairing is an informal **case-crossover design** (Maclure 1991, Am J Epidemiol — transient exposure, abrupt outcome, self-matched control windows; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1985444/), and its condition-on-totals structure matches the **self-controlled case series** family (Whitaker & Farrington, Stat Med 2006, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16220518/), which controls all fixed per-pet confounders *by design*. Citing these costs no code and buys the correlation detector formal lineage — the vet report's methods footnote can name a 35-year-old epidemiological design instead of a house heuristic.

**Lowest-floor methods, in floor order:**

- **g-chart on inter-event gaps** (geometric-distribution rare-event control chart, standard in hospital infection/fall surveillance; https://blog.minitab.com/en/blog/monitoring-rare-events-with-g-charts). Monitors **days between episodes** instead of counts-per-window; informative from ~3–5 gaps; use when <20% of periods contain an event. *The only method in this sweep that speaks at the 4-vomits-in-2-weeks scale (§2 F4)* — and "the gaps between episodes have shortened: 21 → 9 → 4 days" is already a Nyx-voiced sentence.
- **Conditional binomial C-test / E-test** (Przyborowski & Wileński 1940; Krishnamoorthy & Thomson 2004, https://userweb.ucs.louisiana.edu/~kxk4695/JSPI-04.pdf): the exact two-window rate comparison — X₁|(X₁+X₂) is Binomial — for any "N episodes in this window vs M in that one." Closed-form binomial sums; discriminates from ~5 total events. The natural **shared internal primitive for every rate-vs-rate sentence** the engine ever emits (internally gating, externally still phrased as counts).
- **EARS C1/C2/C3** (CDC syndromic surveillance, built for short baselines when history doesn't exist: today's count vs a moving 7-period mean ± k·SD, C2 with a guard band; https://surveillance.r-forge.r-project.org/pkgdown/reference/earsC.html): floor = 7 baseline periods, ~30 lines of TypeScript. The citable "is this week anomalous vs the recent baseline" alarm. (Farrington-flexible — the national-surveillance standard — is *ruled out*: needs 3–5 years of baselines.)
- **Poisson CUSUM / EWMA on weekly counts** (floor ~2–4 baseline weeks): optimal for sustained-rate-shift onset; reset-on-alarm semantics fit escalate-only.
- **Tau-U** (Parker 2011, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21496513/) **+ WWC single-case design floors** (https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/docs/referenceresources/wwc_scd.pdf): rank-based phase contrast usable at ~4–5 points per phase; the What Works Clearinghouse standards — **≥5 data points per phase; ≥3 demonstrations of an effect for strong wording** — are the citable external floor for any diet/med phase comparison. Tau-U is literally a count of pairwise comparisons, so it phrases in Nyx's counts-only voice.
- **Kulldorff temporal scan** (window-agnostic cluster detection, multiplicity-corrected by construction): the generalization of ⑥'s calibration problem; seeded-Monte-Carlo or exact-DP variants exist.

**On N-of-1 formality:** the veterinary elimination-trial literature itself (Olivry & Mueller, Critically Appraised Topics, BMC Vet Res — https://bmcvetres.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12917-015-0541-3) defines the diagnostic standard as **remission → provocation relapse → re-remission** — an A-B-A single-case withdrawal design; >90% of food-reaction dogs remit by 8 weeks (the source already behind the 56-day skin default), and the companion CAT establishes that no in-vivo/in-vitro test can diagnose adverse food reaction — **the diary/trial IS the diagnostic gold standard**, which is the wedge's thesis stated by the clinical literature. Randomization tests (Edgington & Onghena) are mostly a *negative* result: min attainable p = 1/(possible assignments), so owner-driven unrandomized phase changes can never reach conventional significance at small n — itself a useful internal "must stay silent" gate. Segmented-regression ITS floors (≥8–12 points per segment) sit **above** Nyx's 3–6-weekly-bins scale, so the honest post-intervention output is a **level contrast with exact intervals** ("6 itch-days in the 3 weeks before the med; 2 in the 3 weeks after"), never a slope.

**Failure modes with citations (each one a guardrail the engine should encode):**
- **Within-person natural experiments are confounded by construction** — naturally comparable days are rare (median 1.5–2.3 similar days per month/year per factor; Houle & Turner, Headache 2013, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5421357/), and humans can't judge association even from clean calendars (Turner 2020). This is the literature that justifies computing associations *for* the owner, with matched-day pairing non-optional.
- **Expectation bias dies under blinding** — Marcus 1997's double-blind chocolate provocation (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9453274/): the believed trigger didn't survive blinding; premonitory craving explains the diary association as **reverse causation**. Pet analog: a pre-nauseous pet may seek or refuse specific foods, so the food logged just before a vomit can be *consequence*, not cause — a caveat any single-food short-lag low-n pattern should carry.
- **Multiplicity always finds a winner** — an IBS app pilot testing dozens of nutrients per person found "≥1 strong association" in 73% of participants (8 of 11 — a tiny pilot, and the exact per-person nutrient count sits in unverifiable supplementary material) after 2 weeks (Zia 2016) — directionally the external grounding for ①'s Bonferroni family and candidate caps, at pilot-grade strength.
- **Regression to the mean after owner-initiated interventions** — seizure-diary natural variability alone reproduces the historical placebo response (RR50 ~21%; Goldenholz, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5722663/), and owners act when things peak. **Any post-intervention improvement of ~20–30% is expected by RTM alone** — the statistical justification for never phrasing post-change improvement as caused, and the hard constraint on any trial-response lane (§7).
- **Logging gaps are MNAR and predictive** — mHealth compliance declines ~1–2%/week (7–10%/week in eventual dropouts), and in digital phenotyping, *missingness itself* was 2.1–2.8× more frequent pre-relapse (https://www.nature.com/articles/s41537-023-00332-5). Three deterministic moves follow: denominators are logged-days with density disclosed (the C5 rule, now with citations); run the surveillance detectors **on the logging stream itself** ("the record went quiet" as a first-class descriptive event); never let a gap render as a reassuring zero.

## §4 Veterinary clinical literature (research lane 2)

Clinical evidence for what a count-based engine may honestly say about the §2 record. Full agent sweep 2026-08-13; evidence grades noted inline (consensus > systematic review > case series > textbook > expert opinion); URLs inline.

**Chronic vomiting thresholds.** The load-bearing chain is Norsworthy's JAVMA pair (2013 n=100, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24171376/; 2015 n=300, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26331421/): in biopsy cohorts of chronically-vomiting cats, **96–99% had histologic small-bowel abnormality** (2013: 99/100; 2015: 288/300 = 96%, ~150 chronic enteritis / ~124 lymphoma; the oft-quoted 83% figure — 249/300 — is the *ultrasound segmental-thickening* rate, not histology; verification pass 2026-08-14) — "chronic vomiting in cats is a small bowel disease." The famous **"vomiting more than twice a month is not normal"** line is these authors' *practice threshold and expert opinion*, not a consensus statement; it was formally challenged (JAVMA letter, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26827382/), and **Marsilio et al. 2019** (JVIM, doi 10.1111/jvim.15455) found similar histologic findings in 19 of 20 *clinically healthy* cats. (Correction: v1 cited doi 10.1111/jvim.15524 as that study — 15524 is actually Norsworthy's *rebuttal letter* arguing those healthy cats weren't healthy; citing it as the supporting study inverted its meaning.) Cornell's line: a hairball once every week or two is unremarkable, but **vomiting more than once per week — or any frequency with lethargy, appetite change, blood, or weight loss — warrants prompt evaluation** (https://www.vet.cornell.edu/departments-centers-and-institutes/cornell-feline-health-center/health-information/feline-health-topics/vomiting). The engine's honest ceiling: "above the level feline specialists consider worth investigating" — frequency-vs-threshold, never probability-of-diagnosis (a naive engine reading "46% lymphoma" out of referral cohorts would terrify owners with a selection-biased number). **The timing decomposition itself (§2 F1) is exactly the history a workup needs** — timing/content features that move the differential are textbook-grade: undigested/partially-digested food vomited many hours post-meal suggests delayed gastric emptying or outflow obstruction (Merck); bile on an empty stomach in the early morning points at the bilious pattern; and **hair does not de-escalate** — Cannon (JFMS 2013, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23254238/): only ~10% of shorthaired cats regularly produce hairballs, and *frequent* hairballs are themselves "an indicator of an underlying chronic disease."

**Bilious vomiting syndrome — weak evidence, and a trap for a naive engine.** The classic picture (early-morning bile on a long-fasted stomach; first-line response = a late-evening meal) rests on textbook consensus plus **one retrospective case series of 20 dogs** (JAAHA 2016, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27008323/) in which 3 dogs were later reclassified — one as gastric adenocarcinoma. There is **no feline case series**; BVS is a *diagnosis of exclusion*. And the live record contradicts clean BVS anyway: the §2 F1 empty-stomach photos show **partially-digested food retained ≥4–7h post-meal** — food demonstrably still aboard overnight is closer to the delayed-gastric-emptying observation, *arguably the more escalation-worthy fact*. A naive lane that pattern-matched "2–8am + fasted → BVS, suggest a bedtime snack" would simultaneously over-diagnose and under-escalate. The honest computable output: the fasting-gap distribution ("9 of 12 early-morning episodes were ≥6h after the last logged meal") and the retained-food count — never the syndrome name, never the management suggestion.

**Regurgitation vs vomiting.** Textbook-grade, uncontroversial, load-bearing: entirely different differentials (esophageal vs gastric/systemic). Vomiting = prodrome (drooling, lip-licking), active retching, digested food, bile possible. Regurgitation = passive, no prodrome, often soon after eating, undigested and possibly **tubular** content, **never bile** (https://www.cliniciansbrief.com/article/regurgitation-or-vomiting). Owners routinely conflate them. What a log can capture: minutes-since-meal (already captured), effort/retching y/n, prodromal behavior, content descriptors (tubular shape, digestion state — partially in the photo-AI fields already), video as supplement. Caution: even specialists reach only *moderate* agreement classifying owner videos (BMC Vet Res, https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12917-015-0356-2) — structured descriptors are the reliable channel. **The engine surfaces descriptors; the vet makes the call; the engine never emits the label.**

**Elimination-trial response timelines — the consensus literature prescribes monitoring in Nyx's data shape.** The ACVIM-endorsed canine chronic-inflammatory-enteropathy consensus (Heilmann et al., JVIM 2026, https://academic.oup.com/jvim/article/40/1/aalaf017/8429723 — all four numbers verbatim-verified 2026-08-14): 38–89% of CIE dogs are food-responsive; clinical response to an exclusive diet trial "typically within **10–14 days**"; **at least 3 trials with different diets** should be considered before ruling out food-responsiveness; and the consensus prescribes **at-least-weekly CIBDAI/CCECAI monitoring during trials**. Precision note from the verification pass: the consensus does *not* specify who scores the index (CCECAI includes clinician/lab items an owner cannot score) — the honest framing is that the **owner-observable core of these indices is frequency counts of exactly the signs Nyx logs** (vomiting frequency, stool consistency/frequency, appetite, activity, weight — 5 of CIBDAI's 6 items are derivable from event logs), not that the consensus mandates owner scoring. Feline: Guilford 2001 (n=55, the single prospective study, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11215916/) — 29% food-sensitive, culprits were *dietary staples*, 50% reacted to more than one ingredient — **and a further 20% improved on the elimination diet without relapsing on challenge, i.e. diet response alone is not proof of food sensitivity** (the strongest domain-specific citation for the trial lane's no-attribution rule); expected GI improvement 1–2 weeks (Purina Institute). Dermatologic (for contrast): >85% of dogs remit by 5 weeks, >95% by 8; cats 50/80/90% at 4/6/8 weeks (Olivry & Mueller CAT, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4551374/). So **week-3 GI non-response is genuinely informative — about *this diet***, never about food-responsiveness (≥3 trials), and never about a derm-indication trial (too early). Two facts bound any trial-response phrasing: **the engine cannot know the indication** (GI vs derm assessment points differ), and **contamination is invisible to a log** — Raditic et al. found **3 of 4 OTC venison dry foods ELISA-positive for undeclared soy/beef** (https://www.researchgate.net/publication/236662116), so any OTC-diet trial can be invalid at the *diet* level under perfect logged adherence. The engine may state before/during counts and the trial day-count; it may never call a trial "clean" (G2 already forbids the sentence) and never "ruled out."

**Cough in cats — never normal, and it contaminates the vomit stream.** Chronic cough is airway disease until proven otherwise (feline asthma affects 1–5% of cats — Cornell, https://www.vet.cornell.edu/departments-centers-and-institutes/cornell-feline-health-center/health-information/feline-health-topics/feline-asthma-what-you-need-know; heartworm mimics it; cats rarely cough from heart failure). **Correction (verification pass 2026-08-14): no veterinary source pins a numeric chronic-cough threshold** — v1's "≥4 weeks" line is human pediatric guidance, and the veterinary chronic-bronchitis convention is ~2 months; a cough-detector floor must be owned as a product/Dr. Chen calibration, never claimed on guideline authority. **Owner misattribution to hairballs is the canonical error** — the asthmatic cough posture looks exactly like "trying to bring up a hairball," and every major source calls out that this mistake delays diagnosis. **⚠ Citation corrected 2026-08-30 (CUL-671; see `2026-08-event-taxonomy-evidence.md` §V.1d): the Cornell page cited above carries the 1–5% prevalence and does not mention hairballs at all.** Cite **VCA** for the confusion — "It is often confused with 'bringing up a hairball,' but no hairball is produced"; "Cats will crouch down and extend their neck when they cough" (https://vcahospitals.com/know-your-pet/coughing-in-cats) — which also sources the post-tussive sentence below: "Severe bouts of coughing may end with a retch and a cat may even bring up stomach contents, such as bile." The claim itself holds; only its attribution was wrong. Worse for a logging app: **post-tussive gagging can end in actual vomiting** (VCA), so cough and vomit events cross-contaminate in both owner perception and physiology — a caveat that applies to §2 F1's early-morning set. For the §2 F3 course: a 6-week cough beside a vomiting workup is precisely what a vet wants surfaced as a *separate, dated problem line* — and the engine must never write the hairball-explanation sentence.

**Weight.** The **>5% unintentional loss** threshold is the *cachexia-definition criterion* in Freeman's JVIM review (https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/j.1939-1676.2011.00838.x) — precision note from the verification pass: the review carries 5% inside definitional criteria, not as a standalone "evaluate at >5%" recommendation (that directive phrasing circulates in secondary vet-nutrition sources); any product threshold cites the definition, not a nonexistent recommendation. Freeman 2016 (n=569 cats, verbatim-verified): median **8.9% body-weight loss in the 12 months before CKD diagnosis, weight loss already present 3 years pre-diagnosis**. Weight loss is the *most common* clinical sign of feline chronic enteropathy — often preceding vomiting/diarrhea. Implications: weight is the highest-value under-captured stream in the schema (§2: the real cat has zero weigh-ins); ≥2–3 weigh-ins before any trend claim (5% of a 4kg cat is 200g — home-scale noise on one reading); a stable weight never renders as reassurance.

**Do vets actually value owner logs?** No RCT shows diaries improve consults — state that honestly. Three strong indirect anchors: (a) the validated instruments vets already trust (CIBDAI/CCECAI/FCEAI) *are* owner-reported frequency counts, and the 2026 ACVIM consensus prescribes them weekly during trials — the consensus literature literally mandates the data shape Nyx produces; (b) the RVC Pet Epilepsy Tracker precedent — app diaries exported to vets are standard-of-care culture in veterinary epilepsy; (c) unstructured owner diet histories are documented-incomplete in chronic-GI cats (2023, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36799862/) — structured food logging fills a named gap. The trustworthy export register: **counts and dated descriptors, never interpretations** ("2–4 episodes/week for 12 weeks; 14 of 36 within 15 min of a meal; weight −5.4% over 8 weeks; trial day 21 of 56; 2 logged off-trial exposures").

**Six ways a naive engine goes wrong here (each documented above):** hair-in-photo read as benign (backwards); timing-fits-BVS → name BVS (fails diagnosis-of-exclusion; the retained food contradicts it anyway); week-3 non-response → "food ruled out" (contradicts ≥3-trials consensus + invisible contamination); ">2/month" escalated as probable-diagnosis rather than investigate-threshold (disputed expert opinion from biopsy cohorts); treating cough and vomit as independent streams (post-tussive vomiting; misclassification both ways); stable weight or clean-looking record rendered as reassurance (weight precedes diagnosis by years).

## §5 Human-space analog products (research lane 3)

The best human self-tracking products converged, independently, on most of the register Nyx already runs — and their documented failures map the traps ahead. Full agent sweep 2026-08-13; URLs inline.

**N1-Headache (Curelator)** is the closest structural analog to detector ①: an n-of-1 statistical engine over daily diaries producing a per-person **Trigger / Protector / No-Association trichotomy** (visual grammar: distance = association strength, icon size = exposure frequency — the denominator is *visible*). Methodology published (Peris, Donoghue et al., Cephalalgia 2017: 85% of patients *with at least one identified association* had unique trigger profiles, n=326 — the case for per-pet inference over breed averages; https://n1-headache.com/patients/migraine-articles/individual-maps/). Two defining findings: the **suspected-vs-confirmed reveal** — across 488 users, only ~11.5% of user-suspected triggers were statistically confirmed, and 61% showed *no* association (conference/press-release-grade figures, not peer-reviewed — but the "what you believed vs what your data shows" moment is the emotional payoff of the whole product); and the **cost of its 90-day floor** — only 32.4% of users completed 90 diary days (Seng et al., Headache 2018, n=1,561, verbatim-verified; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30334248/). A statistically-honest calendar floor is commercially brutal.

**The backfire literature is the sharpest external input to Nyx's roadmap:** headache-diary trigger judgments are contaminated by prior beliefs (https://ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5938155); formal causal attribution needs assumptions diaries can't carry (Turner & Houle, https://ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3628761); and Paul Martin's RCTs found **advising trigger avoidance can sensitize patients** — avoidance produced no improvement where graded exposure did (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19556046/). An association engine whose implicit output is "avoid X" can harm even when the statistics are right. N1's structural answer is the No-Association map itself — built to *reduce* avoidance. GI-tracker harms are measured, not hypothetical: 77% orthorexia-risk rate in IBD patients vs 47% controls (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11054879/); symptom-tracking app users reported *more* symptoms at 4 months than controls (https://www.researchgate.net/publication/343642538).

**mySymptoms** is the named anti-pattern: food→symptom correlation searched over lags up to 3 days, output as an unexplained scalar Score + Confidence, with its own guide admitting "can't identify cause – only correlation" (https://www.mysymptoms.net/ios-user-guide/) — users call the reports "just about completely useless" and re-analyze raw exports. An opaque scalar over a long ambiguous window reads as noise; Nyx's count-phrased, named-window sentences are the documented fix. **Cara Care** embeds correlation inside a clinician-sanctioned elimination-reintroduction protocol (its 378-patient RCT ran under Germany's DiGA framework) — structurally the same move as Nyx's trial-scoped detection. **FoodMarble**'s valence discipline: "This number isn't a 'good' or 'bad' score — it's simply information."

**Seizure diaries** contribute the field's hardest-won honesty: ~50% of video-EEG-confirmed seizures never reach the diary (Fisher et al. 2012, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1525505012003368), yet *timing precision of promptly-logged events is high* and **within-record temporal structure (clusters, multiday cycles) survives underreporting** — Karoly et al., Epilepsia 2020, forecast electrographic seizures from self-report diaries alone (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32219856/). The mature conclusion — *a diary is a floor, not a census; phrase analytics over "recorded" events; prefer temporal structure over absolute rates* — independently validates both Nyx's "episodes we could time" phrasing and the timing-phenotype direction of §2 F1 over rate-based claims. SeizureTracker's research arm also derived **per-person cluster definitions from inter-event-interval statistics** rather than a fixed global rule (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0920121119303869).

**CGM (Levels/Zoe/Dexcom)**: Levels scores each meal from a *mechanistic, stated* 2-hour post-meal window, and when two meals' windows overlap it **merges them into one jointly-scored attribution zone** (https://support.levels.com/article/32-about-glucose-response) — an honest answer to the dual-meal attribution ambiguity Nyx's 12h vomit window also faces. Dexcom Clarity — a real medical device — ships *deterministic* pattern flags (recurring highs/lows over a 14-day default) positioned explicitly as **conversation-starters with the clinician** (https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/dexcompdf/Dexcom_Clarity_User_Guide_Home_User.pdf). The CGM-for-wellness backlash (spikes are normal physiology; monitoring the healthy manufactures anxiety/orthorexia) shows per-person baselines and "your response" framing do not by themselves stop users pathologizing normal variance — the escalation floor has to encode what normal looks like.

**Oura** (Nyx's named design benchmark) and **Whoop** contribute three mechanics: (1) Oura's **Symptom Radar register** — "No signs / Minor signs / Major signs of *strain*", explicitly not diagnosis, professional-consult rail attached (https://ouraring.com/blog/symptom-radar/) — the closest shipped analog to escalate-never-diagnose; (2) Oura's **Rest Mode** — when you're sick the app *stops scoring you* (knowing when judgment is the wrong register — cf. Nyx's withholding rules on refusal records); missing data gets an explanation card, never silent imputation; (3) **Whoop's Journal floor is the single best mechanic found in this research**: behavior-impact analysis unlocks only at **≥5 "yes" AND ≥5 "no" entries within 90 days** — a *contrast-based* floor gating on the information actually needed (both sides), not calendar time, filling faster for frequent events and never for never-varied ones (https://www.whoop.com/us/en/thelocker/the-whoop-journal/). Whoop also reports daily targets as ranges because users "cannot distinguish between two decimal points" — ranges as honesty about noise.

**Regulatory register (FDA General Wellness guidance, revised Jan 2026):** wellness products may track/display data without medical interpretation, show "patterns over time instead of labeling values normal/abnormal," and route users to professionals — but may not issue diagnostic alerts, and fine-print disclaimers do not rescue copy that talks like diagnosis ("context and user expectation outweigh fine-print language"; https://www.troutman.com/insights/fdas-2026-guidance-on-general-wellness-devices-policy-for-low-risk-devices/). Veterinary software sits outside this regime, but the line — descriptive not evaluative, patterns not verdicts, action routed to the professional — is Nyx's existing register codified by a regulator.

**Lane synthesis (agent's five transferable lessons, condensed):** (1) "No association" is a product, not a null result — but only above its own count floor and never rendered as reassurance (the G2 two-sided rule already encodes this; the *suspected-culprit-at-trial-start → observed* reveal is the diet-trial payoff shape). (2) **Count contrasts, not calendar days** — the Whoop-shaped floor ("N episodes with / N without") beats N1's 90-day cliff, and progress-toward-floor is a designed building state, not silence (bears directly on §2 F4). (3) **Name the window inside the claim** — never a correlation without its window and denominator in the same sentence; merge overlapping attribution zones. (4) **The diary is a floor, not a census, structurally** — prefer within-record temporal patterns over absolute rates (validates §2 F1's phenotype direction). (5) Escalation needs a register that alarms without diagnosing; associations one that informs without prescribing — "avoid X" only ever arrives from the vet, with Nyx supplying the counts.

## §6 Pet competitive landscape (research lane 4)

Analytics-capability refresh of the June 2026 landscape (what competitors *compute* vs store, as of 2026-08-13). Full agent sweep; URLs inline.

**Manual-log apps compute nothing.** The category (PetDesk, PetNote/PetNoter, DogNote, HotSpotter) stores and charts; "spot health trends" means the owner does the spotting. The nearest Nyx-shaped entrant, **Everkin** (https://everkin.io/blog/pet-health-tracking/), markets "smart insights surface correlations automatically" but the mechanism, read carefully, is timeline *juxtaposition* — visual adjacency, no method, no counts, no thresholds, and the insights timeline is paywalled. **No pet product computes a food↔symptom association with a stated method**; the only consumer implementation of that idea is human (mySymptoms, §5). **Diet-trial tooling is an empty category**: the incumbent is a paper handout (VCA protocol pages, printable trackers); Purina's CentreSquare is clinician education; Royal Canin's app does portion math. Post-prandial windowing, symptom time-of-day clustering, and trial-aware exposure accounting are, as of this sweep, **unique to Nyx** — veterinary guidance literally tells owners to hand-write "patterns around meals or time of day" because no tool computes it (https://todaysveterinarypractice.com/gastroenterology/gi-intervention-approach-to-diagnosis-therapy-of-the-vomiting-patient/).

**Where real analytics exist, they ride sensors — and the alert grammar is uniformly vaguer than Nyx's.** **Whistle Health** (Kinship/Mars) is the validation benchmark: collar-accelerometer licking/scratching classification (100k-dog Pet Insight Project), alerts firing when behavior crosses severity bands vs a **7-day rolling average** (exact thresholds published: scratching 0–52/53–119/120–299/>300 s/day), and a peer-reviewed outcome study (~7,000 dogs, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10445133/): alerts raised the vet-visit-within-4-weeks rate from 4.74% → 7.49% (OR 1.63) — **behavior change, not diagnostic accuracy; no sensitivity/specificity reported; ~92% of alerts still led to no visit** (the alert-fatigue base rate any escalation lane should be measured against). **Purina Petivity** (litter-box scale, $129.99, no subscription) alerts on deviation from population-derived thresholds — "*We noticed Star's litter box visits have doubled recently*" — naming stakes in marketing (UTI/kidney/diabetes) while disclaiming diagnosis. **Tractive** completed a health-platform pivot (April 2026): activity-decline over 6-week/6-month windows, sleep-disruption alerts, scratch-change monitoring, resting heart/respiratory-rate change. **Fi Series 3+** claims "85% accuracy" real-time behavior detection (marketing figure, no paper). **PETKIT Purobot** now ships in-box AI stool-image classification. The shared grammar everywhere: change-from-own-baseline, named-possible-causes, "worth checking with your vet" — **never counts, never denominators**, and baseline-normalization designs implicitly reassure whenever silent: a uniformly-bad-from-day-1 record (the B-494 refusal case) would never fire any of them.

**Paywall placement is a live differentiator:** **Whisker** puts its Patterns layer — "you'll be notified of any meaningful changes" — behind Whisker+ at **$7.99/mo** (https://www.litter-robot.com/whisker-plus), i.e. the incumbent charges monthly for exactly the safety-signal layer Pets > $ keeps free. Meanwhile emergency triage being never-gated is emerging as table stakes in the checker category (Omelo).

**AI symptom checkers are stateless; the credible threat is announced, not shipped.** Joii/Petriage/Pearl/Pet Check AI are one-shot triage tools; none reads longitudinal history, none ties symptoms to food. The signal to watch: **Vet-AI (Joii) has announced "longitudinal pet care memory… across a pet's entire care journey"** as a planned capability on its Gemini+RAG stack (500k real consults, 60-vet human-in-the-loop, claimed 81% triage accuracy; https://diginomica.com/how-vet-ai-deployed-ai-pet-healthcare-10-weeks-without-hallucinations) — the first well-funded mover toward record-aware AI. **Population priors exist and are already consumer-facing**: Nationwide's free Pet HealthZone serves age×breed risk profiles from 12M+ claims (https://www.petinsurance.com/healthzone/methodology/) — proof of consumer appetite for "pets like yours," though nobody has fused a population prior with an individual's longitudinal log.

**Lane synthesis.** Nyx is ahead on: computed food↔symptom correlation from logs (nobody), trial-aware analytics (empty category), count-phrased honest alerts (nobody attaches numerators/denominators), deterministic auditable methods (vs "85% accuracy" black boxes), and the 60-second clinical report. Competitors are genuinely ahead on: **passive capture** (Whistle's accelerometer, SureFeed's per-meal microchip-attributed weights, Petivity's per-cat elimination data — hardware attacks the same under-logging problem Nyx attacks with UX, and never forgets), **population-scale norms** (100k-dog behavioral baselines, 12M-claim risk tables — Nyx is n=1 by design with no comparator), and **integrated escalation** (Joii/PetPace/Toletta route an alert into a live consult; Nyx's report presumes an owner-initiated visit).

### C1 — The empty-stomach timing lane, plus the suppression fix (the ⑤ mirror)

**What:** a detector for the fasting-gap phenotype — the fraction of eligible episodes ≥Xh since the last feeding, composed with the clock band it concentrates in; and the composition fix that makes it visible: ⑤ suppresses ⑥ only when the two findings' *episode sets* substantially overlap, not merely when both fire for the same symptom type.
**Evidence for:** §2 F1 (10 of 36 episodes, 2–8am, bile-corroborated by the photo reads — and invisible today precisely because ⑤ fired); the 2026-06 vet council named the phenotype decomposition its top un-surfaced finding; §4 (the fasting-gap distribution is honestly computable; the *retained-food* nuance makes the composed content×timing read more escalation-worthy than the clock pattern alone); §5 (within-record temporal structure is the diary-robust signal class); §6 (meal-relative symptom analytics exist nowhere else in the pet space).
**Constraints the evidence itself imposes:** never name BVS (diagnosis of exclusion, no feline case series, JAAHA reclassifications); never suggest the bedtime-snack maneuver (management is the vet's); the card states gap counts and the clock band, nothing more. Threshold choice (≥4h vs ≥6h) and the suppression-overlap fraction are Dr. Chen ratification items, and the detector needs the ⑥-style uniform-random property test before floors lock (the calibration lesson ⑥/⑦ both paid for).
**Cost shape:** small — reuses ⑤'s eligible-episode machinery, confidence filtering, and grazing-guard scaffolding; the suppression fix is composition-layer only.

### C2 — Capture-taxonomy expansion: cough and sneeze become event types

**What:** two new symptom event types (respiratory), rendered through the existing `EVENT_TYPES` → `EventIcon` single render path; ⑦ (and eventually ④/③/⑥) extended over them.
**Evidence for:** §2 F3 — the owner has *already* logged the course 14 times in free text, and it would fire ⑦'s firm tier today if the type existed (the record is retroactively reconstructible); §4 — chronic cough in a cat is never-normal, the hairball misattribution is the canonical diagnosis-delaying error, and a dated cough line beside a vomiting workup is exactly what the vet wants; the capture-bounds-detection principle (vet council) now has an empirical demonstration in production data.
**Constraints:** the engine must never explain a cough as hairball behavior; the cross-stream caveat (post-tussive vomiting) means cough and vomit lanes should eventually disclose adjacency rather than pretend independence; the 21 existing `other` rows raise a re-typing question (§8 Q2 — semantic re-keying of owner rows is Class-B territory under the B-414 rule: write-path only, never silent/retroactive). Timing note: B-745 just rebuilt the picker and B-746 is scoped for glyph work, so the capture-side cost has never been lower.
**Cost shape:** small on capture (a type, a glyph, copy); moderate on detection (⑦'s registry is designed for per-type extension; cough-specific floors are a **product/Dr. Chen calibration** — the verification pass found no veterinary numeric threshold to borrow (§9), so the floor is owned, property-tested, and never claimed on guideline authority).

### C3 — The trial-response lane (symptom trajectory against the trial timeline)

**What:** a deterministic, count-anchored statement of the symptom record relative to the active trial: episodes-per-logged-week before trial start vs during, beside the trial day-count ("day 21 of 56"), gated by the C/E-test as the internal primitive, phrased under the Change Contract (count-anchored, time-ordered, never verdicted).
**Evidence for:** §2 F5 (as re-run 2026-08-14, Nyx-only) — the live trial's own record is the demonstration: a phenotype-specific response (empty-stomach stopped; rapid persists) and a halved-but-not-yet-bankable pooled rate that today's surface renders as an unchanged "book a vet visit" chronicity card, while an adherent single-protein trial structurally silences ① (the detector the trial band promotes); §4 — the ACVIM 2026 consensus *prescribes* weekly owner-scored frequency monitoring during GI trials (the consensus mandates exactly this data shape), GI response is expected within 10–14 days, and week-3 non-response is genuinely informative about *this diet*; §6 — the category is empty everywhere; §5 — Cara Care shows correlation-inside-a-protocol is the defensible frame, and the trial is Nyx's protocol.
**Constraints the evidence imposes (these are the lane's spine, not caveats):** RTM — owners start trials when things peak, and ~20–30% apparent improvement is expected from natural variability alone (§3, Goldenholz), so improvement is *never* phrased as caused by the diet; non-response is stated as counts, never as "not working" or "food ruled out" (≥3-trials consensus; contamination invisible to logs — Raditic's 3-of-4 OTC venison finding lands directly on Cooper's trial); the engine cannot know the indication (GI vs derm assessment points differ), so no assessment-point verdicts — only counts beside the day-count; G2's two-sided rule governs every sentence; the diet-trial spec's evidence-bound rules (`exposureRange` vs `range`, one predicate) bind any implementation. The strongest wording tier the literature supports is reserved for **re-challenge** (remission → provocation relapse → re-remission, the A-B-A withdrawal standard) — a future capture flow, not v1 of the lane.
**Cost shape:** moderate — new lane, but reads existing tables (`diet_trials`, `diet_trial_foods`, events) through the existing `lib/dietTrial.ts` predicates; the phrasing layer inherits the Change Contract and the B-721 receipt shapes.

### C4 — Content×timing composition over the photo-AI fields

**What:** deterministic joins between `event_ai_analysis` structured fields and event timing: the retained-food count (recognizable food in episodes ≥4h post-meal — the delayed-emptying observation), bile×fasting corroboration (feeds C1's card a second independent source), and hair-frequency honesty (a frequent-hairball count in a shorthair is itself vet-worthy — Cannon; the folk-attribution the engine must never echo).
**Evidence for:** §2 F7 (31 of 36 vomits carry completed reads; two fields consumed today); §4 (each composition has a named textbook-grade clinical meaning; hair-as-benign is documented-backwards).
**Constraints:** the fields are AI-derived — counts must scope themselves to "photographed episodes we analyzed," tristate `unsure` values stay out of numerators and denominators alike, and every output is escalate-or-describe (the n=1 asymmetry already governs the per-incident layer these fields come from).
**Cost shape:** small–moderate; the red-flag join is the proven pattern.

### C5 — Sub-floor honesty: the inter-event-gap lane and the building register

**What:** two separable pieces. (a) A descriptive gap lane — the g-chart shape (§3): "the gaps between episodes have shortened: 21 → 9 → 4 days," escalate-only on shortening, informative from ~3–5 gaps — the only method in the sweep that speaks at Cooper's scale. (b) The building register: progress-toward-floor as a designed state ("what the engine is watching"), the Whoop contrast-floor lesson (§5) applied to §2 F4's three silent weeks — a Signal-surface concern (B-721 E1/E2, B-735) more than an engine one.
**Evidence for:** §2 F4 as re-scoped — the sub-floor state is every real user's first weeks *by construction* (no live-record evidence claims survive the Cooper correction; the mechanical demonstration and the onboarding argument do); §3 (g-chart floor 3–5 gaps; C/E-test discriminates from ~5 events); §5 (progress-toward-floor as designed building state, not silence; ~92% of Whistle alerts producing no visit is the fatigue base rate that argues *for* floors and *for* honesty about them, against lowering them).
**Constraints:** the gap lane fires at very small n by design, so it needs the full adversarial calibration ritual (uniform/Poisson null property tests, the ⑥ lesson) *before* floors lock, and lengthening gaps must never render as improvement (escalate-only asymmetry; RTM). The building register must not become gamification or an implied promise that a card is coming (§8 Q6).
**Cost shape:** (a) small once calibrated; (b) design work on an existing build-ready surface.

### C6 — Weight: the highest-value under-captured stream

**What:** not a detector first — a capture problem. The literature (§4) is unambiguous: >5% loss is the evaluation threshold, weight loss precedes CKD diagnosis by up to ~3 years and is the most common sign of feline chronic enteropathy — and the real cat has **zero** weigh-ins (§2). Report-side composition exists (the B-494 safety band); signal-side anything needs data.
**Evidence-implied shapes:** a gentle weigh-in cadence via the notification foundation (owner-configured schedule = tool, not nudge, per D1); vet-visit weight extraction from Vet Files documents is Phase-2/D8-gated; ≥2–3 weigh-ins before any trend sentence; stable weight never renders as reassurance.
**Cost shape:** small per piece, but the value is gated on capture behavior change, not code.

### C7 — Route-aware medication confounding, and the med-response contrast

**What:** (a) narrow: exempt topical/otic routes from GI-symptom confounder suppression (§2 F8 — the shipped rule Early-capped food→vomit correlations for five weeks over an ear drop; the `route` column exists; the diet-trial C3 ruling already established route-awareness as in-scope elsewhere, zero new schema). **Constraints:** (a) is a falsification-pass item (the identity-agnostic rule was a deliberate safety choice — loosening it needs the adversarial reviewer to try the counterexamples, e.g. a topical steroid with systemic absorption); (b) inherits every RTM/phase-floor constraint from C3's spine.
**Cost shape:** (a) small; (b) moderate, after course objects (B-140) settle.
