02

Who else does this

The shape is borrowed; what sits in it is new. A + that turns to × over a staggered stack of labelled pills is almost exactly Material 3's FAB menu, and Google Calendar and Keep ship versions of it. What we could not find anywhere is an app whose own fan holds recents that write a record in one tap. The nearest relatives live in the operating system: Android shows two fixed shortcuts and then two recent ones behind an app icon, and Apple's Messages fills its quick actions with recent conversations. In logic, the closest is Slack, whose long press leads with a frequent reaction you add in one tap. So the part you found innovative is genuinely ours.

Changing surface for the long tail is the convention. Material caps a speed dial at six actions and says to use something other than the FAB past that; WhatsApp's floating reactions and Slack's frequent reaction both hand off to a full picker. What both platforms ask for is a visible link between the two surfaces: Material's container transform exists to create "a visible connection between two UI elements", and on iOS 26 "sheets can also directly morph out of buttons that present them". So the seam you felt is not the change of surface. It is how the second surface arrives (§03).

One caution travels with the novelty. Apple asks that recent actions "change in ways that people can predict", and a CHI 2004 study found a menu whose top items stayed put was faster than one that reordered by frequency and recency (Findlater and McGrenere). Our fan reorders after every log, and a tap on it writes a meal. The team picks this up in §04.

Delight that is the record moving

Ruled: the four ideas below (CUL-1643 to 1646). Principle 9 asks one question of any motion: cover the words, and can you tell the record just changed? Each of these moves only when the record moves, or when the thing a tap writes to changes, and none needs a new native build.

The meal lands in its card. Today, tapping a recent food plays the same retract as cancelling, then the card rises from below like any app's toast. On the right, the food you chose stays while the rest step back, its meal mark flies into the card's check, and the card arrives where the pill was. You found the quieter version subtle, so the flight is part of the build from the start.

On the right phone, also try the Logging for chip: the pet switches first, then the food pills deal again for the new pet. The right phone's pills carry the proposal's words, tags, chevrons and the split stool pill; the left phone is today's fan.

06

What the team said

Six lenses read in isolation from one briefing and the same two screenshots, then the lead checked every claim that became an issue against the code. All six agreed on the white plus. None wanted option C.

Sr. Product DesignerWhite, and pure white. The split between a fan and a sheet is right; what breaks is that nothing carries across. One veil, one physics, one name, not the morph. The card should grow out of the pill you tapped, and the pills should read the way the record does.
Motion & IAThe seam is a physics problem, not a shape problem: two veils, two engines, two origins, two titles. Fix the physics first. The turn is a control answering a finger, which Principle 9 never forbade; write that rule down, and compare the bounce on a phone.
Owner panel (Jordan, Sam)Jordan barely notices the switch; Sam finds it "like being handed to another screen". Both want one wording. The real 3am risk is the wrong food under the thumb, and Dry looking exactly like Wet. Delight at 3am is quiet certainty.
Dr. Chen + Data ScientistThe seam is not a data problem; the recent slots are. Never release to log on a symptom, never celebrate one, and remember the quick meal's time is the tap's, which the engine treats as exact (CUL-1638).
Engineering + QA + AccessibilityEvery recommended idea runs on the app's current animation engine, no native build. A literal morph is not available on the native driver today. Fix the card that ignores Reduce Motion and the fan's height budget before any polish.
Research consultantThe shape is Material 3's FAB menu; the one tap recents are yours. Switching surface for the long tail is conventional. What the guidance asks for is a visible link between the two.

Method: one shared briefing, six isolated reads (Designer; Motion & IA; Jordan and Sam through the product review agent; Dr. Chen with the Data Scientist; Engineering with QA and an outside accessibility consultant; a research consultant with web search), then the lead's pass: every claim that became an issue was checked in the code. A static read is not a device pass: the jump on first open, the card overlap and the large text cut are computed from the code and flagged for the phone.
