§05

The rules a History v2 would carry — its spec's §0

Nineteen rules, every one enforced somewhere on the frames above. Rule A from round 2 (the diet in force stated once; a meal of it unnamed) is retired with the standing line that anchored it; every meal names its food, once per run.

1
A photo never renders on a list surface.A row carries a glyph; the photo is on the record where the vet will ask to see it.
2
A note is quoted, never summarised, never counted, never on a shared render.Its fate is stated where it is written (CUL-848 ships its cue before or with the row that shows the note).
3
No lens over a read's verdict exists."With a read" is the honest lens, and its empty state names the unread.
4
The day key is local midnight; a day link parses instants.Never compared as text (C-29, C-40). CUL-1073 lands first.
5
A count is a record fact.Headers count closed days; the count line and the strip come from counts over the same query; a compact line's count is computed over a whole day, never a page (C-3, C-42).
6
A row not in the record says so.A quarantined row reads "not saved to your records" and offers the re-save.
7
The read on a row is one word, in rose, only when it was worth a call; the rose is derived.Never the sentence, the observations, a flag as text or a confidence; a calm or failed read shows nothing; the rose comes from the fields as edited and the server's flags, one predicate for History, Home and the month (the dismissal rule is open: round 2's R2-5).
8
Motion answers a fact, once.The eight gestures of §04 and no others; nothing on scroll; no cascade across days; no pulse; the static frame under reduced motion; the identity rule for the first paint.
9
The doorways are a contract.Every deep-link parameter kept byte for byte; one test per doorway, per flag state.
B
A run crosses wet and dry of one product and speaks its counts; nothing else.Any other fact breaks it: a symptom, a dose, a photo, a note, a rating below most, a different product, midnight. The meal a timing line points at is its own row.
C
The header is the date, the total, and every non-routine kind.Symptoms in rose, "other" and a look neutral; meals, doses and weights leave it. The date never wraps; a long count line goes left onto its own line.
D
No chevron on a single row; a down chevron on a compact row.Every row is a door, so no row says so; the compact row opens here instead, so it does. No stub at rest: the rail appears with the open box.
E
No cross-link lines; both halves inline."in the rabbit meal" on the dose; "with Prednisone" on the meal.
F
The rose only, and nothing standing at the top.The calm word lives on the record. A calm read arriving resolves un-animated.
G
One line of the note on the row, cut at a word, the ellipsis inside the quotes.Its own line; never beside a verdict, a badge or a count; the full note on the record.
H
Morning to night inside a day; days newest first; the sort key is (time, id).A same-minute pair never swaps between renders.
I
The strip is bounded by the date lens and paged by the week.Seven local days from a per-day query; a mark per cell (hollow · plain · a rose dot), never a number; a swipe or an arrow moves one week and snaps; the arrows disable at the window's edge and say why; days outside the window are absent; the count is in the header the tap lands on and in the cell's spoken label; under Noticed a mark, never a count of looks.
J
The first paint draws each visible day once; the landed day draws once; a removed row folds shut.Home's spine gains the same first paint in the same PR, or neither does (round 2's R2-3).
K
Every meal names its food; a foodless meal says so."Meal · Selected Protein PR · dry"; "5 meals · Selected Protein PR · 1 wet · 4 dry"; "Meal · no food recorded". The same rule Home follows.
