Six lenses read it cold and came back with one shape and four decisions: the record drawn as days in the spine's language, one door per row, the filters kept and extended, the photo's read as one word. §01

What History is for — six reads, one shape

Home answers "is she okay, what happened today". History answers "what happened, when, exactly" — the record itself, readable.

Every lens said a version of the same sentence. The owners open it the night before the vet ("first vomit Aug 30, seven since"), mid-walk ("did I already give the pill"), a week after a food change ("fussy, or sick"), and with the phone tilted at a partner. Dr. Chen needs the owner to be able to find and say four things in the room. Data called it "the enumeration itself: nothing added, nothing folded away" — so every number on it is a claim about the enumeration. The IA called the job retrieval. Nobody asked History to judge anything. The Signal does that, on Home.

What they agreed, before anything was drawn

1 · one door
A row opens the record.Edit and Remove live there, where Remove already names the photo. The expand state, the three text buttons and the long-press go. No swipe-to-delete: a swipe on a moving bus is how a record loses a vomit (Jordan), and the sliding button is chrome moving (Motion).
2 · days
Days at local midnight, in the spine's language.One header per day; rows are the 44pt node Home already draws, so three days fit above the fold instead of one. A day's header counts kinds ("10 logged · 2 vomits · 1 cough · 7 meals") and never intake, never an adjective, never a verdict, never a streak.
3 · the unlogged day
Silence is drawn.Sep 20 is a row: "Sat, Sep 20 · nothing logged". A week away collapses to one line, never seven (the owners' nag wall). Under a lens the gap names the lens: "no vomit logged · Sep 13 – 16 · 4 days".
4 · closed days only
A count is a record fact, never a page fact.The screen pages by whole local days, so no day is ever split and a header never changes under the thumb when the next page lands. The one line at the top comes from a count over the same query, never from the rows loaded.
5 · compaction, stricter
"3 meals · 12:41 – 5:07 PM", and never across a fact.Home's wall (a symptom, a dose, a look, a weight, a photographed meal, treat vs meal) plus History's: a different food or format, any rating below all, a note, a combo vehicle, local midnight. Opens in place, row-scoped.
6 · the filters, kept
Two pills, every option visible, the tint, the doorways byte-for-byte.Extended, not replaced: months and "since the trial started" in the date sheet; "All symptoms" in the type sheet; a count on a non-default pill; a third pill only as a fact lens. Never a lens over a read's verdict — its empty result would read as wellness.
7 · the read, one word
A photographed row carries a glyph and, when a read landed, its verdict in the record's own words.Never the observations, the sentence, a thumbnail, a server flag as text, or a confidence. A failed or dismissed read shows nothing, never "clear". What the calm word does at list scale is the one thing the reads split on (§04, R1-2).
8 · notes
A note is quoted, never counted, never summarised, never a flag input.The glyph on the row; search over the record is the honest first use; the Signal never reads them; the disclosure of where a note goes ships where the note is written, before notes become more visible anywhere (CUL-848).

§07
