The six rules, and the fact each one is forbidden to hide

A
The diet in force is stated once; a meal of it is unnamed on its row.The standing line under the header names it and stays pinned while you scroll. A row names its food only when the food was something else, so the exceptions are what you read. Never hidden: a foodless meal says "no food recorded" (it must not wear the unnamed shape); a day with two diets in force names every meal (a format tag is not a food); the line is a span, not a sentence for all time, so at each boundary (Jul 26 here) a marker sits in the stream and rows before it name their food. "Unnamed" comes from a positive match on the trial's dated allowed set, never from ignorance.
B
A run may cross wet and dry of one product, and speaks its counts."5 meals · 1 wet · 4 dry". Never hidden: any other fact still breaks a run (a symptom, a dose, a photo, a note, a rating below most, a different product, midnight), and the meal a symptom's timing line is measured from stays its own row, so "3 min after eating" always has a visible "eating".
C
The header is the date, the total, and every kind that is not routine.Symptoms in rose, "other" and a look neutral; meals, doses and weights leave it. Never hidden: the total makes the remainder visible, and the enumeration covers everything that is not a meal, a dose or a weight, so Sep 13 reads "9 logged · 1 itch · 2 other", never "9 logged · 1 itch" over two noted rows. The date never wraps; a long count line goes left onto its own line.
D
No chevron on a single row.Every row is a door, so no row needs to say so; the compact row keeps its chevron because it opens in place rather than routing. Never hidden: nothing; a chevron carried no fact.
E
No cross-link lines.The dose names its vehicle inline ("in the rabbit meal"); the meal names its dose inline ("with Prednisone"). Never hidden: both halves of the pair, so under the Meal lens the rabbit still says it carried the dose (the Motion check's catch).
F
The rose only.A photographed row carries its glyph; a worth-a-call carries the word in rose; a calm read carries nothing, and the calm word lives on the record. Never hidden: the escalation. And one sentence stands at the top under every lens, "A photo's read can raise a flag, never clear one", because once some rows are rose the quiet ones would read as checked-and-fine. The rose derives from the fields as edited and the server's flags, one predicate shared with Home and the month (R2-5).
G
The note, one line, on the row.Cut at a word, the ellipsis inside the quotes, on its own line, never sharing a line with a verdict, a badge or a count. Never hidden: the full note, on the record; and never shown on a shared render. The cut is the risk ("Looked fine after, but then…" cannot become "Looked fine after…" without the ellipsis saying so), which is why the marks are the attribution and the ellipsis is inside them.
