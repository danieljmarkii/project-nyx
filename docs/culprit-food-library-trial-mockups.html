Screen C

Mid-trial add — the confirm sheet

Ruling 3's whole mechanism is §3.2's dated membership, exactly as shipped: a new diet_trial_foods row with allowed_from = today. The sheet states the two consequences and asks nothing else — role is derived from the food's type (permittedRoleForFood), never asked (Principle 1).

  • "Earlier feedings keep the reading they already have" is the load-bearing line: an add never rewrites history, so a sweet-potato feeding from day 5 stays an off-diet exposure on the report. Without this line the add reads as an amnesty.
  • Entry points: Screen B's button, and Screen D's action on food detail. Both land here.
  • No red path exists — declining just closes the sheet.
Dr. ChenThe sheet is deliberately quiet about whether adding is wise — that's the vet's call, and copy that second-guesses it ("are you sure this fits the trial?") would judge the owner. The dated record is the safety mechanism.

Screens F–H · the unruled call

Should the log-time picker say anything at all?

§6.4 permits the library to verdict because browsing is pre-decision; the picker is the moment of logging. Dr. Chen's constraint binds all three variants: nothing may gate the log or make an owner hesitate to record a transgression — an unlogged exposure is worse than the exposure. Pick one.
