01

The Signal on Home: a door per finding, and far fewer words

Nyx's Signal today holds four findings: a foreign-material photo read, vomiting in 5 of 8 weeks, coughing in 7 of 8 weeks, and vomiting soon after meals. As built, the first three are full paragraphs, each ending with the same disclaimer. Only "Open ›" in the header looks like a door, and it always opens the top card.

The ruled design keeps the three things safety requires. The ask stays on Home, in words, and never moves behind a tap. Safety findings lead. A safety card never gains a chart (S1). What moves to the screen is the explanation, the receipts and the cross-mentions. The disclaimer is said once for the whole zone.

The fix is for the title to name the finding's claim, and for the screen to open on the evidence only that finding has.

05

The FAB's colour, and whether teal stays the app's accent

The FAB shipped in the darker teal because the bright teal failed contrast: a white plus on #00C2A8 measures 2.17:1 against the 3:1 minimum. The darker teal passes but reads muddy, which is the drab you saw. The Culprit indigo clears contrast by a wide margin. The in-app brand spec (2026-07-10, rule 3) says indigo is only ever a ground and teal is the sole interactive accent. The ruling keeps the brand's teal as the live spark on the plus and puts indigo behind it, with the FAB named as rule 3's one exception.

The FAB, as one gesture

The best loggers do two things the current FAB doesn't. The menu grows out of the button instead of appearing as a panel, and one press can go all the way to a saved log. Try both on the phone below: tap the button to open the fan, or press and hold, slide to an item, and let go. Drawn with option C's colours.

  1. Touch-down answers before release. The disc compresses to 90% the instant a finger lands. Today nothing moves until the finger lifts.
  2. The plus turns with weight. A 135° spring with a small overshoot lands on ×, replacing today's 45° linear turn.
  3. Home steps back. A soft indigo scrim with a light blur, and Home settles to 97%. The menu reads as in front of Home, not stuck on top of it.
  4. The menu grows out of the button. Items fan up from the disc's corner, nearest first, 35–40ms apart. Recent foods sit closest to the thumb, and each names the time it is usually fed.
  5. Hold, slide, release later · CUL-1278 Hold for 250ms and a teal ring closes around the disc. Slide over an item and it lifts, with a selection tick each time you cross into a new one. Let go on it and it logs. One gesture, no decisions at the moment of the event (Principle 1).
  6. The completion beat names the record. "Vomit, 3:12 PM · Logged for Nyx · Undo" routes through the existing completion card (C-17), so this surface never invents its own confirmation.
  7. Close is faster than open. Items retract in reverse order in about 180ms. Open is a moment; close is getting out of the way.
  8. Reduce Motion is a crossfade. No turn, no fan, no scale. It is the frame BRK-37 found missing, and the button gains its role and a modal menu at the same time.
07

The rulings, and what each one builds

D1 · The Signal on Home ruled B

Every card becomes a headline, the ask and a chevron. Insight cards carry a small chart; safety cards stay words, with every ask on Home. The disclaimer is said once per zone, and the header's "Open ›" retires. The S1 wording edit ("plain" is not "long") is written in the build PR. Build: CUL-1270.

D2 · Twin screens ruled (a)

The title names the finding's claim, and the screen opens on the finding's own evidence. It lands with D1, since the Home headline and the screen title are the same words. Build: CUL-1270.

D3 · The FAB's colour ruled C

An indigo #251F57 disc with a bright teal #00C2A8 plus. In-app brand rule 3 gains one exception, the FAB, written in the build PR. Build: CUL-322.

D4 · Indigo as the app's action colour parked

Judged after the indigo FAB has been on a phone. Parked as: CUL-1279.

D5 · The FAB choreography ruled (a)

Press-down, the spring turn, the scrim, the fan, the completion card, the faster close and the Reduce Motion frame ship with the colour, closing BRK-37. Hold-and-slide follows after a device test. Build: CUL-322, then CUL-1278.
