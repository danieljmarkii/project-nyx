§05

Reminders

You asked for reminders. The notification foundation ruled them out of its first part (G4) for one reason: an owner who relies on a reminder that doesn't fire, or fires on the wrong phone, is the worst case. Dr. Chen agrees reminders are worth building and named the real hazard: a double dose, when two people give the same pet its medicine. This round draws reminders built around that hazard, on Mochi's Clavamox, which Jordan and Alex both give.

A phone can't take back a reminder after a dose is logged on another phone until the server can send one, which needs a push provider (still an Open Question). So until then, a reminder never says give it: it names the planned time, says check the app first on a shared pet, and opens + after a sync, where the course's button shows the last dose logged.

§10

Medicines with no end date

You asked how a medicine with no end date fits, since the course screen's plan and its end question assume one. Dr. Chen's answer: a medicine for good is a different job. It shows today's plan and whether each dose was logged, the last four weeks in counts that add up to what was planned, a heads-up before the box runs out (for methimazole, phenobarbital, steroids and insulin, running out is the risk), and a recheck date only when your vet set one. No countdown, no percentage, no streak.

As needed is different again: no schedule, so nothing is ever planned, reminded or missed. How often is the record.
