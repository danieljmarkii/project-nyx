§04

Nyx's prescription, and tapers (D10)

Your label: prednisolone 5 mg tablets, 15 of them; one every 24 hours for 10 days, then every 48 hours until gone. Your log matches it to the day, and the 15th went in at 8:51 this morning. Round 2 drew the ledger before the rule under it was written; the critique asked for the model first, and an adversarial check broke the first draft on this record (its slots put step 2 on the odd days). The model, as checked:

  1. A course holds steps, each with an interval, an amount and a bound (for N days, for N doses, until gone).
  2. The target is in doses, worked out from the steps and confirmed at setup: 15 doses from 15 tablets; a step-down can be 15 doses from 10 tablets.
  3. Slots start from a first dose confirmed at setup, never from whatever was logged first. A later step's first slot is one of its intervals after the previous step's last: Sep 30, then Oct 2, 4, 6, 8, 10. A dose with no free slot is drawn beside its step and still counts.
  4. No date is projected from the log. The schedule shows as the label's, and the log never moves it.
  5. The end question comes once the last slot holds a dose, or the day after it. It states the record first, then asks. The vet's plan for after has no default.
