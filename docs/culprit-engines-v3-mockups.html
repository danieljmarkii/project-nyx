E-2 · What makes Home stop asking §01 · §03

Deciding: what acknowledges a concern, given that no visit records what it covered.

  • A restated (recommended): the owner's answer, per sign, in "How did it go?" or once on the finding's screen for a past visit. Why: only the owner knows, and no visit enters a count.
  • A with a snapshot: also count the Get ready rows a visit displayed (dissent above).
  • C: the "My vet knows" tap only.

Consequence: unblocks EN-9's spec. It needs an AC 10 brief: the engine's shell may read owner-entered facts. At GA, Nyx's Home looks like §01's middle frame until the owner answers.

E-3 · A calendar question when no recheck is set §04

Deciding: whether a watched concern with no recheck on record ever asks again on a calendar.

Dr. Chen, Sam, Jordan: V, one "Did your vet want to see her again?" at eight weeks, stating the count.

Designer, Data Scientist, Engineering: N, no clock; only tested record facts and stale-evidence prompts.

PM decision needed: does a calendar question exist when no recheck is recorded?

Consequence: either way the drafted re-raise of the old ask is dropped. V needs a better-than-the-rule brief against DF-5.

TD-4 · Where the answer lives §02

Deciding: whether Home gains a fourth write class for answers to its asks.

Jordan: J, "I've called · Not yet" on the Home row, one tap, through a Tier-2 amendment.

Designer: D, the card face stays a door; answers on the Signal and incident screens and in "How did it go?".
