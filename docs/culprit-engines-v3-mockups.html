No zero anywhere beside a masking drug or a recent visit

EN-10's lines already hold the rule you ruled on Sep 29: while a drug that can hide the sign is on board, or ended within 42 days, or a visit is 42 days old or less, no window that touches that span shows a zero. The lines sit on screens that also draw weekly bars, a compare, the trial's vomiting sentence, a phone script and the timing lanes, and those still print the zero. PR-38's adversarial pass found six places. Each is drawn below as it is today and as proposed. Until this lands, engines_v3_en10 stays off for every account.

One rule drives every proposal: a masking span runs from a masking course's start to 42 days after it ends (or today, while it runs), and from a visit to 42 days after it. A count over a window that touches a span never prints as 0. Windows that touch no span are unchanged, so a pet with no drug and no recent visit sees exactly today's screen.

00f · Yours to rule

D1 · The bars decide

Deciding: how a weekly chart shows the weeks a masking span touches.

  • A (recommended): shade and name the span, drop only the zero's numeral, keep non-zero numbers. Meets the Sep 29 rule and keeps the escalation-direction count.
  • B: keep every number and caption the span. Breaks the rule as written.
  • C: no numerals in the span. Hides the counts a vet wants.

Consequence: A changes WeeklyBars and its spoken label only where a span is in play.

D2 · Zero only, or every fall decide

Deciding: whether the compare, the trial sentence and the phone-script compare are withheld on any fall beside a masking span, or only on a zero.

  • Every fall (recommended): "7, then 2" beside maropitant is the same "it worked" reading as "7, then 0"; a rise always stays.
  • Zero only: mirrors the server lines exactly; a fall to 2 still draws.

Consequence: every fall adds one withheld reason to the compare and one gate on the trial sentence.

D3 · The dates decide

Deciding: how "Most recent 8 days ago" stops implying a zero beside "Started 6 days ago".

  • Name the drug beside it (recommended): an On board row in the phone script, and the course line says what the drug can hide, in both forms. Needs a one-clause change in the server's course line.
  • Drop "Most recent" while a span is in play. The vet loses the date.

Consequence: the recommendation touches generate-signal/careContext.ts in this PR (copy only; it deploys on merge, dark behind the key).

D4 · What leads decide

Deciding: what a timing screen leads with when a masking drug runs inside a trial.

  • A (recommended): one undivided lane, no trial mark; the drug and the trial stay named in Around this.
  • B: keep the split lanes and move Around this above them.

Consequence: A reuses the undivided shape the screen already draws; B reorders the screen for one case.

The call is its own small record in Vet visits, never an events row (Ask reads every event's notes) and never a vet_visits row (it would move the vet report's window). One escalation per bout owes at most one follow-up, however many reads the bout had.

Notifications default off, and the foundation has no server scheduler. So the follow-up is drawn the way most owners will meet it: inside the app, on their next open after two days. The confirmation never promises a day it can't keep.

EN-9's re-raise tolerance ruled A

Deciding: how often a stable pet's owner may be asked again for nothing, now that the adversarial pass showed the drafted pair can't both hold. At 2 a week, no count test catches a doubling reliably within four weeks, so fast worsening is left to the burden card and the dense-day arm.

  • A · cap false returns at 5% of stable pets in eight weeks (recommended), and take the sensitivity that buys, reported per base rate. Why: noise is what sank the drafted trigger (59 to 81% re-asked), and the fast cases have their own nets.
  • B · cap at 10%: roughly twice the sensitivity at a low base, twice the noise.
  • C · set a sensitivity floor first (e.g. half of doublings caught within eight weeks) and accept the noise it costs.

Consequence: PR-16 measures the whole frontier on 1,000+ synthetic pets per scenario; you pick the point; PR-23 waits until the chosen setting meets it.
