02

The chart vocabulary — one language, every variant

Five marks, used identically on the dashboard and the detail screen so the drill-in feels like zooming, not switching apps. Color is by event family, never by verdict: symptom rose, meal teal, food/med slate. Verdict tone stays where it already lives — the delta text — under the shipped n=1 rules.

Event stems
A symptom day is a discrete mark, not a wiggle in a line — height = count that day. Honest about zeros: no line implying continuity between incidents.
Meal ticks + coverage gaps
Logging density under every timeline. A hollow tick is a day with nothing logged — silence renders as a visible gap, never as "all clear."
Change markers
A new food, a started med, a trial start: a slate diamond with a date. States the fact, never draws the arrow.
Baseline band
"Her usual range" as a quiet grey band behind the line — the glance question becomes inside or outside the band? Renders only once the baseline is established.
Heat cells
Day cells shaded by count — one hue, light→dark. White = logged, quiet day. Dashed = nothing logged at all: a coverage hole, visually distinct from a good day.
Words budget Each dashboard card gets at most a kicker, a number with its denominator, and one optional sub-line. The AI summary survives as a single collapsed line under the hero — tap to expand. Everything longer lives one tap down, on the approved detail screen.

V3 · Dashboard. The month as texture: the cluster reads as a dark streak, coverage holes as dashed cells, the food change as a slate ring. The time-of-day strip is net-new capability — the evening cluster is visible before anyone asks.

Data Scientist — on the time-of-day strip Hour-of-day clustering already has a floored server-side detector (⑥). The dashboard strip renders raw dots with counts — descriptive, always true. The sentence beneath it ("4 of 5 after 6pm") appears only when the count clears the same floor the detector uses; below the floor the strip shows dots with no sentence. Same discipline as everywhere: the chart may show, only the engine may say.
