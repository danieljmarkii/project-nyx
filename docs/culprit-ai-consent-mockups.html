§5

Decision for you

Brief D · existing accounts and the reviewer

Deciding: whether anything asks on its own at first launch, now that onboarding doesn't.

Options: (a) Recommended: nothing asks at launch. Existing owners meet a door the first time they need AI, and the reviewer notes plus a screen recording point App Review at Settings → AI reading and at a vomit log with a photo. (b) A one-time sheet at first launch for accounts that used AI before the update: it catches the reviewer for certain, at the cost of the interruption you just took out of sign-up.

Consequence: this unseats CUL-552's onboarding step and first-open sheet (2026-08-20 design), and rescopes CUL-1834 to the doors, the write path and Settings. Under (a) the review risk is a reviewer who never finds the ask; one app was rejected in March with "We were not presented with the consent prompt", so the notes and the video carry it (CUL-1848, CUL-188). Forecast: no new rows; CUL-1834 and CUL-1835 trade scope.

Team call, logged and reversible: every in-context door writes source = 'backstop' and Settings writes 'settings', so no migration is needed. If you want each door named in the dispute record (record, capture, Ask), that is a small additive migration in its own PR before CUL-1834.
