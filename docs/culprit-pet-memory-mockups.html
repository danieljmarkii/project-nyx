Where the two owners landed

Each read the frames below alone, with the captions removed. They agreed on two of the three screens and split on the top. This section draws what their reads add up to; the directions they read are unchanged underneath.

Their picks

The topJordan: daylight, then the record leads, a conversation, night. Sam: the record leads, then daylight, night, a conversation. Neither put night or the conversation first. Night read to Jordan as "a dark wall of homework", its counts as an inbox. The conversation "sounds sure of itself" to Sam.
The factsBoth: now, then before. "It matches how I think." By kind "opens on Nothing recorded for a cat who has lost 1.6 lb, which reads as a clean bill".
The reviewBoth: safe ones together, on one condition: show all seven before the button. As drawn, four were hidden with their switches on. Sam: "the finished paragraph problem again".
What would end itJordan: the tab opening every time on a question and "8 to review". "That is an inbox." Sam: "The AI speaks first and talks about paperwork. If its opening sentence never mentions that she has lost a sixth of her weight, I conclude it does not see the cat."
A real catch"Before a visit" said her weight was down 0.1 lb. True since the last visit, and it would wrongly relax an owner whose cat is down 1.6 lb since June. Fixed on the page, and now a rule: a "since last time" number never appears without the standing one.
The extrasBoth want: gaps only questions, before a visit (the one each would miss most), sources under the read, hand it to the vet (off until chosen). Still true: fine if rare. Set aside: Jordan wants it; Sam fears using it "on whatever I was tired of hearing".
BlockingThe permission screen's line about how long notes are kept and whether they are trained on has to be the real answer before either would tap Allow.
