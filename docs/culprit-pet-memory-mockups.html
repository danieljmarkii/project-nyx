CUL-2787 · Mock round 1 · exploratory · 2026-11-15

One page for what Culprit is working from

The idea: a single place that lists the standing facts the app holds about a pet, says where each came from, and lets the owner change or remove any of them. Every frame below uses Nyx's real record as of Nov 15. Nothing here is built and nothing is ruled. Where a decision has options that look different, they sit side by side, and the team's pick has a green outline.

What each section is asking you

§C · Editing and removing

An edit here is the same write the fact's own screen already makes. The page adds no new way to change the record, with one exception: health problems, which today can't be removed at all. A removal takes a fact away. It never says the opposite.

G2 · With the page. Each line opens its fact. The outlined line is new. The count, the comparison and the ask above it are word for word the same.
Never drawn, on purpose

The version this must never become: "Nyx has long running vomiting on record, so this is expected." A listed problem that explains a sign away is how a real illness hides behind its own label. In cats with long running vomiting that reached biopsy, 99 of 100 had bowel disease. The rule proposed in brief 4: a fact from this page may add a line or an ask. It may never remove, soften, delay or re-rank one.
§H · The model

A view over what exists, or a new store

Both shapes can draw §A. They differ in where the truth lives.

A view, plus one table grown · recommended

The page is put together on the phone from the rows that already hold each kind of fact.
reads
pet · medicines and doses · diet trial and its foods · weights · visits · files · your answers about the vet · vet instructions (when built)
Health problems grows: who said it, how sure, when, which visit, a second kind for foods and medicines she may react to, and a real remove.
"From your logs" lines are counted fresh each time and stored nowhere.
Cost: one migration on one table. No new sync table, no new deletion path, one source of truth per fact. Can't hold: a free sentence that fits no kind.

A new store of facts

The page reads one new table of facts: a kind, a sentence or value, who said it, when, a status.
which copies or points at
medicines · trial · weights · visits (each now true in two places)
New: the table, its sync, its access rules, its place in account deletion and export, and a rule for every reader about which copy wins.
A free sentence the engine "reads" needs a model to interpret it, on Home.
Cost: a migration, a synced table, and a second truth for every fact that already has a home. Gains: one place to delete, export and audit; room for any sentence.
