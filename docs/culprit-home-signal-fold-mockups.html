02

The motion — tap it

The delight is continuity, not ornament. The rail is the same line before, during and after — it shrinks with the row and never changes colour or opacity. The words leave first (what is leaving needs no reading), the box closes around the line, and the strip arrives with the box, never after it. Unfolding is that line growing back, with a soft settle and the sentence landing a beat after the box. The physics are identical on every class: a safety fold must feel like neither a reward nor a punishment. No haptic, no wash, no bounce on close, no loop.

Tap Keep it compact on either card; tap a strip to unfold it. The frame runs the spec's beats in CSS.

The fold and the unfold, as specified (§12) Fold: 0–150ms the words fade and drift 4pt toward the line (ease-out); 150–400ms the box closes around the rail while the strip fades in as one piece, and everything below rises. Unfold: 400ms the box grows back with one soft settle (iOS spring, damping 0.85; ease on Android), the face fades in, and the sentence lands a beat after the box. Reduced motion: instant geometry, a 150ms crossfade, no drift. PR 3 · CUL-788 · RN Animated + LayoutAnimation, no new deps · no haptic

fold · 0→150 body opacity 1→0, translateY 0→−4 · 150→400 height ease-in-out, strip opacity 0→1
unfold · 0→400 height spring (≤2pt overshoot), face opacity 0→1 · 0→250 sentence translateY −4→0
never · loop · scale · sideways · wash · rail colour change · bounce on close · haptic

04

For the PM — two picks

DF-10 — the fold motion (approve, or ask for a variant)

Deciding: whether PR 3 builds the choreography in §02 as drawn.

Options: (a) as drawn — 150 + 250 fold, 400 unfold with one soft settle, the rail as the continuous thread, identical on every class — recommended · (b) quicker — 100 + 200 fold, 300 unfold, no spring (calmer, less felt) · (c) the spring on both directions (warmer, but a bounce on closing a safety card reads as relief — the Designer's veto).

Consequence: (a) and (b) are the same build with different constants; (c) crosses the one line the motion spec draws.

DF-11 — the card direction (pick one)

Deciding: what PR 4 builds, if anything.

Options: (a) B carrying A's foot — the ruled margin + the quiet foot — recommended: the only direction that makes the fold more legible, for the price of a deleted divider · (b) A only — the quiet foot, no frame change · (c) keep the cards as they are (PR 4 is closed) · C is vetoed and not offered.

Consequence: (a) needs one Tier-2 line in the Signal spec (row hairlines → the ruled margin; the doorway hairline stays); (b) needs nothing; (c) closes CUL-790.

07

What the fold does not fix — said plainly

"A fold with nothing new underneath is a smaller version of boring" (Jordan). Nyx went from 12 episodes to 2 across two four-week stretches and Home still cannot say it. That sentence belongs inside the safety card's evidence and phone script, with one line on why the ask still stands — never as a separate calm card while the symptom is chronic (CUL-787). And when the card finally stops firing it should say so once, in a labeled line, instead of vanishing (CUL-786). Both are engine work, filed as v1.1 in the project. The broader answer — what leads Home, what changes daily, whether "three slots" was ever the right proxy — is the Home Redesign spike's mock round, fed by this session's interviews and research.

Nothing on this page touches detection, thresholds, ranking, or the plainness of a safety card. The fold silences the size; the record decides when the full card returns; the owner cannot make a safety card go away.
