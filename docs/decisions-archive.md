# Decision archive — resolved Open Questions

Resolved rows lifted out of `CLAUDE.md` § Open Questions (2026-07-25). **Nothing here was rewritten or condensed — the rows are verbatim.**

## Why they moved

`CLAUDE.md` is auto-loaded into every session on every turn. The Open Questions table was **47% of the file**, and 21 of its rows were decisions that had already been *made* — a resolved question is a record, not a live decision surface, and a record does not need to be re-read on every turn of every session. Same rationale and same pattern as `docs/CLAUDE-md-history.md`.

## What stayed behind

Two classes deliberately remain in `CLAUDE.md`:

- **Genuinely open questions** — they gate live work and must stay in scroll-range.
- **Four resolved rows whose ruling has no other home** (Ask D2/D7 · the B-247 PR 3 stool seam · the B-340 red-flag elevation · the PDF-library call). Every row moved here restates a decision that also lives in a `/docs/` requirements doc, so moving it loses nothing. Those four do not, and moving them would have made `CLAUDE.md` the only copy of a build guardrail that a session could no longer see. They move once they have a home (backlog item filed).

## Reading this

Read the row for a track before building on that track — though in every case the linked `/docs/` requirements doc is the fuller record and the better read. This file is the index, not the source.
