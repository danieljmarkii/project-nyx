The seams: into the form and out of it

Design pass frames only, left to right in the order the owner meets them. The frames outside this screen (the + menu, the meal card, capture's confirm) are drawn as their own tracks ruled them; this round proposes nothing on them.

A · From the + button

The + menu's Log food opens the food step directly (/log?type=meal); the sheet's Meal, Medication and Weight tiles open their steps the same way. Today the leading control on that step is a back chevron, and it lands on a type grid the owner never saw (§15). Pass (P1): the step opens with a close, and close goes home.

B · From the meal card: a dose given with the meal

The meal card's last row, + Add a med given with this, opens the dose step bound to that meal and its pet. Pass (P8): the band names the meal, its time and its pet before the owner picks.

C · A food that is not in the library yet

Snap a new food opens capture with fromLog=1. Capture's confirm is PR-12's ruled frame (P4, P6: the card of facts, "Biscuit's meal · 7:54 AM", Looks right), and Looks right both saves the food and logs the meal, so the owner returns to Home and the meal card, not to this picker.
