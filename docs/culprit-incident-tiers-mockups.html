The tier is a new column written beside today's verdict, never in place of it (the critique's GAP-3, CUL-1277). Old phones keep reading the old column and keep today's words. "Part of a pattern" is drawn here as a tier the owner sees. Whether it is also a stored value is your second ruling (§11, K2); the team recommends it is drawn at render from a live Home finding and never stored.

05

The GA day: two rules, never one number

Reads are stored per event, so on the day the tiers go live a pet's record holds reads made under two rules. Three things keep that honest (GAP-30). An old read never gets new words: a September "Worth a call" is never relabelled "call now" or "call today", because nobody knows which it would have been. An old-rule read says so on its record. And the month never adds the two populations into one count, so a steady cat never looks improved because the rule changed.

Nothing about the change reaches Home, a notification, a motion or a haptic. The owner meets it on the first new read, once per pet, in one dated line.

07

EN-5's question

The read cannot see whether a cat ate, and today it guesses from missing ratings: it told Nyx's owner three times that she "hasn't eaten a full meal recently" when meals were logged but not rated. EN-5 asks instead. The question lives on the record under the read, with no time limit, until it is answered. No answer is stored as unknown, never as "normally". The deadline is a named hour, never "since yesterday" (at 7 AM that spans 31 hours).

An answer only adds evidence. "No" can raise the read (T10); "Yes, ate well" never lowers a tier the owner was shown.

EN-4's floor: where it runs, offline, and why the re-run never re-sends a photo

Today the rule-based check runs only inside analyze-vomit, after sync, and only when a photo was attached or someone opens the record. EN-4 runs it on every vomit. It has to live somewhere that works at 3 AM with no signal, never spends the model or the photo cap, and never lowers a call the owner already saw (GAP-9, GAP-34).

  1. One pure rule. An import-free, copy-free module beside lib/incidentVerdict.ts (so the Edge Functions can import it without pulling client copy into their closure, C-26). In: the settled rows (vomit onsets marked witnessed or found, lethargy, meal ratings, the EN-5 answers), species, age, courses on board, known conditions, and the tiers already shown. Out: a tier, a reason class and the counts behind it.
  2. Its authoritative home is the server. A floor-only mode of the incident pipeline in _shared/incident-analysis.ts (a request mode on analyze-vomit or a small sibling function, decided in PR-28's plan). It reads the settled record with the caller's JWT, computes the context flags and the tier, and writes the read fields only, through resolveReanalysisWrite. A Deno test drives it with a fake client whose Storage download and model call both throw, and it must complete.
  3. Triggers. The insert of a vomit, a lethargy log or a meal; a later rating on a meal; the edit or soft delete of any of those. A delete re-runs the floor and can still never lower a shown tier.
  4. The durable marker. A local queue row written in the same local transaction as the event. It is a schema constant and on the wipe list (LOCAL_WIPE_TABLES), survives the app being killed, and drains only after the event's own push has landed, marking the version it sent (C-23, C-24).
  5. Offline. The phone runs the same rule over local SQLite and shows the tier on the completion card and the record, with one line: "Worked out on this phone. It's saved when Nyx's record syncs." The preview never reaches History, the month or Home's band, which read the stored tier. The tier the phone showed rides the marker, and the stored tier is never below it, since an owner who was told "call now" was told it.
  6. The 24-hour re-run. A lethargy log or a refusal re-runs the floor over every vomit of the same pet in the prior 24 hours. It reads each stored row's structured fields for photo findings (Pattern 9), adds the new context, and writes the tier. It never downloads a photo, never calls the model, never regenerates model words and never spends a cap unit. A raised read's words are the context template for its new reason.
  7. One arrival per bout (GAP-33). When a re-run raises several reads of one bout at once, only the most recent carries the arrival, the announcement and Home's row; the others take the tier silently and render as part of it.
  8. Never lower a shown tier. A recompute may add evidence; only the owner's own act lowers a verdict, recorded as such. Proven by mutation: delete the guard and a test with "call today at 8:00, a meal rated All at 12:00" goes red.
  9. Counting. The rungs count witnessed onsets in a span that contains this vomit and two others, merged within 30 minutes of the first without chaining. Found piles count toward the 24-hour rule and are dated by when they were found, never as onsets. It is a second, differently named predicate beside lib/symptomEpisodes.ts, registered under C-34, and its copy says "vomits logged", never "episodes" (GAP-10).
  10. Behind the flag. Flag-off never runs the floor-only path, so today is byte-identical; a flag rollback never lowers a tier written under the flag (R-1).
  11. Known limits, stated where the tier shows. A found pile in a two-cat home goes to the pet on screen (CUL-807, MFU-5); the tier names the pet it was logged for. Species "other" runs the count rungs only (T26). An unknown birthday prints the young-animal line (T7).

One table that EN-4's floor, EN-3's watch-for lists, EN-5 and the shipped Noticed door (lib/lookEmergency.ts) all read, so they can never give three answers again (BRK-3). The direction column compares each row with today's single "Worth a call". Under E-6, a louder row is adopted provisionally; a quieter row waits on harness proof (PR-16's pass line: no injected red flag below its shipped tier), your sign-off and the paid vet review (CUL-1312). Where two tables disagree today, the louder row stands until then.

Your three rulings

K1 · PMD-14: where a later or photoless call shows decide before PR-28 and PR-30

Deciding: whether a vomit with no photo whose check says "call now" takes the owner to its record.

  • A (recommended): call now routes to the record; every call joins Home's band; a later raise is said on the log that caused it. The 3 AM owner sees the reason and the script without hunting.
  • B: Home's band and the completion card only; incident D2 unchanged. Fewer screens, but the call's detail and EN-5's question sit one History tap away.

Consequence: A needs a Tier-2 edit to docs/nyx-incident-screen-requirements.md D2 and G3 (drafted in the spec, §6). Either way unblocks PR-28 and PR-30. §06 draws both.

K2 · What puts a read in "part of a pattern" decide before PR-25

Deciding: whether "part of a pattern" is a stored value or drawn at render. This is a better-than-the-rule brief: D1 (9/26) named it as one of four stored tiers, to stop a pattern being called once per vomit.

  • Drawn at render (recommended): store three tiers; a read below a call shows "Part of a pattern" while Home holds a live finding on the same sign, and "Keep an eye out" otherwise. It never goes stale against Home, and EN-3 no longer waits on EN-9.
  • Stored: the floor writes pattern when a finding is live at read time. A September read keeps pointing at a concern Home stood down in October (GAP-11's counterexample), and EN-3 must wait for PR-23.

Consequence: the protection D1 wanted (no call per vomit for a known pattern) holds either way, because the call tiers come from §10's rows, not from this chip. Recommended keeps the migration at four values.

K3 · CUL-531: an observation from a partial read decide any time before PR-27

Deciding: what "Blood: none visible" says when one of three photos couldn't be opened.

  • C, scope it (recommended): "none visible in the 2 photos read" plus "1 photo couldn't be opened". Keeps the vet's context and reads no wider than it is.
  • A, hide it: loses a real observation.
  • B, keep as shipped: reads as covering all three photos.

Consequence: C is a copy change in PR-27 on the record and the stool record; the vet report is already guarded and doesn't move.
