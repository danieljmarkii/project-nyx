§G · D1

Where the model sits

Three readings of "give Signals as much of the app's data as possible". They differ in who does the reasoning, and in what happens when the model is wrong.

A · Feed the engine

Model reads one visit, proposes facts
↓
Owner confirms each
↓
Engine counts confirmed rows, as today
A wrong extraction is caught at the confirm step or is a wrong row the owner can see and fix. The engine's rules do not change. It cannot use anything that is not a countable fact.

B · The model as analyst

Model is handed notes, documents and logs
↓
Model decides what is worth saying on Home
Uses everything, including what no table can hold. A wrong read is a wrong card with no row behind it and no test that would have caught it. It unseats "LLM as Phraser, never Analyst", and the no reassurance rule would rest on a prompt.

C · Split by surface (leaning)

Signals as in A: confirmed facts and structured fields only
The summary reads one visit when asked, and shows its sources
Ask may recall one visit's notes when the question is about that visit
Home stays deterministic and testable. The reasoning over prose happens where the owner asked for it and can see what was read. Costs one amendment to Ask's boundary on top of A.
