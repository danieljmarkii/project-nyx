§D · the one decision left on this page

What "add to the record" meant, and whether it comes back

Round 1 used a phrase that explained nothing. Here is what it meant. Your notes say Nyx was put on Motozol. Your medicines list has no Motozol in it, so the app can't count doses, show the strip on Home or know an ear treatment is running. "Add to the record" meant: offer to put Motozol in the medicines list for you, from the summary. The same for a reminder to make the call your vet asked for. That is the part that would let Signals use what the vet said. It is also the part that made round 1 feel like a lot.
