The receipt, drawn at real time

Option A, locked. Each dot sits at the episode's true minutes-after-eating, on a scale that gives the first half-hour room — so the cluster the vet cares about is the thing you see. Below: the confident case, the two states that keep it honest, the tap-through, and the rules to build to.

What this locks

  • Real times — one dot per timeable episode at its true minutes-after-eating
  • Expanded-early scale — first 30 min = 60% of the lane; 30m label on the window edge
  • Soft & jittered dots — honest about ± a few minutes; never hair-precise
  • Gate the noisy case — grazing / uncertain timing falls back to the split
  • Degrade above 12 — too many to count individually → the stacked compare
  • Anamnesis copy — shows timing, never a cause, mechanism, or eating-speed

The distribution, up close

The default state at full size, with the design rules called out. Every position is a real minute; the scale just spends its width where the finding lives.

1Expanded early window. The first 30 min takes 60% of the lane, so a cluster at 4–11 min is legible instead of crushed into a sliver.
2Honest boundary. The dashed edge is 30 min, and the "30m" label sits on it — fixing the shipped card's label floating at mid-lane.
3Soft, jittered dots. A faint halo + vertical jitter say "real, but ± a few minutes" — the plot never implies clock-precision the logs don't have.
4Median tick + compressed tail. The rose tick marks the typical timing (~9 min); later episodes stay visible but compress into the right 40%.

Rules to build to

The design contract the requirements will formalize — geometry, the state machine, and the guardrails that keep A honest.

Geometry

ScaleExpanded-early: 0–30 min → 0–60%, 30–120 min → 60–100%. Honest ticks at true minute values.
DotsOne per timeable episode; 8px; soft halo + vertical jitter for near-ties; rose in-window, grey later.
MedianRose tick at the median rapid minute; label lives in the meta line, not on the lane.
UntimedNever plotted; disclosed in words ("N weren't near a logged meal").

State machine

DefaultTiming reliable & ≤12 timeable → the distribution.
GatedTiming unreliable (grazing / free-fed active / uncertain attribution) → the split + honesty line. Never a cluster.
12+More than 12 timeable → the stacked compare (DOT_LANE_MAX).
PrecedenceGate wins over count: a noisy 20-episode finding is a split, not a compare.

Guardrails

CopyAnamnesis only — timing, never cause / mechanism / eating-speed (§9.2 validatePhrasing).
NeverNo precise cluster on unreliable timing; no reassurance on the gated card; absence never = wellness.
PayloadAdds minutesSince[] as an additive-optional mirror field (the SR-4 medContext pattern); old cache renders the split.

Open — before / with build

ValidationData Scientist + Dr. Chen: does the detector reliably know when timing is noisy? The gate is only as good as that signal.
