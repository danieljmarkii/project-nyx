Mock round 1 · night surfaces · 2026-10-27

The moon, drawn in the dark

Linear puts a hairline outline of its mark in the empty space of a new chat, faded toward the bottom, barely above the ground. It is identity without decoration: no motion, no colour, nothing to read. These frames borrow that move for the two places the Culprit mark already lives at night: the Daily Recap the 9pm notification opens, and the launch screen.

The watermark

What gets drawn

  • The real geometry. The crescent's outer arc and its carve arc, plus a ring where the Signal dot sits, all from CulpritMark's viewBox. The carve rule holds: it is an outline, so the ground passes through.
  • One hairline. 1pt stroke in the night border tint, about 22% at the top and gone by three quarters down, the way Linear's fades into the input.
  • No teal. The dot is a ring, not a fill. Teal stays the mark's own dot; a teal haze was already rejected on device (2026-07-12).
  • Still. No loop, no fade in, no parallax. Principle 9 stays whole, and Reduce Motion has nothing to switch off.
  • Silent to VoiceOver. Decorative, hidden from assistive tech, under every touch target.
01 · The Daily Recap

Where the 9pm notification lands

Same placement in every state: top of the screen, centred, a touch wider than the phone, scrolling away with the page. The lead and chips sit over its faded lower half.

Deciding
Whether the Daily Recap carries the moon watermark, and in which states.
Options
  1. A · No change.
  2. B · Every state, one placement. Recommended: it reads as the screen's identity, never as a comment on the day.
  3. C · Empty day only. Prettiest single frame, but a calm moon beside "Nothing in the record" can read as an all clear, the one thing that copy is written to avoid.
Consequence
B adds one still, decorative layer to /day-summary; no copy, data or record change. The register rule (§1.2) already makes this a night surface.

02 · The launch screen

The dark blue screen while the app loads

This is the native splash: a picture iOS shows before any of our code runs. It can only be a centred image on a solid colour, so whatever goes here is baked into the image itself.

S2 · Handoff, as a filmstrip

The alternative: keep today's splash, then when our code takes over, fade the outline in once and let the moon settle into the Landing hero. It only plays for logged-out owners; a signed-in owner goes to Home.
