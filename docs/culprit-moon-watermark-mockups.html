Sr. Product Designer · cold read of three full-day placements (round 3)

"Put no watermark on the full day. Linear shows its mark on an empty chat and removes it once there is content. We should borrow it the same way. On a full day the record is the picture (Principle 8), and on any symptom day the top of this screen is a safety surface."

  • F1, behind the lead: its arc runs behind "One vomit today" and the chips. That is decoration under safety content, which the clinical veto rules out (brand spec §7.5), and it reads as a moon cradling a vomit.
  • F2, up in the corner: the only one clear of safety content, but the edge cuts the ring and it crosses the header line, so it reads as a fragment. A fixed version was drawn in round 3; you chose no mark.
  • F3, at the foot of the day: a calm moon as the full stop after a vomit day is a reassurance beat. Nobody reaches it on a long day, and it collides with the recap's turn-on offer at the foot.
  • Also on the empty day: the fade ran into the title, so the moon is now 24pt higher. No other change.
