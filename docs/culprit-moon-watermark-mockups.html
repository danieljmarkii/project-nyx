03 · Motion

Pushing the envelope, inside and outside the rule

Principle 9 (2026-10-05): motion is the record moving, chrome never moves on its own, and the one loop is the tick while a request is in flight. Its test is to cover the words: can you tell from the motion alone that the record changed? Three ideas pass using gestures the app already ships; two would need you to bend the rule. Every frame rests on its final state; press the Play under a screen to watch it. Timings are the shipped ones: the thread's 370ms, a row's 300ms landing, the settle curve cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1).
