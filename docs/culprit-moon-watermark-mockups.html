03 · Motion

Pushing the envelope, inside and outside the rule

Principle 9 (2026-10-05): motion is the record moving, chrome never moves on its own, and the one loop is the tick while a request is in flight. Its test is to cover the words: can you tell from the motion alone that the record changed? Three ideas pass using gestures the app already ships; two would need you to bend the rule. Every frame rests on its final state; press Play to watch it. Timings are the shipped ones: the thread's 370ms, a row's 300ms landing, the settle curve cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1).

On the launch screen

From the splash to the Landing

Every cold start passes through the Landing for a beat, signed in or not: it is the first screen our code draws, and a signed-in owner is moved on to Home from there. On paper, today's handoff makes the moon hop about 17pt left and 19pt up and grow about 7%, while the ridges, stars and wordmark appear around it in one cut. That hop is the only motion on this screen today, and nobody designed it.

Native splash for 700ms, then our code takes over.
