§01

Where the design stands — the audit

Four lenses read the app this session: the Designer (the surfaces), the Data Scientist (what the charts claim), Engineering (what the motion stack can and cannot do), and the Product Owner (what is already filed). The verdict is not "the app is badly designed". It is narrower and more useful than that.

The design system is a set of guards. Guards prevent bad. They have never once produced good.

What is genuinely working

  • Token discipline is exceptional. One flat constants/theme.ts, every colour role-named with its contrast receipt written beside it, three build guards on colour-on-ground and typography. The Aug 22 audit found zero drift. This is the foundation a leader needs and most teams never get.
  • The type pairing is right. Geist body, Newsreader for the sentence that matters. Nobody in pet health has a serif carrying its headline; it reads as a considered product the moment it appears.
  • The motion vocabulary exists, and it is distinctive. The Signal fold (components/motion/foldMotion.ts) and the incident read's arrival (arrivalMotion.ts) share one physics: the rail is the continuous thread — the line grows first, the box follows with one settle, the sentence lands a beat later. The daily look lifted the same module for its ring. That is a house choreography. It is simply used on three surfaces.
  • The completion system is finished. Two registers, the record's own sentence, Undo on both, seven haptic verbs with a tone rule (a symptom commit is never a success). This is the calmest log confirmation in the category.
  • The clinical spine is a moat. Counts never verdicted, colour gated on an established floor, silence on safety, absence never reassurance. Every direction below inherits it untouched.

Where it reads as a records system

  • Patterns is a B2B dashboard by design, not by accident. The analytics spec (§3) explicitly took "the four-layer KPI card (label / big number / sparkline / delta)" from Amplitude and Mixpanel. MetricCard.tsx is that card. The page is a column of them: a label, a big "5", an 88×32 line with no baseline, "3 fewer than last month". It answers "how many" and never "what happened to Nyx". The AI summary that leads it is a paragraph.
  • The sparkline is a shape with no ground. Sparkline.tsx strips every axis, point and baseline on purpose ("the background doesn't compete"), draws a line through counts (a line asserts continuity between two vomits; there is none), and is 32pt tall. It cannot be read; it can only be glanced at.
  • The calendar is a spreadsheet with dots. FrequencyCalendarCard.tsx: numerals in a grid, one to three rose pips under a day, a "×N" numeral past three. It replaced an opacity heat-ramp that "never read as legible even with a legend". The pips are legible. They are also the least designed object in the app, and a tap opens a bottom sheet away from the month.
  • The per-incident reads are invisible as a set. The app reads a vomit photo with a vision model and lands a verdict on the record. On Patterns that magic appears as a count. Nowhere can an owner see the month's incidents as the photographed, read, dated things they are.
  • The Signal card is honest to the point of density. One card carries the sentence, a sample line, badges, a density disclosure, a control disclosure, an evidence control and a fold control. Every guard the spec earned became a sentence on the card. The receipt (the numbers behind the sentence) is text when it could be a shape.
  • The delight inventory is real and it is all transitional. See the table. Nothing moves on the data; the only motion an owner meets daily is the fold.

The Whorl's "background blur" — a diagnosis, not yet a confirmation

The night moment paints three layers: a solid indigo, then NightGround's three soft radial ellipses (violet upper, indigo lower, a teal wash), then a screen-width Whorl at half opacity. The most likely reading of what you see is the aurora ellipses — three low-contrast radial gradients on an indigo band, which on an OLED phone band into visible steps and read as a smeared blur rather than a sky. The pull-to-refresh band met exactly this on device in July ("the aurora glow read as unpolished") and was cut to a flat light strip. Two lesser suspects, both worth one look on your phone: the 700ms dissolve at the end of the cold start fades the whole composition over Home, which reads as a ghost for most of a second; and useAppActive reads 'inactive' as paused, and iOS launches apps through inactive, so the first frame can be the static Whorl with its 22% glow disc rather than the animated one. §08 draws the options.

The root cause

The seven principles are all restraint principles. The design principles document says never or not 66 times and delight zero times. Its motion section is four sentences, three of which are prohibitions, and the fourth allows exactly one considered animation in the whole product. The Definition of Done has an adversarial-review line, a voice line, a privacy line and no line that asks whether a PR made anything better to look at. On Aug 22 the one graphic exploration on the table, the Home Trend zone, was killed in favour of a verbiage change. Every one of those decisions was correct on its day. Their sum is an app that cannot be wrong and is not yet right.

The claim this brief makes. The fix is not a redesign and not a new palette. It is (1) two aspirational principles and one motion vocabulary written down with the same force as the guards, (2) one project on the data surfaces, because the data is where the magic already is and where the design is weakest, and (3) a process rule so that a dated principle can be challenged by a better design instead of quietly winning.

Design Principles v2.0 — the proposal

The seven stay. They are the floor and they are right; the trial-preship review, the fold spec and the daily look were all built on them without a failure. What v2.0 adds is the ceiling: two principles about what the app should be when it is right, written with tests as sharp as the seven have, and one process rule. It also rewrites the Visual Language → Motion section from four sentences into a vocabulary, because "restrained and purposeful" is not a spec anyone can build against.

1 · Zero decisions at moment of event — kept verbatim.
2 · Confirmation over entry — kept verbatim.
3 · Home is an intelligence surface — kept, with the 2026-05-30 and S1 amendments.
4 · The nudge is warm, not nagging — kept verbatim.
5 · Empty states are features — kept, with S6.
6 · The vet report is clinical-grade — kept verbatim.
7 · Premium wraps convenience, never care — kept verbatim.
Principle 8 · newproposed

The data is the delight.

The app reads an ingredient list off a photo and a verdict off a vomit. The display has to be as magical as the capture, or the magic is wasted. So: every number owns a shape, every shape reads in three seconds without a legend, and a shape draws itself the first time you see it. The most beautiful object in Culprit is the pet's own record, drawn well — every other app can have nicer chrome; only this one has Nyx.

What it forbids, so it stays inside the clinical spine: a shape never says more than its number (no smoothing a count into a curve, no colour below the established floor, coverage always drawn beside occurrence so a quiet day and an unlogged day never look alike).

The testWould Jordan screenshot this screen and send it to their partner? If the honest answer is "no, it looks like a report", it is not done.
Principle 9 · newproposed

Motion is the record moving.

One physics for the whole app: the rail is the continuous thread, the line leads, the box follows with one settle, the sentence lands a beat later. Motion happens when the record changes state — a log lands, a read arrives, a card folds, a chart draws, a day opens — and nowhere else. Never in chrome. Never on a loop, unless the app is doing real work on the pet's behalf and the loop is the one ambient gesture. Never as a reward for a symptom. Touch is the same vocabulary felt: every commit reaches the hand, a symptom is acknowledged and never congratulated, and safety is silent. A haptic or a motion is verified on a phone, never in a test.

This absorbs the Aug 22 D4 ruling ("no looping animation in app chrome; content announces, chrome doesn't") that CUL-635 is still waiting to write into the document, and replaces the v1.1 Motion section's "the only moment that warrants a more considered animation is the first time the Signal appears".

The testCover the words. Can you tell from the motion alone that something in the record just changed, and roughly what kind of thing? If the motion could be any app's, it is chrome.
Process rule · new · every workflow, not only designwritten this session

A principle is a floor with a date on it.

Every rule in this document, in CLAUDE.md and in the specs was written on a day, against what was known that day. When a better design (or a better plan, query, or test) violates one, the team surfaces the conflict in a better-than-the-rule brief — the rule, its date and what it was protecting; the better thing; whether the protection still holds; a recommendation — and the PM rules. Quietly building the compliant, worse thing is the anti-pattern. So is quietly building the better thing.

Where it lives: one line in CLAUDE.md § Presenting decisions to the PM (written this session, paid for by a trim under the byte ratchet; amend or revert on this brief), and the shape in docs/personas.md beside the Persona Conflict Protocol. §05's R-5 is the first use: D8 killed the Trend graphic on Aug 22; this brief argues a better design now exists.

Two smaller edits the v2.0 document carries

  • Philosophy. Beside "Invisible Complexity. Visible Calm." add one sentence: Calm is not quiet. Where the pet is, the app is alive.
  • Visual Language → Color. Keep "one accent, never decorative". Add the chart rule the validator gave us this session: chart text wears ink, never the series colour — teal at full strength is 2.2:1 on white and the three category hues fail every colourblind-separation check as neighbours, so a chart is never colour-alone: a shape, a label, a gap.

The full v2.0 text is written as a Tier-2 edit after the R-1 ruling (a session proposes; it does not write the principles document on its own). The version bump goes in the header, never the filename.

§07

Direction E — One physics: the record moving

The app already has a motion identity; it just lives in one module and three surfaces. The direction writes it down as the vocabulary of Principle 9 and applies it to the data: a chart draws in from its baseline the first time you see it; a day opens in place under its week (§04); a read arrives on its rail (shipped); a card folds (shipped). Four verbs. Everything else in the app is still, and chrome is always still. Press Replay.

The chart draws in — bars grow from the baseline, staggered left to right, the label lands last

420ms per bar, 40ms stagger, one settle at the top (the fold's spring, damping 0.7), the label at the end. Once per screen open; never on a re-render. Reduced motion: the bars are simply there. A safety chart draws in exactly the same way — S1's plainness is what the card says, and identical motion is what keeps a safety draw from feeling like a reward or a punishment (the fold spec's own rule).

Draw inA shape appears from its baseline the first time a screen shows it. Bars grow; an area rises; dots fill in order; a strip fills left to right. Once per open.
Open in placeA row grows around its own content under its own position — a day under its week, a receipt under its sentence. The rail leads, the box follows, the text lands.
ArriveA result the app produced lands on the mark that was already there (shipped: the read's arrival). The rail is the continuous thread.
FoldA thing seen compresses to one line and comes back when the record changes (shipped: the Signal fold).
StillTab bar, headers, buttons, chips at rest, the FAB. Chrome never moves on its own. A chosen chip's settle ring is a touch response, not chrome motion, and it fades to nothing.
FeltThe haptic verbs are the same vocabulary in the hand: routine commits succeed, symptom commits are acknowledged, safety is silent, destructive confirms are rigid. Verified on a phone or not shipped.

The engine — why delight has landed jankily, and the one decision under it

Every motion in the app is built on React Native's Animated plus LayoutAnimation. The fold module's header documents the cost: two engines, one for geometry and one for everything else, with a Fabric snap-back the code works around by pulling the rail out of layout flow for exactly the frames that animate. It works, and it took a spec section to make it work. Animated also cannot drive an SVG path on the native thread, so "the area rises" or "the ring draws itself" either run on the JS thread (the frame drops the pull-to-refresh sky died of) or get faked with View transforms (the daily look's ring is a scale-and-fade for this reason). react-native-reanimated is the tool built for both problems: layout transitions and shared-value props on the UI thread, in Expo's managed workflow, with react-native-svg support. It is not installed. It is a native module, so it rides a dev-client rebuild — the same rebuild CUL-616 has been waiting on since August.

Designer: the data motion in this section is the delight; without a UI-thread engine half of it is either janky or faked.

Dir. of Engineering: agreed on the tool, with two conditions I will hold to. One motion engine, not two — adopting Reanimated for the charts while the fold and the arrival stay on Animated is drift by construction, so the adoption issue includes migrating foldMotion.ts / arrivalMotion.ts / lookMotion.ts onto it (the constants and the sequence are the spec; the engine underneath is not). And every motion PR gets an on-device pass before merge, because a motion cannot be verified in jest and the last two "feels great in the mock" moments were cut on the phone.

PM decision needed: R-8.

R-8 · The motion engine

Deciding: whether the data motion is built on Reanimated or constrained to what Animated can do.

  1. Adopt Reanimated as the one engine; migrate the three shipped motion modules; the on-device gate on every motion PR. Recommended — the alternative is designing around the tool's limits forever, which is how the Whorl got its glow disc and the ring got faked.
  2. Stay on Animated; draw-in only for View-based bars, no area or ring motion, days open with LayoutAnimation. Cheaper now; the ceiling is permanent.

Consequence: (1) needs the dev-client rebuild (one, shared with R-9) and one migration issue in wave 2 before any data motion ships; (2) drops two of the four verbs.

§08

Direction F — The wait, and the things you have to feel to verify

The night moment spends the app's one ambient loop on a screen almost nobody sees twice: the cold start plays once per install, and the aurora under it is the part that has already failed once on a phone (the pull-to-refresh band, July). Under Principle 9 a wait is the record arriving, so the cold start becomes a skeleton that the data draws into — which is a designed moment every returning owner sees, not a branded one nobody does. The night moment keeps the one wait that is real work on the pet's behalf and long enough to earn a screen: building the vet report. There it loses the aurora and keeps the Whorl at a size the copy can sit beside.

R-7 · The wait

Deciding: where the night moment plays and what it is made of.

  1. Cold start → skeleton and draw-in; the night moment keeps the report build, loses the aurora and the glow, and shrinks the Whorl. Recommended — the blur goes with the aurora, and the moment that stays is the one that earns it.

Haptics — the verbs are right and nobody has felt them

Seven verbs shipped on Aug 22 with tests. A test can prove the code asked for a buzz; it cannot prove the phone buzzes, and the whole rule (a meal succeeds, a vomit is acknowledged, safety is silent) is a difference you can only feel. expo-haptics is native; the dev client on your phone predates it, so on that build every verb is silent. CUL-616 has carried the one step since August: cut a dev-client build, run the ten-step feel script. R-8's engine rides the same build.

R-9 · Cut the build; add the gate

Deciding: whether the haptics pass and the motion engine share one dev-client rebuild now, and whether "verified on a phone" becomes a Definition of Done line for motion and touch.

  1. Yes to both: eas build --profile development once; CUL-616's script runs on it; the DoD gains one line — any PR that adds or changes a motion or a haptic carries an on-device pass before merge, with what was felt written on the issue. Recommended.
  2. The build only; keep the gate informal. The last two moments cut on device say informal is not enough.

Consequence: (1) closes CUL-616 and unblocks wave 2; the DoD line is a CLAUDE.md edit paid for under the ratchet.

§09

Direction G — The first week: retention is a sequence of arrivals

Your chain is the right one: a kickass experience → retention → data → the report → care. The lever inside it is the first week, when the record is thin and the app has the least to show. Today the first week has one designed moment, the dawn sweep on day seven, and six days of empty states before it. The direction (held from the Aug 22 appendix as "The First Week arc") makes every day's first meaningful thing an arrival in the §07 vocabulary, with no streaks, no scores and no cheer — the 2026-09-03 alignment vetoed those and this does not reopen it. Drawn here as a strip, not frames: it is a direction for wave 3, and it wants its own mock round.

Day 0The first log lands on the named card, and Home's Today strip gains its first mark. Shipped.
Day 1Today fills; the first "two meals, both finished" sentence. The Trend zone is still quiet, and says so (S6).
Day 3Nyx's first shape: the Trend strip appears with three days and draws in. The first thing worth a screenshot.
Day 7The first pattern arrives (the dawn sweep, shipped) — and the month ribbon now has a week.
Day 14The first report is offered, from the record's own sentence: "Two weeks, every episode, ready for your vet."

What we cannot prove. The app ships no product analytics today, so "retention" is a qualitative bar until it does. Wave 3 should carry the smallest honest measure (day-7 and day-30 return, on-device, privacy-reviewed) or accept that the bar is your own hand on the phone.

R-10 · The first week

Deciding: whether the arrival arc is filed as wave 3 of this project.

  1. File it as wave 3, its own mock round first. Recommended — it is where the chain you named is won or lost, and everything it needs (the verbs, the shapes) is built in waves 1–2.
  2. Hold it until Home v2 resumes. Defensible; it leaves the first week as it is through launch.

Consequence: (1) is a design issue, not a build issue, filed with the wave-3 slot.
