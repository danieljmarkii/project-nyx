Culprit · Vet contact · Round 2 · exploratory

When the vet answered by phone

On Nov 9 Nyx's vet recommended an ear medication after a phone call and a few texts. There was no exam, so it could not be logged as a visit, and the course landed in the record with no source. This round draws one entry for any vet contact, a channel choice on it, and how a phone or text contact reads in the record and on the vet report, without ever counting as an exam. Issue: CUL-2749.

Notes

What stays put, and what has no pixels

UnchangedBooking (an appointment is always a visit), Get ready, At the vet, Home's appointment strip, and every existing visit (they read as At the clinic).
The schema call (PM)A channel on the visit record plus a check that makes every reader say "exams only" or "any contact" (Data Scientist), or a contacts table of its own (Dir. of Engineering). The frames look the same either way.
Care stateDoes a logged call count as "my vet knows" for a concern Home is watching? Dr. Chen: yes for awareness, never as an exam. Not drawn this round.
Not in scopeReading the phone's message history or inbox, and any model reading the screenshots or the notes (Vet Files keeps AI out until its own Phase 2 gate). The screenshots are the owner's own files in a private store, location stripped.
Later: share into CulpritSharing a screenshot straight from Messages or Mail into a new contact is the fastest path, but share-sheet import is parked in the Vet Files spec. Worth its own round once this lands.
HistoryThe call gets a row on Nov 9 in History, in the visit row's shape with the Phone tag. Not drawn: it is the shipped row plus the tag.
SequencingTeam view: build after App Store 1.2.0, alongside the "call me if" instruction track, which wants this same record to hang its instructions on.
