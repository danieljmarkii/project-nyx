Rules the build notes carry (from the critique; none needs a ruling):
  • The drug name is printed as stored, never a generic stand-in and never re-cased. No fixture or copy string hard codes the placeholder; each surface that prints a name has a longest name fixture (the tag pill is one line).
  • PR-04: the flag-on logging sheet, field by field against BookVisitSheet.tsx: Day, Time (new on a past contact), What it was about, then Where (Clinic, Vet, the hint) on Phone, Text and Email alike. A device check at 320pt and at the largest text size with every field and Save reachable.
  • PR-05: plan rows are PlanRows.tsx as shipped. The saved mark keeps the shipped 44pt. A document added offline shows Vet Files' shipped pending and failed states; the contact page's footnote about the report is true once the upload lands.
  • PR-06: the list and the after screen keep their shipped loading and failure states. VisitRow's one line limits stay; "No exam" is the protected half.
  • PR-07: the context line renders inside the shipped Home card surface and no line renders until the contact read has answered (C-12). On a card that already carries the shipped "You called on" line, the contact line is added only when its day or channel differs (G3), and a fixture proves the sentence, count, ask and order identical in that double case.
  • Editing a contact (G4, PR-04 or PR-06): Edit shows all four channel chips and the footnote follows the chip. A test proves the report window follows an edit in both directions: Phone to At the clinic moves the start to that day, and At the clinic to Phone moves it back to the last exam.
Rules under the frames (unchanged from round 3.1):
  • Intake is never preference. Nothing started from a contact changes how eating is read. A decline keeps its full weight on Home and in the report.
  • Awareness only. A contact may add a context line to a warning. It never removes, softens, delays, reorders or rewords one, and it is never an exam for the care state.
  • The schema. A channel field on the visit record, default At the clinic. Every reader of visits declares exams only or any contact, held by a guard.
  • Free, always. Contacts, attachments, instructions and the report line are core logging: Pets > $.
  • Unchanged. Booking, Get ready, At the vet, Home's appointment strip, every existing visit (read as At the clinic). No model reads screenshots, notes or typed instructions. Culprit never reads Messages or an inbox.
