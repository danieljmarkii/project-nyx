§01

The signature — the answer to "is it enough"

Round 1 was honest and it was incremental: better charts in the same cards. What you reacted to was not a chart. It was a chart arriving. That is the signature, and it is worth stating as one sentence the whole app can be held to:

The record moves. Every number draws itself the first time you see it, every day is a thing you can touch, and every wait is the shape of what is coming.

Three consequences, and they are what changed between the rounds. One: Patterns stops being a page of cards and becomes one instrument, a month you scrub with your thumb, with layers you switch on, and the photographed reads sitting on the timeline where they happened. Two: the Home trend stops being a bar chart and becomes the calendar's own mark, fourteen days you can read at a glance and open where they are. Three: the branded wait goes; every wait is the silhouette of the screen that is coming, and the two gestures that remain (a tick that breathes, a moon that phases) are the record's own rail and the brand's own crescent, nothing invented.

What did not change. The clinical spine: a shape never says more than its number, colour needs the established floor, coverage is drawn beside occurrence, safety cards stay plain, a symptom is acknowledged and never congratulated. Every demo below inherits it. The Home layout stays parked (CUL-810 / CUL-829); §04 changes one card's content, not Home's composition.

The engine, and why round 1's demo not moving is the same lesson

The demo failed on a one-word selector. The app's motion has failed on the phone for a version of the same reason: a tool that cannot see the thing it is asked to move. React Native's Animated cannot drive an SVG path or a layout change on the UI thread, so the fold module carries a two-engine workaround, the daily look's ring is faked with a scale, and the pull-to-refresh sky dropped frames until it was cut. Reanimated is the tool built for exactly this (UI-thread layout transitions and shared-value SVG props, in the managed workflow); it rides the same dev-client rebuild the haptics have waited on since August. The Dir. of Engineering's two conditions stand: one engine (the three shipped motion modules migrate), and an on device pass on every motion PR, with what was felt written on the issue. That is R-8 and R-9, unchanged.

Principle 8 · newproposed

The data is the delight.

The app reads a label and a photo like magic; the display has to be as magical as the capture. Every number owns a shape, every shape reads in three seconds without a legend, and a shape draws itself the first time you see it. A shape never says more than its number.

The testWould Jordan screenshot this and send it to their partner?
Principle 9 · newproposed

Motion is the record moving.

Six gestures, one physics: draw in, open in place, arrive, fold, the wait as the shape of what is coming, and the moon for the one long wait. The rail is the continuous thread. Motion happens when the record changes state and nowhere else; chrome is still; a symptom is acknowledged, never congratulated; safety is silent. A motion or a haptic is verified on a phone, never in a test.

The testCover the words. Can you tell from the motion alone that something in the record changed?
