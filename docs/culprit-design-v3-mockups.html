§04

The rules this rethink crosses — four better-than-the-rule briefs

Your process rule, in use. Each names the rule, its date, what it protected, whether the protection still holds, and the recommendation. None of these is a reason not to build; each is a Tier 2 edit your ruling authorises.

Rule 1 · design principles §3, Zone 3 "The Trend: one chart" (May 2026) · D8 (2026-08-22)
The trend card retires; its chart becomes the Signal's.Protected: one clinically relevant chart on Home without a week-over-week verdict. Still protected: the Signal's chart is the same counts, unverdicted, gated by the Signal's own floors, which the trend card never had. Recommendation: revise Principle 3 to two zones and a door (the Signal; today; the month), and close D8 as superseded.
Rule 2 · med-strip spec D1 "context + a one-tap confirm" (2026-07) · Home's three write classes (CUL-871 / CUL-903, 2026-09-10)
The medication write leaves Home; the dose stays as a fact on the spine.Protected: adherence for a diet-trial pet, by confirming a predicted dose in one tap. Still protected, less directly: the FAB's dose log is two taps, and the spine shows a missing dose as an absence beside the meal that carried it. Recommendation: amend §0.1 to two write classes (the look, the appointment strip's resolution) and update guards/homeWrites.test.ts's allow-set; question 2 asks whether any medication context stays.
Rule 3 · the Signal fold spec (2026-09-03): "the control is Keep it compact, never the face tap"
The face tap becomes a door to the Signal's screen; the fold control moves to that screen and to the strip.Protected: a fold is seen, never dismissed by accident; the face tap never folds. Still protected: the face tap navigates, it never folds, and the fold's own physics are unchanged; a strip re-opens on the record as before. Recommendation: amend §3 (the face tap is a door) and §12 (the detail screen's "Keep it compact" folds the Home card on return).
Rule 4 · the Daily Recap spec DR-2 (2026-08): TodayZone v2's compact day lane
The horizontal lane of dots retires; today is the vertical spine in daylight.Protected: the glance and the evening read share one node language so they cannot drift. Still protected: the spine on Home is DaySpine on the light ground with the day tints, the same component as the recap's, and the lane's count line survives as the header. Recommendation: amend DR-2 §3 (the band becomes the spine) and keep nodeTints.ts as the one map.
