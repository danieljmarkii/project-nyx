Two rulings

What this round asks you to decide

D1 · A setting to grade every meal?

Decidingwhether owners get a switch that brings the per meal question back.

  • (a) No settingRecommended. The overhead goes for everyone, and the engine never trusts a mode the owner stopped following. Drawn in §1.
  • (b) A per pet switch, off by default. Drawn in §7.
  • (c) The same switch, on by default.

Consequence(a) needs nothing new. (b) or (c) add a schema PR and copy that knows the mode on the report, Ask and the trial card.

D2 · What the Didn't eat door records

Decidingwhether Noticed's door answers a day question the safety checks can read.

  • (a) The day questionRecommended. Two taps, no duplicate meal, an honest Not sure. Drawn in §2 and §3.
  • (b) Today's door, a new graded meal, kept reachable all day. Drawn in §7.
  • (c) An appetite word on the look. Reopens the Oct 2 ruling. Drawn in §7.

Consequence(a) needs a Tier 2 edit to the daily look spec, one schema PR, and Dr. Chen signing the words and the 24 hour and 3 day rules.

Why this isn't ripping out intake

The grade, its scale and its column stay. Every safety check that reads a grade keeps reading it. What leaves is one question asked at the wrong moment, and it has been going unanswered since August.

The door adds what the record is missing: a way to say she isn't eating that the checks can read. The Signal's appetite check gets an input again for the first time since mid August.

§6 · What the checks hear

An answer only ever adds concern

Not sure, Haven't seenUnknownNever counted as eaten. A dated line names when to call.
No answerUnknownNothing either way. Silence is never read as eaten or not eaten.
A graded bowlThat meal's gradeThe same observation as the day answer, counted once.

Who reads it: the vomit read, the Signal's appetite check, Noticed's not eating state and the trial card, all through one shared intake check. Nothing here ever lowers a warning.

The backstop: a grazing cat can eat 30 to 50% less without one bowl that looks left. No answer catches that; weight does. So this goes live together with the weight lane, never before it.
