What this mock cannot settle

  • Signal card copy. The two card sentences approximate the deployed phrasing layer’s register (hedged, specific, no alarm). The live cards are whatever generate-signal emits on the seeded data — the capture is the truth, this mock is the layout.
  • The report body. Drawn schematic here; the deployed generate-report HTML is the authority, and the redeploy is held behind B-494 — recapture frame 4 if it lands before submission.
  • Typefaces. Geist and Newsreader aren’t embedded in this page; body text uses the system face and display lines use a serif stand-in. On device the real faces render.
  • Exact crops. What clears the fold on the real 6.9″ screen (whether the TrialStrip makes frame 1, how much report body shows in frame 4) is decided at capture, per the plan’s per-frame notes.
