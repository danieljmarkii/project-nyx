Store screenshots · Round 2.1 · 2026-08-09 — D-SS1/2/3 ruled

The 6.9″ set, before it exists

Round 2 adds the design directions — the answer to "take the app, screenshot it, add a title" — built from the 2026-08 research brief (docs/research/2026-08-app-store-creative-landscape.md: 15 live sets examined, Apple's rules verbatim, the A/B case corpus). Each direction below composes the full 1320×2868 export: ground + caption + device as one artwork. The Cooper capture states from round 1 remain the staging spec under every direction.

What the research changed: caption overlays and marketing frames are explicitly permitted (Guideline 2.3.3), the search card (frames 1–3 at thumbnail size) is where ~60% of visitors decide, and the premium register is unoccupied in the pet category — so the designed set is the recommended submission set, with plain captures kept as the fallback floor.

What this mock cannot settle

  • Signal card copy. The two card sentences approximate the deployed phrasing layer’s register (hedged, specific, no alarm). The live cards are whatever generate-signal emits on the seeded data — the capture is the truth, this mock is the layout.
  • The report body. Drawn schematic here; the deployed generate-report HTML is the authority, and the redeploy is held behind B-494 — recapture frame 4 if it lands before submission.
  • Typefaces. Geist and Newsreader aren’t embedded in this page; body text uses the system face and display lines use a serif stand-in. On device the real faces render.
  • Exact crops. What clears the fold on the real 6.9″ screen (whether the TrialStrip makes frame 1, how much report body shows in frame 4) is decided at capture, per the plan’s per-frame notes.
