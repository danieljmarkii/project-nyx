01

Soften the verb, keep the clock, keep the reason

Deference lives in the verb: "worth a call" in place of "call". The urgency lives in the data and the clock word, which stay. A deferential ask never drops today or tonight, never says something can wait, and never offers an out that rests on the owner's sense of normal.

Why that last rule (Data Scientist, falsification attempt). The friendliest draft was "If this isn't like Mochi, a call today is worth it." It fails for a chronic vomiter: a dog that throws up twice a week is like Mochi, so the owner's baseline turns the call into a stand-down. That normalised, chronic case is the one vets most often hear about too late. The draft was rejected, and the rule holds.

The four decisions

D1 · What the words are keyed on

Deciding: how the build tells blood from repeat vomiting, when the tier alone cannot.

Options: (a) ★ a words layer keyed on the reason already stored on the row (blood, object, cat intake flags), shared by phone and server, with an unknown reason getting floor words. No schema change. (b) A new tier value: a migration, and installed builds rank it as now. (c) Soften every tier and leave the floor to the read text: a blood photo's chip would say "Worth a call today".

Consequence: (a) is four rows: client copy, server copy, skills and specs, and the adversarial pass. That is one more than the issue's forecast of three, so it comes back to you before anything is built.

D2 · The "now" rows (persona conflict)

Dr. Chen: T1–T3 were tiered now because hours matter there: dehydration, an unseen obstruction, a lethargic vomiter. Soften them and no now read is left on the floor.

Designer / Jordan: The ruling named four signs. A vomit count is something the owner watched happen. "Worth a call now" keeps the clock and still lets them decide.

PM decision needed: (a) the now tier joins the floor; (b) deferential with the clock kept, as drawn in §03. (c), softening to today, breaks the formula and is not offered.

D3 · A cat not eating for 24 h

Deciding: which predicate the floor uses.

Options: (a) ★ no meal rated Most or All in 24 h for an owner who rates meals. It already drives the record's cat call, and today's refused-day, trial and door predicates all meet it. (b) Any cat intake decline: louder than today's Signal. (c) 72 h of poor meals: two days later than the ruling's 24 h.

Consequence: under (a) the trial card's "stops eating" line splits as in §05, and the dog side of 72 h refusal stays deferential.

D4 · Floor cases that read soft today

Deciding: whether the floor may get firmer where today's words are soft.

Options: (a) ★ firm them up. The banner says "call your vet today" over blood, an object or a cat meeting D3, and the cat intake read text drops "sooner rather than later". Sam: owners read a cat off food as fussiness. (b) Leave them, since the ruling froze today's words.

Consequence: (a) changes three banner strings and two read texts. (b) leaves the banner as the one place where blood says "worth a look".
