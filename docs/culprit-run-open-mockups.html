Today's opening is not short of a flourish; it has seams. The rows below jump 44pt, then bounce about 12% past their place, then the teal line pops to its length after everything has stopped, and the close ends with another jump.

The proposal (§02) takes every seam out. 06

A mark for "several": ruled

Today a folded run of four meals draws one bead, the same bead a single meal draws, so the thread shows three beads under "4 logged". You ruled for the mark: up to three beads stacked down the thread behind the front one, each showing as an arc below it, tucking under the front bead as the line leaves and sliding back out as it returns. Two meals draw two beads, an exact count; three or more draw three, which says "several" and leaves the number to the words. The Data Scientist, Dr. Chen and the owner panel dissented (§07, D4): a capped stack cannot tell four meals from eight, and a mark that only ever appears on meals eaten normally or unrated could teach owners that it means "fine". Their conditions ride the build. The flying version, where the beads travel down to their meals, stays cut.

The stack, live currentFour meals. The back beads tuck under the front one while the line leaves the bead, and slide back out as it returns. It is the proposal, the same as §02.

The dissent's conditions, carried into the build (CUL-1736).

1 · The words keep the count. The line's number and its spoken label are unchanged, and the mark adds nothing to what VoiceOver reads.

2 · The mark never changes with a rating, a format or anything else in the run, so it can say "several" and never "how it went".

3 · The phone check asks one owner, cold, what the arcs say. An answer about how the meals went, rather than how many, brings it back to you.

Lead the line linearly at the box's opening speed so its tip never stalls, ease the box with no overshoot, land each meal as the edge reaches it, close faster than you open. The deal only counts as open in place if the beads ride the line; the real gain is a still stack, which says "several" outside the plus's column.

What the team will not do

No haptic. Opening a group is not a commit, and the row this lives in paints Worth a call; the haptics guard forbids one by design.
No bounce on the rows below, and never on Worth a call, which rides the same geometry.
No flying beads, glow, pulse, count up or celebration. A run of meals is the calmest thing on the day, and "8 meals" is not a score for a cat that may be eating less.
No decoration that only ever plays over calm content, save the one mark you ruled for. Runs hold only meals eaten normally or unrated, so the stack (D4) is built to say how many and never how it went (§06's conditions).
No duration that grows with the number of meals. Twelve meals settle inside the same 400ms as two.
Nothing drawn over another record. The box clips while it moves; the teal line never crosses a rose bead.
Nothing run only on the Patterns month. It shares the mechanism fixes and keeps one motion for every verdict.
No new animation library. The two engines the app already uses are enough.

How this page was made

Today's phone is rebuilt from the code. Every beat is the shipped constant: the 160ms lead, 80ms lag, 370ms spring at damping 0.7, 40ms and 300ms landing, 180ms leave, 300ms close, 220ms trail, 30ms slack and the 44pt band. The proposal's numbers come from the seats: an 80ms linear lead, a 240ms ease out box, a 150ms landing with a 4pt drift where the box's edge reaches each meal (never starting after 250ms), a 100ms leave, a 200ms close and an 80ms retract.
