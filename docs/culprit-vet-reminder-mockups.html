8 · Two decisions, ruled

The PM took both recommendations on 2026-11-13. The briefs stay for the record.

D1 · The date on the morning reminder. Deciding: whether the lock screen says Make a report of Sep 16 to today or Make a report to take along.
Options: (a) drop the date (recommended: R4 lists day, time and name, and the last visit's date is a fact from Nyx's record; Get ready shows the window live one tap away) · (b) keep it, as PR-03 built it.
Consequence: (a) deletes the record read from the planner's copy and simplifies its inputs; (b) widens R4 to allow a record date. clinical-guardrails: neither reassures; the question is R4's scope.
D2 · The on line on the booking sheet. Deciding: whether it names this booking's two times or says reminders are on.
Options: (a) name the times (recommended: it's true at the moment it's shown, and it is the only place an owner sees R2 skip a reminder they booked too late for) · (b) the general line.
Consequence: (a) the sheet calls the planner's two instant functions live; the picked state uses the same words. (b) needs no planner call. Either way edits move the real schedule (PR-03).
