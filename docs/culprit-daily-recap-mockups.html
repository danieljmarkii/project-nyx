What tapping it does

Opens the recap screen for the day the notification fired for — a 9pm banner tapped at 12:40am opens the day you logged, not an empty new one; a stale multi-day-old tap clamps to today. (The midnight-anchor ruling, D-4, is recommended and awaiting sign-off.)

  • Fires every evening the category is on, logged day or not — a ritual that skips days teaches the owner the channel is unreliable; the zero-log day is handled by the screen.
  • Neutral body by default — “Biscuit’s day is ready to read” exists only behind an explicit opt-in (D-6, open).
static, safe body one per account tap → the recap

2 · The screen — the day as a timeline, in the brand’s night

The recap renders on the night ground (ruled: “let’s not blind someone as they’re opening the notification”) and the timeline is the event list — time on the left, a category-tinted node on the thread, the event and its intake/adherence beside it. Every node is a doorway into its event. The contract that keeps this buildable: everything on the screen is a record fact or a doorway — no verdicts, no severity, no reassurance, ever.

Top to bottom

  • The lead line — one count-anchored sentence, picked by fixed precedence: symptoms first, then trial facts, then counts. Curation by precedence, never a verdict.
  • Count chips — the day’s inventory, tinted by category, never totalled into a score.
  • The spine — the day itself. Gaps between nodes are real gaps; “Trial diet” on a meal is a record fact.
  • The trial strip — day-position + today’s count from the existing trial predicates, dooring to the full trial card (which owns every viability reading).
  • The forward line — only when a real tomorrow-fact exists; never manufactured.

  • No all-clear over the calm evening rows; no severity anywhere on this surface.
  • The forward line is absent — no real tomorrow-fact exists on this day.
