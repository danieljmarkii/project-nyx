§01 · The bar, converged

The pet is the tab — with the fit risk closed

Your ruling: glyphs + labels, and the Pet tab becomes the pet (avatar + name). The risk you named — long names, small phones — is closed with a fallback ladder, not a truncation: the label tries the full name at 11pt; if it doesn't fit the tab's width it tries 10pt; if it still doesn't, it falls back to the word Pet. A name is never cut mid-word — the avatar carries the identity in every state, and VoiceOver always speaks the full name. At the narrowest supported width a tab fits ~12 characters, so the fallback is the rare case, not the norm.

§03 · Ruled — now specced

The arrival moment, written down

Your call: delightful, spec it. The dawn sweep (round 1, §02 option 2a) is the treatment. The spec, ready to lift into the build issue:

Behavior

  • Trigger: the Signal cache transitions building → live with ≥1 finding, and the per-pet arrival marker is unset.
  • Sequence (~1.2s total): 0ms the rail turns live → 250ms the wash begins (teal into a breath of moment-gold, left-to-right, 900ms, ease-out) → the building rows dissolve as the first headline crossfades in (400–900ms) → sub-line fades at 1200ms. One soft success tap at 900ms.
  • Once per pet, ever: a per-pet played marker (AsyncStorage), registered in wipeLocalSession per the B-402 rule — a shared device never replays another account's moment. Device-local, so a reinstall may replay once; accepted, harmless.
  • Never for a safety finding: if the first-ever finding leads the safety band, the card appears plainly and instantly (S1 — plainness is the severity signal) and the marker is set; the sweep never decorates concern.
  • Reduced motion: plain crossfade, no sweep; the haptic still fires (touch is not motion).

Build shape

  • SignalZone-local Animated values — no new dependencies, no new component; the E1 building card and live card are both already in place.
  • Tests: marker-once semantics, safety bypass, wipe-path inclusion; the animation itself gets the reduced-motion static-frame check the loading system already models.

Goes into the project as its own small issue; no decisions left open here.

§04 · Completion registers — the background you asked for

What happens after you tap "log"

Every log ends with the app saying saved. The finding: the app currently says it six different ways, decided by which door you walked through — and you've probably only ever seen two or three of them, because two fire on paths you rarely use and one is behind the picker beta. Here is the full map:

Three costs, concretely: (1) the white flash is the single worst moment in Jordan's literal brief — one-handed, in the dark; (2) there is no Undo anywhere — a wrong-food tap in the dark is fixed by History → detail → Remove, five taps; (3) paths 3, 5 and 6 waste the confirmation — the app knows exactly what it just saved ("Vomit · found by 5:33 PM", "Weight · 12.4 lb") and says "Logged" instead, or nearly nothing.

Deliberately not doing
