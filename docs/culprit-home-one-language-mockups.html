§02 · G4

What colour says "you can tap this": indigo

Better than the rule. In-app brand rule 3 (Aug 2026, amended Sep 26 for the + button, CUL-322) says teal is the only colour for things you tap, and indigo is only ever a background. It protects against two accent colours fighting, and it kept the Signal off a dark ground (D8). Making indigo the action colour was parked as CUL-1279 until you had judged the indigo + button on your phone. You have it now.

Deciding: which colour means "you can tap this" on Home, and so what teal is left to mean.

(A) Teal means "you can act". Links and the + glyph stay teal; MOST and GIVEN go grey. The Designer's and Data Viz's position.

(B) Teal means "a good fact". Meals, MOST and GIVEN stay teal; links go to ink. Jordan's position.

(C) Indigo means "you can act", teal means "a good fact". Recommended: it settles the A-or-B split by giving each side its own colour, and puts the brand colour you said is missing into every door. The protection rule 3 kept still holds: indigo never becomes a ground behind the record, and the Signal stays light.

Consequence: C is an app-wide change, not a Home one. Home alone in indigo would be a new inconsistency with History, Foods and the trial screen, the exact problem this milestone fixes. So C opens CUL-1279 as its own short round across screens, with a new action token, a rewrite of brand rule 3 and a full contrast check. Home's G1 to G3 build does not wait for it; only the colour of links does.
