  • A refused meal gets its own mark: a hollow ring in the corner. It is told apart by shape, never by an alarm colour (§04's rule holds; Sam: never amber). The day keeps the broken line because the bowl was not finished.
  • The legend counts refusals and left-some meals apart, over one denominator: the meals in the shown month with an amount logged (treats and free-fed bowls are not counted, the intake lens's own definition). A refusal is never folded into "left some"; a meal is counted once, refused first.
  • The ring and both counts ride the Meals layer. The refused row appears whenever the month holds a refusal; the left-some row keeps its place and gains its count.
  • The day says how many meals were refused ("2 meals refused") and adds "a meal left unfinished" only when another meal that day was left.

3 · An honest weight line current

Weight · last 12 of 30 readingsAll 30 ›
Down 0.3 lbs (3%) since Jul 3 · lower at each of the last 8 readings
Log a weigh-in
  • A run is stated, never shrugged off. When each of the last three or more readings is lower than the one before, the line says so and the home-scale caveat does not print, whatever the size. A rise is stated only beside an overall rise; a fall against an overall rise is joined with "but".
  • The caveat prints at exactly two readings, or when the readings scatter around the first one (a reading at least 50 g above and one 50 g below it, in the series and in its later half, neither side holding most of them, and the last three readings back at the start or on both sides of it) with no run at the end. A loss that holds its lower level at the end never gets it. The move must be under both of a scale's bounds, under 5% of the first reading and under 0.2 kg, strict, in whole grams of the stored kilograms, and the line never prints it beside a percentage that reads as 5%.
  • One population. The header counts the readings the dots draw ("last 12"), names the record's total beside it, and the door opens all 30.

One meal among dose-only days: "No vomiting logged on 1 day with a meal or symptom · through Sep 16"
  • Both days stay logged: the white square, the line and the unlogged count do not change (the coverage question as R3 ruled it).
  • Only a day holding a feeding or a symptom entry may add "no vomiting". A dose, a weight, a stool or an "other" entry alone says what it holds and nothing about vomiting.
  • The month's line follows: "No vomiting logged" needs at least one such day in the drawn month; without one the line names the window and the coverage only. When only some logged days hold a meal or symptom, the line names how many it stands on.
  • The same rule holds under every lens ("no itching" only on a feeding or symptom day). That extends the ruling past vomiting, flagged on CUL-1553.
