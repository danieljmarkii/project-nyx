04

The resting state, and what can grow on Home

You asked for day 7 to be better than day 1. The team's honest answer has two halves. What can grow on Home: the answered row can answer back with a count whenever there is something to count — a symptom-class word with history, a first-marked date. What cannot: a run of quiet mornings, because on a healthy pet a growing count of "nothing unusual" is read as wellness, and the record has no way to tell a careful owner's 30 honest looks from 30 reflex taps. So a quiet day 200 looks like a quiet day 40 — until the morning something is different, when the receipt says first in 118 mornings you looked, which is the sentence the vet wants. Below: the states in order, then the two options that would make a quiet Home grow.
