Where our bars stand, pose by pose

Your two screenshots of 1.3.0 on the Duo come down to one cause each, and to one thing we got backwards: iOS moves its bars to a strip down the side when the phone is folded and lays them flat again when it is unfolded and upright. We built the opposite. Two decisions below settle where our tab bar and the + stand.

Ruled
D1 = A and D2 = A, PM, 2026-11-10: "A on both, go ahead and build it." One rule follows: our bar goes where the system's bars go. The frames marked A are what was built (CUL-2703, PR 1505); the boxed frames stay as the options that were weighed.

D1 · Folded

What stands in the 84pt strip?

The folded screen is the short one. The strip is there whatever we do, unless we ask iOS to take it away.

Deciding
Whether the folded Duo keeps a bottom tab bar or moves the tabs and the + into the system's strip.
Options
A, tabs in the strip (recommended): it matches every Apple app on this phone and gives the short screen its height back. B: bottom bar beside an empty strip. C: opt out of the strip.
Consequence
A makes the bar's side follow the system's edge, which also covers the phone on its side and Split View with no further design. B or C keep the bar's side tied to width, and those two poses stay as they are.
Risk
A depends on iOS reporting the strip's edge to us. If the phone says otherwise, B is the fallback and costs nothing extra to switch to.
D2 · Unfolded, upright

Bottom bar, or our side rail repaired?

iOS has no strip in this pose. Both frames fix the clipping; they differ in where the bar and the + live.

Deciding
Where the tab bar stands when the Duo is open and upright.
Options
A, bottom bar (recommended): one rule for every pose, "our bar goes where the system's bars go". B: keep the side rail here, repaired.
Consequence
A unseats part of CUL-2242 (the rail at every wide width) and keeps its work: the rail still stands when the phone is on its side. B keeps CUL-2242 whole and makes this pose the exception to D1's rule.
