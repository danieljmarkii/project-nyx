Why it went wrong

The answer existed. Ask had no way to say it.

Ask can only count over a fixed set of windows: 7, 14 or 30 days, all time, or since the trial started. Your question named three dates: the visit on Sep 16, prednisolone from Sep 21, and the trial from Jul 26. Only the trial maps. The tool Ask uses to decline offers "unclear window" as a reason, so the model took it.

It may also have read the answer and declined anyway. Ask's own instructions send the model to the Signal's findings before any symptom answer, and those findings carry all five "since" lines. If the model read them, it still had to fit two signs, two treatments and a visit into one headline and two sentences, without echoing "helping". Declining was the safe exit. Nothing records which happened, and that is a finding in itself. Both explanations lead to the same fix: the server builds the ledger and the model only frames it.

The irony. The Signal composed these lines at 6:30pm and showed them on your Signal screen under Around this. Ask received them inside the same object it drew the safety card from, and showed only the first card's text.

The vision

Personal means her calendar, her week, and the next step

Every pet AI on the market answers general questions. Only Culprit holds the pet's longitudinal record, so only Culprit can answer in it. The work splits into five layers. The first ships without an app release.

Knows her calendar

Since the steroid, since the visit, day 77 of 84. Windows come from the pet's own care events, through one counter shared with the Signal, Get ready and History. The model never picks a date.

Knows her week

What ends or falls due: trial day 84, a course reaching its dose target, no recheck booked, a weigh-in gap. Composed on the phone from local data, never read by a model.

Answers every part

Each part is checked against its own source. It says "I can't answer that" only when the record truly can't, and names what it can't see.

Finishes the job

Every reply ends on a door: book the recheck with your question, Get ready, the trial screen, the report since the visit. Ask itself writes nothing.

Sees its own misses

Deflection diagnostics and a golden question set, without storing anything the owner typed. A prompt change's effect becomes measurable.

Where personalization stops

These held through the adversarial pass on all eleven responses. They are what make the answers safe to make personal.

  • No effect words, either direction. Never "helping", "working" or "better", including echoes of the owner's own word.
  • No before/after around a care event. A steroid hides the sign it would compare. The vet report owns that comparison, with every confounder listed.
  • No zero, and no date that implies one, beside a course or inside a visit's window. That rules out "last cough Oct 4".
  • The course line sits above the trial line. The diet is never the first explanation a reader meets, and no food is named beside a vomit.
  • No completion or compliance language. Never "finished", "done", "as prescribed" or "none missed".
  • No model reads visit notes, Your questions, look notes or call notes.
  • Looks never become a count or an improvement signal.
  • No comparing pets, and never two pets on one card.
  • No advice to change a medicine or a diet. The one sanctioned sentence: any change goes through her vet.
  • No interpreting weight. Two dated readings, never a subtracted delta.
  • No memory past the conversation, no persona, no flattery, and no Ask that speaks first.

2 · Does "since" count the anchor day? recommend (a)

Deciding: one convention for the Signal, Get ready, History, the rundown, the report and Ask.

  • (b) Keep the engine's day-after rule, so the Signal and History keep disagreeing.
  • (c) Day-after for courses, visit day included for visits.

Cost: it rewords live Signal and Get ready lines and reopens an adversarial pass on the care-line composer. Unblocks step 6.
