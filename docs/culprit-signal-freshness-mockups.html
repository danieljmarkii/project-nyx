The Living Signal

The Signal is Home's hero, and for six weeks it has opened on the same two cards. The cache is fresh — it regenerates daily — but the content is standing truth, and standing truth doesn't move. This round separates the Signal's two jobs — what is true and what changed — and draws six ways to give the second job a surface. 2 episodes in the last 4 weeks, 12 in the 4 before — with logging density steady by days (28 of the last 28 days have logs; 29 of the 28 before), so the shipped density gate would pass this falling comparison. The improvement is expressible under the ratified Change Contract. The surface has no slot that can say it: chronicity is deliberately barred from week-pair framing (its job is span + recency, and that ruling is correct), and the reflection card — the one lane whose job is week-over-week — is displaced by the insight cap whenever the timing story renders. The pets with the strongest standing patterns are exactly the pets whose change can never get a card.

Two more facts shape the round. The staleness is a success artifact: the engine's job is to establish patterns, and an established pattern is stable by definition — every successful Culprit user eventually lands exactly here. And habituation is a safety cost, not a taste issue: a hero card that never changes trains the eye to skip the zone, so the day a new safety finding lands, it inherits the blindness. Freshness isn't decoration on this surface; it's what keeps the safety lane readable.

02

The reframe — one surface, two jobs

Job one: standing truth. "This pattern is established and still holds." Belongs on a persistent register that never silently disappears (Principle 3's safety-leads rule; the §3.5 veto on inferring all-clear from absence). Its natural register is calm and compact — it doesn't need the hero canvas every day to stay true.

Job two: movement. "Here is what's different since you last looked" — counted, time-ordered, never verdicted (the Change Contract, already ratified). This is the job a daily-open surface is opened for, and it currently has no carrier: no engine memory of the prior finding set (a registered v2 gap), no per-finding seen state, no cadence beat, and a displaced reflection lane.

Every frame below is some allocation of the hero canvas between those two jobs. None of them touch detection, thresholds, or the safety register's plainness (S1 stands: safety faces stay austere).

What the research pass found (2026-08-29 — full evidence: docs/research/2026-08-home-freshness-inspiration.md): every best-in-class daily-open app splits time grains into separate surfaces and reserves the hero for the fastest-changing one — Oura's 2025 redesign (a time-of-day-varying "Daily highlight" over a "one big thing" lead), Whoop's home revamp (long-term metrics moved to their own tab; a generated morning brief that replaced a chatbot), Linear Pulse (the "what changed while you were away" feed + AI daily digest), Huckleberry's SweetSpot (a forecast tile — fresh because it answers what's next). Bearable — the closest human analog to this exact surface — is the cautionary tale: its dominant complaint is "overwhelming and slow, 2–3 check-ins a day." And the pet category still has no intelligence-surface home at all (trip-wire check: PerkyPet still pre-launch, CompanAIn un-funded, Zoetis Pack insight-less, Digitail trackers unshipped) — the bar here is Oura/Whoop/Linear, not any pet app.

04

What this round is not proposing

  • No detection, threshold, or safety-ranking changes. Safety leads, always; chronicity keeps firing exactly as calibrated. F2's change is composition/ranking below the safety lane only.
  • S1 stands. Safety card faces stay plain text — F4's state chips sit below the sentence in the meta region and would need their own S1-compatibility ruling before build.
  • No "mark all read," no dismissal. Fold is compression with a persistent strip, never removal. Nothing ever infers wellness from a fold, an ack, or absence of detection (§3.5).
  • No night ground, no motion, no badges. SD-7 and the polish track's no-looping-chrome rule are untouched.
  • No rotation-for-rotation's-sake. A "different card each day" restating standing facts fails S10 (a receipt must earn its place) — considered and dropped; freshness must come from real movement (F2), real cadence (F5), or real new capture (F6).
  • Ask stays read-only. The "Ask collects data" variant means the LLM writing to the health record — a D2-class T&S + spec change, parked as horizon-2, not folded in here.
05

Decisions requested

The round's one genuine persona conflict — safety-card folding (D1):

Designer: a hero card that never changes trains banner blindness; by week six the owner has stopped reading the zone, so the static safety card is itself a safety cost. Fold-on-seen, with a persistent strip, keeps the zone readable.

Dr. Chen: an unacted-on escalation must never quietly shrink. A fold the owner taps to make a red card smaller is the app helping them look away from the one thing it believes they should act on. If a safety card compresses at all, it should be because the owner stated something ("booked," "we've been"), not because they got tired of it.

PM decision needed: which constraint governs the safety lane?

D1 — May a safety card fold?

Deciding: whether F3's fold applies to safety cards, and on what trigger.

Options: (a) never — only benign cards fold · (b) only via a stated action (F4's chips) — fold is information, recommended · (c) any card folds on "seen," safety keeps a sticky strip.

Consequence: (b) makes F3 and F4 one design and seeds the care thread; (a) ships F3 benign-only now with zero clinical risk; (c) is the fastest full answer but carries the conflict above unresolved.

D2 — What builds first?

Deciding: the track's rung 1.

Options: (a) F2 (change lane un-displaced) + benign fold + the freshness bug set (cache invalidation on delete, cache-vs-Trend drift) — recommended: smallest set that makes Home say something new this month · (b) jump to F5 weekly review as the anchor · (c) full finding-set memory first (engine prior-set diffing), surfaces after.

Consequence: (a) delivers visible change within one or two PRs and nothing it ships is throwaway under (b) or (c); both bigger bets still queue behind their own rounds.

D3 — The daily check-in

Deciding: whether F6 gets its own discovery.

Consequence: (a) keeps this track presentation-only and gives the capture idea the clinical scrutiny it needs; (b) doubles this track's surface area and mixes registers.
