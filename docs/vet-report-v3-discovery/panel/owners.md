# Owner panel: the vet report, after the button

Two owners, written separately, in their own voices. Both looked at the same things:
- the phone renders (`cooper-trial-dog-screen*.png`);
- page 1 of the Cooper PDF (11 pages) and of the Nyx PDF (10 pages);
- `APP-SCREEN.md`, which describes today's flow;
- the competitor flows (Pawsitive, PawSolids, WagWatch).

The words are theirs. Each section ends with a short **Team read**.

## (A) Jordan: Cooper, Labrador, day 46 of a 56-day hydrolysed diet trial

> I'm the person who logged 43 days out of 43. I want that to count for something when I'm standing in the exam room.

### 1. Walking today's flow

**Getting there.** I'd come in from the trial screen ("Vet report"), because that's where I live right now. Or from the Pet tab, where the button says "Open vet report". Fine. I'd never find it from Home: it only shows when the Signal is "quiet", and Cooper's Signal is never quiet.

**"Report range: Default | Custom…"** This is where I stop. *Default what?* Default sounds like "the generic one", and I don't want the generic one. I want the trial one. I wouldn't know that Default already *is* the trial until the report finishes building and a little line says "Active diet trial · May 18 – Jul 2". Or does it pick "Since the last vet visit"? My last visit was the day Dr. Chen started the trial, so is that the same thing? I can't tell before I commit. I'd probably tap Custom just to feel in control, and then I'm fiddling with two date pickers at the counter. That's exactly the 10 seconds I don't have.

**"Include your Noticed notes."** It's on, and I don't remember what Noticed is. Daily looks? Did I write something snarky on a bad day ("he's being a nightmare")? I'd flip it off to be safe, and then wonder whether I'd just hidden something useful.

**The preview.** It's a whole A4 page squeezed onto my phone. I can't read it without pinching, and I won't pinch through 11 pages. What I *can* read at that size are the bold bits, and they scare me:
- "**4 of 154 logged feedings did not match the trial diet.**"
- "**The record shows Chicken in Cooper's diet during the trial**" — my first thought is that the vet is going to think I cheated.
  - Reading on: one was a feeding before the food was "permitted", and the Dentastix *Dr. Chen said were fine* have chicken on the label. I logged them honestly *because* she okayed them. Now the report leads with them like a confession.
- "Elimination diet trial — **Soy**". Soy? I'm feeding hydrolysed protein. I thought the point of hydrolysed was that there's nothing to react to. Is the report saying I put him on soy by mistake?
- "Switch onto the diet … Jun 28". I switched on May 18. Why does it say June 28? It reads as if the app thinks I've only been compliant for four days.
- "a floor, not a total", "Antigen exposure", "Label contamination", "NOT TRIGGERED". I don't speak this. The "NOT TRIGGERED" box is three lines of double negatives. Is it good news or not?
- "Itching spans 35 days… most recent 7 days ago." Hang on. That's the best news in the whole report: **no itching for a week.** And it's written as a warning, in the scary box.

**The bar at the bottom.**
- If I hadn't set the allowed-food list, the button says "**Send anyway**". That sounds like I'm about to do something wrong.
- "Your saved vet documents aren't part of this report." I uploaded his allergy panel to Vet Files. Why can't it ride along? Now I'm sending two things.
- The sync warning ("Connect to the internet and reopen") would panic me in the car park.

**Send.** The share sheet opens and I pick Mail. Then I have to remember the clinic's reception address and type a subject line. When I'm done, nothing in Culprit says I sent anything. Did it go? To whom? Did they open it? A week later Dr. Chen's nurse says "we never got anything" and I've got no record to show her.

**My fears, in order:**
1. The vet reads "Chicken in Cooper's diet" and decides the trial is ruined, or that I'm careless.
2. The vet doesn't read it at all, because it's 11 pages.
3. I sent the wrong window.
4. Something I wrote in a note embarrasses me.

### 2. My jobs

| When | What I need | How often |
|---|---|---|
| **Before the recheck** (week 4, week 8) | The trial so far: did it work, was it clean. The main event. | 2 in 8 weeks |
| **"How's he doing?" email from the clinic, mid-trial** | A quick update, often from my desk at work, in a few minutes between meetings | 1–2 |
| **Something weird happened** (blood in stool, a stolen sandwich) | "Here's what he ate in the last 48 hours". Not a full report, just the slice. | Maybe once |
| **End of trial / rechallenge starts** | The before-and-after, kept so I can compare when we reintroduce chicken | 1 |
| **2 a.m. emergency** | Probably never for Cooper. If it happened, I'd want one page for a stranger at the ER. | Rare |

About **2 a month** during a trial and almost none after. That means I'll never get good at this screen, so it has to work cold every time.

### 3. Dream flow (≈10 seconds)

1. On the trial screen, tap **Vet report**.
2. The app already says it, in words I understand: **"Cooper's trial so far · Day 46 of 56 · for Dr. Chen"**, with the recheck date if I've booked it ("Recheck Thu 9 Jul"). No "Default", and nothing to choose unless I want to.
3. Below that, four lines I can scan, each with a count:
   - Itching 16 times · last one 7 days ago
   - Loose stool 1
   - Off the list 4 (2 were the vet-okayed Dentastix)
   - Logged every day: 43 of 43

   Each line has a little switch, like PawSolids. I won't touch them, but seeing them tells me what's going in.
4. **"Anything to ask Dr. Chen?"** Two or three suggested questions as chips, plus a box to type my own. Optional; skipping it is one tap.
5. **Send to Dr. Chen.** It remembers the clinic email (or link) from last time. One tap.
6. Done beat: **"Sent to Dr. Chen's clinic · Jul 2, 9:14 · saved to Thursday's visit."**

**Decisions the app should make for me:**
- the window: the trial, or since the last visit, whichever matches what I'm doing;
- which sections go in;
- whether to include notes (ask me *once*, and remember);
- the file name and subject line;
- who it goes to;
- the format.

**What it should never decide for me:** hiding the off-list feedings. I *want* them in. I just want them in with their context ("Dentastix, vet-approved Jun 8"), not as the headline.

### 4. Saved reports and visits

- **Attached to the visit: yes, absolutely.** The recheck is the thing I care about, so the report should live *on the visit*: "Sent for this visit · Jul 2". After the visit I'd add the vet's notes next to it. That's the story of the trial in one place.
- **A "Reports" list:** fine as a back-up, but I'd never browse it. I'd look for the visit.
- **What I sent last time: yes, I need to know.** The reasons:
  - the nurse saying "we didn't get it";
  - not sending the same thing twice;
  - at week 8, comparing against what Dr. Chen saw at week 4.

  I want "Sent Jun 1 to reception@… · covered May 18 – Jun 1" and the ability to reopen *that exact one*, not a regenerated version that's changed since.
- **A reminder before the appointment: yes, the day before.** "Cooper's recheck is tomorrow. His trial report is ready. Want to add a question?" One reminder, not three.

### 5. Vet link vs PDF

- **A PDF is what I trust.** It's a thing: it lands in the clinic's inbox, goes into Cooper's file, and can't change.
- **A link** is great *if* I can see it was opened ("Opened by the clinic, Jul 3"). That alone would make me choose the link. With a link I could also send it before the visit and the vet sees the latest version on the day.
- **Revoking:** I'd revoke after the trial ends. I don't need my vet's front desk seeing Cooper's poop forever.
- **Expiry:** match it to the trial. Expire 30 days after the trial ends, or let me pick "until the next visit".
- **Best answer: both.** A link in the email with the PDF attached, so the clinic can do whatever their system does.

### 6. AI summary

- **Yes, I want to read it before Dr. Chen does.** Not to change the numbers, but because if it says something about my dog, I should know what it said.
- **Would it scare me?** Only if it's written like today's safety box. "Chronicity: itching spans 35 days" reads as alarming. "Itching logged on 16 days; none in the last 7" reads as true and calm. Same fact.
- **Would I edit it?** I'd *want* to, the first time I saw "The record shows Chicken". I know I shouldn't be able to rewrite what the data says, though; the vet has to trust it isn't my spin. What I want is **"Add a note for the vet"** under it: *"The Dentastix were approved by Dr. Chen on Jun 8."*
- **Reopening it later:** yes. Saved with the report, unchanged, so I can see what she saw.
- **One more thing:** the summary has to tell me if it's *AI-written*, in plain words. If it's just the app counting, say that, because then I trust it more.

### 7. Questions for the vet

**Picked or typed?** Picked first, then edit or type. Suggestions help me remember what I meant to ask in the waiting room. But don't suggest questions that sell anything, and don't suggest ones that answer themselves ("Is Cooper doing well?").

**The three I'd actually ask:**
1. "The Dentastix you okayed on Jun 8 have chicken on the label, and he's had about 25. Does that reset the trial clock, or do we keep counting?"
2. "He's had no itching logged for 7 days and only one loose stool all trial. Is that enough to start reintroducing chicken at week 8, or should we extend?"
3. "He's been on Apoquel the whole time. Could that be why the itching looks better, and should we taper it before we judge the diet?"

### 8. The report itself

**Proud to hand it over? Not yet.**
- It looks like a court document. My app has the moon and the warm dark screens, and the itch chart I love on the trial screen. The report looks like it came from a different company.
- On my phone it's a shrunken printout.

**What would make it feel like mine:**
- **Cooper's name big, with the Culprit mark small.**
- **The trial shown the way the app shows it:** day 46 of 56 as a bar, itching across the 46 days as dots, the off-list feedings as marks on the same line with their names.
- **A first page that fits on my screen** without pinching, made for the phone and not shrunk. The 11 pages can stay behind it for the vet who wants them.
- **Less text.** Every sentence that starts "This is a floor, not a total" makes me feel I'm being cross-examined. Put a footnote at the bottom once.
- **Credit for logging.** Somewhere it should say *43 of 43 days logged*, plainly, near the top. That's the line that makes a vet take me seriously.

### Team read (Jordan)
- **"Default" is the biggest point of friction.** Name the anchor before generating ("Cooper's trial · day 46").
- **The best news is framed as a warning:** 7 days since the last itch is printed inside a red-flag box. *(Copy and safety question: Data Scientist and Dr. Chen to rule. Any "improving" read must stay within n=1 / absence ≠ wellness.)*
- **"Soy" and "Switch onto the diet: Jun 28" look like errors to an owner.** Check them against the spec.
- **Owner context belongs beside an off-list feeding** ("vet-approved Jun 8"), as an owner note, never an edit to the counts.
- **What the owner wants after sending:** a sent record, the visit attached, and an "opened" signal.

## (B) Sam: Nyx, a grazing cat who's eating less and vomiting a lot

> Every time she throws up I go through the same loop. Is it a hairball? Is she just being fussy about the new food? Or is this the time it's actually something? I made a report because I wanted someone else to tell me.

### 1. Walking today's flow

**Getting there.** I'd come in right after logging a vomit, on Home, at night. Today the link isn't there, because the Signal isn't "quiet" (it's never quiet with Nyx). So I'd have to remember it lives on the Pet tab. At 11 p.m., with a cat being sick, I wouldn't remember.

**"Default | Custom…"** I don't know what Default is. Since when? Since her last visit in April? Since I started the app? I'd sit there scared of choosing wrong, as if picking the wrong window would hide the important thing. Then I'd tap Custom and pick… what? I don't know when this started. That's literally why I'm making a report.

**"Include your Noticed notes."** *Oh no.* I write things in there. "Is she just being a brat about the duck?" "Gave her Temptations so she'd eat SOMETHING." Some of them are at 2 a.m. and a bit unhinged. If I leave it on, the vet reads my panic. If I turn it off, maybe I've hidden the thing that matters. I'd agonise. Then I'd probably turn it off, which is the wrong answer.

**The preview, page 1:**
- "**FOREIGN MATERIAL — possible plastic fragment.** AI read · unconfirmed." My stomach drops. I *saw* that photo. I thought it was a bit of the toy mouse wrapper and I'd forgotten about it. The report is shouting it at me in a black box. Is she going to need surgery? Should I be at the ER right now instead of making a PDF?
- "**The owner was told to call the vet soon.**" …Did I call? I didn't call. Now it's printed that the app told me to and I didn't. That feels like evidence against me.
- "**No event of any kind logged after Jun 30 (the last 2 days).**" It says it twice. I was at my sister's; my flatmate fed her. The vet will think I stopped paying attention.
- "**20 treats · Treats & table food.**" Right there on the front page, as big as the vomiting. I gave her treats *because she wasn't eating*. It looks like I'm stuffing her with junk and then wondering why she's sick.
- **What's missing is my actual question.** She's eating less. That's the whole reason I'm scared. And page 1 doesn't say a word about how much she's eating. It's in appendix E on page 8, I think, written as "Ate most ×1". The thing I'm worried about is invisible, and the thing I'm embarrassed about is on the cover.
- "No home weigh-ins recorded." I don't have a cat scale. Is that my fault too?

**Send.** It's 10 pages. I'd send it, then lie awake wondering if the vet will read past page 1, and whether page 1 makes me look like a bad owner. Afterwards nothing tells me it went. If I'm at the ER, I'd be handing my phone over the counter with a share sheet open, which isn't a thing.

**My fears, in order:**
1. That the report is telling me it's an emergency and I'm not hearing it.
2. That the vet reads it and thinks I'm neglectful (no logs for 2 days, treats, didn't call).
3. That it's fine and I've made a fuss and look hysterical.

### 2. My jobs

| When | What I need | How often |
|---|---|---|
| **2 a.m. / ER** | A stranger vet, now. One page: what's happening, since when, the scary photo, what she's eaten. Shown on my phone or AirDropped at the desk. | Maybe every couple of months, honestly |
| **The vet asked** ("keep a log and send it before the follow-up") | Since the last visit: vomiting count, eating, and the photos. | 1 a month while this goes on |
| **Before a booked visit** | Same as above, plus my questions, because I always forget them in the room | 1 a month |
| **Phone triage** ("the nurse said to send anything you have") | The last week, fast | 1–2 a month on bad stretches |
| **At the desk** | Never. I'm never calm enough to make a report at a desk. | — |

About **2–4 a month** in a bad patch. Half of them are at night, and every one of them is anxious.

### 3. Dream flow (≈10 seconds, one-handed, cat on the floor)

1. Straight after logging a vomit, the confirm screen offers **"Make a report for the vet"** (and it's always on the Pet tab).
2. The app asks **one** question with two big answers:
   - **"For Nyx's vet"**: since her last visit, Apr 4. Pick this for the booked follow-up.
   - **"For an emergency vet"**: the last 14 days, one page, safety first. Pick this if I'm going to a clinic that doesn't know her.

   If my vet is in the app it pre-picks one. At 2 a.m. it should lean towards the emergency one.
3. The preview's first screen fits on my phone and says, in this order:
   - **why to look now** (the photo flag, 4 vomits in 7 days);
   - **how much she's eating** compared with how she used to eat;
   - **how often she's vomiting**, with a little chart;
   - **what she's eaten**, treats included but small.
4. **"Anything you're worried about?"** Suggested questions to tap, plus a box.
5. **Send**, or for the ER **"Show at the desk"** (a big QR / link on screen, or AirDrop).
6. Done: **"Sent to Riverside Vets · Jul 2, 23:40."** And separately, kindly: **"If you're worried right now, call your vet or an emergency clinic."** That line should sit with the safety flags, not hidden.

**Decisions the app should make for me:**
- the window;
- what's on page 1 (based on what's actually wrong);
- the order;
- the format for where I'm going;
- whether to include photos (yes: it's what the vet wants and I can't describe it).

**What I want to be asked:** whether to include my Noticed notes, but asked properly: show me which notes will go, so I'm not guessing.

### 4. Saved reports and visits

- **Attached to the visit: yes.** Then when the vet says "Remember, I asked you to watch for X", I can open what I sent and what she said.
- **A Reports list: yes, more than Jordan.** I send these at odd times to different vets (my vet, the ER, the out-of-hours line). I need to see **who got what, when**. Mainly so I don't send the ER a second one in a panic, and so I can show my regular vet what the ER saw.
- **A reminder before an appointment: one, the day before, warm.** "Nyx's visit is tomorrow. Her report is ready whenever you are." Not "You haven't logged in 2 days!", because that would just make me feel worse.
- **Last time I sent:** I don't remember. That's the problem. So yes, I need to know.

### 5. Vet link vs PDF

- **For my regular vet: a PDF.** It goes in her file; I know where it is.
- **For the ER: a link or QR on my phone screen.** Nobody at a 2 a.m. desk wants me to email them a PDF.
- **The link worries me a bit.** It has photos of her sick. If I can see it, see when it was opened, and **revoke** it, I'm fine.
- **Expiry: short.** 7 days for the ER one; until the next visit for my vet's one. Let me revoke with one tap from the visit.
- **What would really help:** "Opened by the clinic". I'd stop refreshing my email.

### 6. AI summary

- **Do I want to read it first? Yes, desperately.** And I'm a bit scared to, which is the honest answer. If it says "possible plastic fragment", I need to see that *before* the vet does, so that I can act on it, not be surprised by it in the room.
- **Would it scare me?** Yes, but that's not a reason to hide it. What would help: say what to *do* next to the scary line ("Possible plastic fragment in one photo, May 23. Worth asking about an X-ray."), and say once, plainly, that it's an AI reading a photo and might be wrong.
- **Would I edit it?** I'd want to soften it. I'd want to type "probably a hairball". **Please don't let me.** On a bad night I'd write it down to nothing, and that's the night it matters. Let me **add a note beside it** in my words ("I think this was the wrapper from her toy mouse"), and leave the AI's words alone.
- **Approve?** Not "approve", as if I'm signing off on my cat's diagnosis. More like "I've read this". And it should go to the vet even if I haven't.
- **Reopen later:** yes, so I can see exactly what the vet saw.

### 7. Questions for the vet

**Picked or typed?** Suggested, please. In the room I forget everything and say "she's just… not herself". Let me tap suggestions, edit them, and add my own. But the suggestions must never be soothing ones like "Could this just be hairballs?". I'll ask that myself; I don't need the app planting it.

**The three I'd actually ask:**
1. "She's eating maybe half what she used to for about three weeks. How long can a cat eat this little before it's dangerous? Should I be worried about her liver?"
2. "The photo from May 23 might show a bit of plastic. Does she need an X-ray or ultrasound, or can we wait and watch?"
3. "She's vomiting about four times a week now. Is that ever normal for a cat, or should we do bloodwork to rule things out?"

### 8. The report itself

**Proud to hand it over? No.** I'd be *nervous* handing it over.
- It looks official and cold.
- It puts my shame (treats, gaps) at the same size as her symptoms.
- It doesn't say anything about the thing I actually noticed, which is that she's eating less.

**What would make it feel like mine:**
- **Her photo-free name card**, with Nyx in big type.
- **Charts that look like the app's:** the vomiting dots I see on History, and an "eating" line.
- **Less text on page 1.** "The record cannot show how long the sign predates it" made me read it three times.
- **The gaps drawn honestly but not as an accusation.** A grey "not logged" stretch on the chart says it better than repeating "no event of any kind logged" twice.
- **A report that sounds like it's on her side and mine.** Not soft; just not a prosecutor.

### 9. The deleted-treats problem

Honestly? If the report said "**3 entries deleted by the owner**", I'd feel caught. I'd feel like the app went behind my back and told on me. I might stop logging treats entirely, which is worse.

But I also know why I delete them. On a bad day I look at the treat count and think "that's why she won't eat dinner, it's my fault", and I delete them to make the screen stop judging me. And if she's not eating, the vet *needs* to know I was hand-feeding her Temptations to get anything in. So hiding them is actually bad for Nyx.

**What I'd want:**
1. **Ask me at the moment I delete:** "Logged by mistake, or did this happen?" If it happened, let me *keep it but stop seeing it* on my own screens.
2. **At report time, one kind line:** "You removed 3 treat logs since her last visit. Treats help a vet understand how much she's eating. Put them back for the report?" With a one-tap "Put back".
3. **On the vet's copy:** if I put them back, nothing about deleting. If I didn't, I can live with a neutral line in the small print, like "3 entries removed by the owner, not shown", as long as it isn't on page 1 and isn't in the safety box. **Never** "the owner deleted entries" in a tone that sounds like tampering.
4. **Treats framed as feeding, not as fault.** On a cat who isn't eating, "ate 6 treats when she refused dinner" is a symptom, not a confession.

### Team read (Sam)
- **The owner's real question isn't on page 1.** Reduced intake sits in appendix E; treats lead the tiles. *(Data Scientist: intake is not preference, so refusal belongs near the top.)*
- **The safety box shows an action the owner may not have taken** ("was told to call the vet soon"). The way that's phrased reads as blame.
- **An ER / "stranger vet" mode** has a different job from the "my vet, since last visit" report. Don't fold them into one default.
- **Keep the AI wording fixed and add an owner note beside it.** Sam asked us outright not to let her soften the AI wording.
- **Deletions: reconcile in-app before the report**, with a neutral disclosure only if they weren't restored. Needs the T&S and Dr. Chen lenses, and a PM ruling: the vet's copy is clinical and on the never-list.

## Where the two owners agree

1. **Get rid of "Default".** Name the anchor in plain words before generating ("Cooper's trial · day 46", "Since Nyx's visit, Apr 4").
2. **The first screen should be native to the phone,** readable without pinching. The full report sits behind it for the vet.
3. **The best and the worst news both need context:** Jordan's 7 itch-free days; Sam's eating drop. What the owner most needs to see shouldn't be buried, and what they're ashamed of shouldn't headline.
4. **AI text stays fixed, and the owner adds a note beside it.** Both said they'd edit if allowed, and both said not to let them.
5. **Questions for the vet:** suggested, editable, never reassuring, and printed on the report.
6. **After sending:** a "Sent to … · date" record, attached to the visit, reopenable exactly as sent, with "opened" if it's a link.
7. **PDF for the regular vet; a link or QR for the stranger vet.** Both should be revocable, with a short expiry.
8. **One reminder the day before a visit.** Never a guilt nudge.
