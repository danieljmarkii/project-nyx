07

Did she heave first?

Whether a cat heaved before food came up is the fact that most changes what a vet does next: it separates vomiting (stomach or gut) from food coming back up without effort (the throat). A photo can't show it, and nothing in the log records it today. Nyx's quick-after-meal bouts can't be told apart without it. Asked after the save, it adds nothing at the moment you're cleaning up.

No new questionToday. The report can describe timing and content, never effort.

09

Decisions

Five are yours. The rest are team calls inside rules already ratified, logged on CUL-1765 with the recommendation applied; any of them can be reversed.

D1 · R3: name her kinds on your screens? yours · conflict

Deciding: whether "within 30 minutes of a logged feeding, 14 of 43" appears on each vomit read, one tap away, or only in the report (§04).

  • A: on the read, with the total.
  • B: timing and count on the read, kinds on the profile and report. Team recommendation; a familiar label lulls on the read.
  • C: report only.

Dissent: Dr. Chen wants A. The count is the long-term finding.

Consequence: B makes the profile (§05) the home of the long-term finding, so it ships second and waits on the weight work (EN-8).

D2 · Narrow R2: drop novelty lines yours · re-ruling

Deciding: whether the read prints lines like "first bile in six weeks", which you reopened at kickoff.

  • Keep them.
  • Drop them from the read; "last seen" dates live on every profile row. On Nyx they fire on 7 of 29 vomits, 5 of the 9 lines rest on an absence nobody saw, and a read without one starts to mean "nothing new".

Consequence: unseats part of R2. Timing and counts keep the reopening; novelty moves to the profile.

D7 · Ask "Did she heave first?" yours · new capture

Deciding: one optional tap after saving a witnessed vomit (§07).

  • No new question.
  • One tap, after the save, starting on "Didn't see". The fact that separates vomiting from regurgitation, and the record can't tell without it.
  • An optional short video for the vet.

Consequence: one schema PR; the report prints the answers as counts.

D10 · Fund the photo evaluation yours · money

Deciding: whether any model or field change ships without a measured comparison.

  • About $50 to $100 through batch, blinded labels from you plus a vet pass on blood and foreign material. Your 3 edits on 44 reads can't be the answer key; they'd teach under-calling.
  • Your edits only.
  • Skip it and upgrade the model.

Consequence: the model change and new fields wait on the vet labels (CUL-1312, cancelled 10/2, would need reopening).

D6 · When "food visible long after a meal" may be said yours · clinical, changes a shipped line

Deciding: the shipped Signal line "Recognizable food 6h or more after eating" counts food inside normal cat digestion (half-emptying ran 3.5 to 12.8 hours in normal cats, Costello 1999).

  • Keep 6 hours.
  • Keep 6 hours for the empty-stomach band; give food-visible its own 8-hour line, printed as a time, never as a sign.

Consequence: changes an owner-facing Signal line and corrects the 6-hour decision doc. Nothing Nyx sees moves; she has no eligible case.

10

Proposed run order

Everything starts after 1.2.0 clears review, except the lily issue, which takes the first build after it. The four lanes are independent except where named.

  1. FirstThe lily question on every cat plant read (and a plant line for dogs), CUL-1782. One server PR, Dr. Chen's wording.
  2. Lane 1The read, with the record (§02, §03): a pure module for the timing line and count over the shipped rules, then the read card and present-only Bile, then a wider ban list in the no-reassurance test. About 3 client PRs, no migration. The count waits for EN-4 to be switched on for the pet.
  3. Lane 2The request, then the evaluation (§08): structured output on today's model for vomit and stool, the full-request stamp, the evaluation harness, then the present-only schema in its own migration PR, then a model change chosen by the numbers. About 6 PRs, mostly server.
  4. Lane 3Her vomiting over time (§05), after Lane 1 and after EN-8's weight history: the partition module with a test that rows sum to the total, the profile, the album, and a guard keeping it out of Ask. About 4 client PRs.
  5. Lane 4The report page (§06) and, if you rule D7 yes, the heave question (§07). About 3 server PRs plus one schema PR.
