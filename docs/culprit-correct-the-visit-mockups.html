Correct the visit

Round 1, exploratory. An audit on 2026-11-19 found 11 things an owner cannot fix about a vet visit and 10 that are hard to find. D1 · What leads the record

Deciding: whether the Visited face opens on the notes or on the plan.
  • A1, recommended: notes capped at four lines, then the plan. It is what you ruled on Sep 17, it reads like a clinic's visit summary, and the cap alone puts the plan on the first screen.
  • A1b: plan first, full notes last. Nothing is ever collapsed, but your own words drop to the bottom of your own record.
Consequence: A1 needs no new ruling. A1b re-rules round 6's order.

Deciding: whether each row opens its own small sheet, or the page has an Edit mode.

D3 · What a visit may claim

Deciding: which existing meds and trials a saved visit may attach. This unseats After the visit R3 (Nov 14).
  • C1, recommended: only what began on or after the visit's day and is tied to no other visit. R3's protection holds, and the vet report cannot credit a vet with an earlier prescription.
  • C1b: anything, on the owner's word. The team's dissent: Dr. Chen's lens would not trust a "started at this visit" line that the app let predate the visit.
Consequence: under C1 your Nov 14 visit can take any med you started that day or after. A med kept or changed at a visit, but started earlier, stays unattached under both options; that is a different fact and is not in this round.

D4 · The safety net on a cancelled booking

Deciding: what replaces today's confirm. Convention C-21 (2026-08) gives a destructive action a confirm or a way back, never both.
  • E1, recommended: drop the confirm; Undo at the moment plus a Didn't happen list. It keeps C-21 and is one tap shorter. Home's one tap It didn't is unchanged, and the way back lives on the vet page, so Home gains no write.
  • E1b: keep the confirm and add the list. Safest on paper; it breaks C-21 and nags on every cancel.
Consequence: E1 means a cancelled booking is no longer hidden everywhere, which changes round 6's vet page by one section.

D5 · What may change pets

Deciding: whether only a booking can move to another pet, or a saved visit too.
  • Bookings only, recommended: a saved visit has meds, a trial and paperwork that belong to one pet. Moving it would strand them or move a medical record across animals. A visit saved under the wrong pet is deleted (D6) and logged again.
  • Saved visits too: allowed only while nothing is attached. One more rule to explain for a rare case.
Consequence: bookings only keeps the pet change to one screen and one review.

D6 · What happens to the booking behind a deleted visit

Deciding: a visit usually came from a booking. When the visit is deleted, where does the booking go?
  • Back to Waiting on you, recommended: the booking was real; only the log of it was wrong. You answer it again: how did it go, or it didn't happen. Its questions return with it.
  • Gone with the visit: simpler, and it loses the prepared questions, which is the same loss D4 exists to stop.
Consequence: the recommended option means deleting a visit never destroys questions.
